Hi PartnerStack team,

I'm writing to appeal SaaSpare.org's recent rejection from the PartnerStack publisher network.

I understand your automated review likely flagged us as a new domain with low traffic — and that's fair. I want to share context on what SaaSpare actually is, because I think a human review would reach a different conclusion.

**What SaaSpare is:**

SaaSpare.org is an independent B2B SaaS pricing and comparison platform. It's not a content farm or coupon site. We run a Price Intelligence Engine that:

- Tracks 15 SaaS vendors weekly (HubSpot, Notion, ClickUp, Asana, Monday.com, Pipedrive, Linear, and others — all PartnerStack advertisers)
- Snapshots their pricing pages and diffs any changes
- Publishes timestamped pricing histories at `/pages/[tool]-pricing-history-2026`
- Runs a Buyer Pain Index built from 646 classified buyer-intent signals harvested from Reddit, Hacker News, and B2B forums

This is the kind of site PartnerStack's advertisers specifically want coverage on. When a buyer searches "HubSpot pricing history" or "ClickUp price increase 2026," they land on SaaSpare and make a purchase decision.

**The traffic picture:**

We launched in April 2026. Current metrics:
- 1,034 indexed buyer pages across 16 verticals
- 6,540 Google Search Console impressions in 17 days (position trending from 35 → 20)
- Programmatic SEO pipeline generating new comparison pages weekly
- Domain Authority growing with active link-building outreach

Traffic is low because we're 3 weeks old — not because the content is thin or the audience is wrong. The impression curve and position improvement both signal rapid growth.

**Why PartnerStack specifically:**

Seven of our 15 tracked tools (HubSpot, Notion, ClickUp, Asana, Monday.com, Pipedrive, Linear) are PartnerStack advertisers. We have dedicated pricing history pages for each. Without PartnerStack approval, we can't monetise the traffic these pages already send to vendor websites.

We're not asking to be treated like an established publisher. We're asking for a 90-day probationary approval — you can review our metrics again at the 90-day mark and remove us if we haven't grown.
