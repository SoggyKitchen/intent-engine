## 4. Promotion methods (for Impact application form)

SaaSpare promotes Advertisers through:

- **Organic search content** (1,000+ comparison, pricing, free-trial, alternatives, deal pages).
- **Onsite recommendations** (tool-vs-tool comparison pages with side-by-side verdicts).
- **Pricing guides** (per-vendor pricing breakdowns linking to vendor pages).
- **Alternatives / "best-of" lists** with editorially-ranked tool selections.
- **Free-trial walkthroughs** that link readers directly to vendor signup pages.
- **Opt-in email newsletter** ("Weekly SaaS Deal Digest") with explicit double-opt-in.
- **Direct buyer recommendations** via `/pages/saas-stack-audit-checkout` Stack Audits.

SaaSpare does **not** use:

- ❌ Fake / bot-generated traffic
- ❌ Cookie stuffing
- ❌ Adware / spyware / malware
- ❌ Hidden iframes
- ❌ Scraped lead lists
- ❌ Spam email
- ❌ Unauthorised brand bidding (no PPC on advertiser brand keywords)
- ❌ Misleading redirects
- ❌ Incentivised clicks (unless explicitly approved by the advertiser)
- ❌ AI-generated comparisons without manual research and source verification

This list is mirrored on `/media-kit` and `/affiliate-disclosure` so reviewers
can see it without leaving the site.
