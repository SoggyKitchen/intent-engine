## STRATEGY: Show Enough, Not Too Much

The goal is to **pass compliance review without handing reviewers a map to your business model**.

**What you MUST show** (compliance is non-negotiable for approval):
- You are a real business (ABN, real name, real email)
- You have an affiliate disclosure that meets FTC/ACCC standards
- Your content is editorial, not fake testimonials or manufactured reviews
- You have a methodology (you don't need to explain every detail of it)

**What you do NOT need to reveal:**
- That content is programmatically generated — just say "editorial comparison content"
- Exact traffic numbers — say "growing organic search traffic" (they can check SimilarWeb)
- Your profit margins or commission structure — they set commissions, not you
- That you use a scoring algorithm — just say "independent editorial scoring rubric"
- How many pages you have in total — lead with quality categories, not raw volume
- That you are a one-person operation — say "independent editorial team"

**The narrative that gets you in:** You are an independent B2B SaaS research platform that publishes comparison guides to help buyers make informed purchase decisions. You monetise through referral commissions disclosed transparently on every page.

## WHAT TO SAY ABOUT TRAFFIC (consistent across all applications)

Never give a specific monthly visitor number unless you know it is above the program's minimum and you can prove it. Use one of these:

- **If asked for a number and traffic is low:** "Growing organically — primarily decision-stage B2B buyers from search. Happy to share analytics access if required."
- **If asked for a number and traffic is moderate:** "Thousands of monthly sessions, growing month-on-month through SEO. Primary audience is decision-stage B2B buyers."
- **If the field is optional:** Leave blank.

---

## WHAT NOT TO SAY (avoid these phrases in every application)

| Phrase to avoid | Why | Use instead |
|----------------|-----|-------------|
| "programmatically generated" | Flags automated/thin content | "editorial comparison content" |
| "1,015 pages" | Invites "are they all thin?" scrutiny | "comparison content across 16 verticals" |
| "AI-written" | Auto-reject at most networks | omit entirely |
| "we are building traffic" | Signals low/no traffic | "growing organic search traffic" |
| "coupon codes" | Some programs reject coupon sites | call it "discount and deal guides" |
| "I run this alone" | Signals low resources | "independent editorial platform" |

## POST-APPROVAL CHECKLIST

Once approved to a network, do these immediately:

1. **Generate tracking links** for your 20 highest-traffic pages (check Cloudflare Analytics)
2. **Replace placeholder `#` affiliate hrefs** with real tracking URLs in those pages
3. **Test every link** — click → confirm it lands on the vendor's pricing or trial page with tracking params visible in the URL
4. **Add the program's required disclosure language** if different from your current banner (check each program's T&Cs under "Publisher Guidelines")
5. **Set a calendar reminder** for 30-day check-in: are commissions tracking? Any clicks with zero conversions could mean broken links

## WHAT REVIEWERS ACTUALLY CHECK (do this manual audit before submitting)

Walk through these yourself before hitting submit on each application:

- [ ] Open `saaspare.org` in an incognito window — does the homepage load cleanly?
- [ ] Open one buyer page (e.g. `/pages/hubspot-pricing-2026-plans-costs-what-you-actually-pay`) — is the affiliate disclosure banner visible **before scrolling**?
- [ ] Is `/affiliate-disclosure` a real, readable page?
- [ ] Is `/methodology` a real, readable page?
- [ ] Is `/about` a real page with a real name and contact details?
- [ ] Does `/contact` work and show a real email or form?
- [ ] Does `/privacy` exist and mention affiliate/advertising relationships?
- [ ] Does `/terms` exist?
- [ ] Send a test email to `partnerships@saaspare.org` — does it arrive in your inbox?
- [ ] Is `https://saaspare.org/ads.txt` returning content? (required for some networks)

If any of these fail, fix before submitting.
