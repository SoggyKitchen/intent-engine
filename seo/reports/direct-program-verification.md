**Stop expecting revenue here.** Keep the pages for SEO/traffic value only; don't
prioritize new Ahrefs content on the assumption it'll ever monetize directly.

## Datadog — ⚠️ Ambiguous

agencies/consultants/resellers, not content/affiliate sites. No clean self-serve
affiliate signup page found. Would need to email their partner team directly to find
out if a content-affiliate path even exists. Low priority given the ambiguity.

## Linear — ❌ No affiliate program
9 pages live. "Linear Startup Program" is free software credits for accelerator-backed
startups — not an affiliate program, nothing to apply to. Remove from monetization
