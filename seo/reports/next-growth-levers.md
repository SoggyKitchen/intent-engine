# Next Growth Levers

The highest ROI additions now are not more generic pages. The site needs better signals, stronger capture, and faster feedback loops.

## Added in this pass

- Cloudflare-first lead capture with FormSubmit fallback.
- Search Console API ingestion for the SEO agent.
- Reports explaining exactly how to wire GSC and Cloudflare Email Service.

## Recommended next additions

1. **GSC-driven refresh queue**: after the service account is connected, use `gsc-opportunities.md` weekly to update only pages Google is already showing.
2. **Source-backed comparison upgrades**: top pages should get vendor pricing source links, last-checked dates, and visible "what changed" notes.
3. **Partner inbox workflow**: once `hello@saaspare.org` is receiving mail, route partner emails into a CSV/import workflow instead of manual copy-paste.
4. **Editorial trust pages**: keep About, Methodology, Affiliate Disclosure, Corrections and Pricing Verification Policy visible in the footer.
5. **Authority outreach list**: build a small weekly list of SaaS newsletters, startup directories, founder communities and tool vendors that can link to specific comparison pages.

## What not to add yet

- Forced login walls.
- User accounts before there is a clear saved-list or renewal-alert feature.
- Broad top-of-funnel articles that do not connect to affiliate buyer intent.
- Fake reviews, fake ratings, invented discounts, or unsupported "tested" claims.
