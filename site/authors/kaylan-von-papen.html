About Kaylan

Kaylan von Papen is the founder of SaaSpare.org, an independent B2B SaaS comparison platform covering software pricing, reviews, and buyer guides across 16 verticals. SaaSpare is built on one rule: no paid rankings, ever.

Kaylan's work focuses on B2B SaaS pricing transparency — tracking real-time price changes, uncovering vendor tactics, and producing buyer guides that give procurement teams and founders the information SaaS vendors don't want them to have. Every review is based on live pricing verification, hands-on testing, and cross-referencing with user data from G2, Capterra, and Reddit.

SaaSpare attracts high-intent buyers at the bottom of the purchase funnel — people searching for specific tool comparisons and pricing by name, actively ready to make a decision.
