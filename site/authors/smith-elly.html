About the Author

Kaylan von Papen is the founder of SaaSpare.org, an independent B2B SaaS comparison platform that reviews, prices, and compares business software without accepting sponsored rankings or undisclosed affiliate deals.

Smith's research focuses on B2B SaaS pricing transparency, tracking price changes, uncovering vendor tactics, and producing buyer guides that give procurement teams and founders the information SaaS vendors don't want them to have.

Every review, comparison, and pricing guide on SaaSpare is independently researched using a consistent methodology: live pricing verification, hands-on testing where possible, and cross-referencing with user reviews from G2, Capterra, and Reddit.

Editorial Standards

  • No paid rankings. Tools are ranked on merit only, never for payment.
  • Affiliate disclosure. Some pages contain affiliate links. These are always disclosed and never influence rankings. Full disclosure policy →
  • Pricing accuracy. All pricing is verified directly against vendor websites and updated when changes are detected.
  • Independence. SaaSpare has no investors, no advertising partnerships, and no ownership stakes in any reviewed software.
