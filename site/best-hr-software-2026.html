Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best HR software for a company under 100 employees?

BambooHR or Gusto are the top picks for companies under 100 employees. Gusto is best if US payroll is your primary need. BambooHR is better for companies needing performance reviews, onboarding workflows, and time tracking on top of payroll.

How much does Rippling cost?

Rippling starts at approximately $8 per user per month for the core platform, with additional modules priced separately. Most mid-size teams pay $15-25 per user per month fully loaded. Rippling does not publish pricing publicly — you need a sales call.
