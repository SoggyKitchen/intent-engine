15 Best SaaS Tools for Remote Teams in 2026

By Kaylan von Papen · 2026-05-25 · Methodology · Tool Guides

Remote teams have different SaaS needs than office teams — you need tools that work async, across time zones, with strong mobile apps and no VPN friction. Here are the 15 best tools for remote-first teams in 2026, ranked by category.

Video & Communication

1. Zoom — still the gold standard for video, $13.33/user/mo. 2. Loom — async video messages that replace 80% of meetings, free for up to 25 videos. 3. Slack — the async HQ for most remote teams, $7.25/user/mo on Pro.

Project Management

4. Linear — fastest issue tracker, loved by engineering teams, $8/user/mo. 5. ClickUp — most flexible for mixed teams, free plan is generous. 6. Notion — docs + wiki + database in one, $8/user/mo.

HR & Payroll (for global teams)

7. Deel — hire contractors and employees in 150+ countries compliantly. 8. Gusto — best for US-based remote teams needing payroll + benefits. 9. BambooHR — mid-market HR with strong remote onboarding flows.

Security & Access

10. 1Password Business — team password manager with zero-knowledge architecture, $7.99/user/mo. 11. NordLayer — business VPN with dedicated IPs for remote access, from $8/user/mo. 12. Okta — SSO and zero-trust access for teams with 50+ apps.

Productivity & Files

13. Tresorit — end-to-end encrypted cloud storage for sensitive files, $24/user/mo Business. 14. Miro — virtual whiteboard for remote workshops, free for 3 boards. 15. Canva Teams — design without a designer, $14.99/user/mo.

How to Choose Your Remote Stack

Start with communication (Slack/Zoom), project tracking (Linear/ClickUp), and security (1Password). Add HR/payroll only when you have employees in multiple countries. Avoid tool sprawl — 5 tools used deeply beat 15 tools half-used.

Frequently Asked Questions

What SaaS tools do remote teams need?

The essential stack: a video tool (Zoom or Loom), a project manager (Linear or ClickUp), a team chat (Slack), a password manager (1Password), and cloud storage (Tresorit or Google Drive).

What is the best project management tool for remote teams?

Linear is best for engineering and product teams. ClickUp is best for mixed teams with varied workflows. Notion works best when you need docs and projects in one place.

How much does a full remote team SaaS stack cost?

A typical 10-person remote team pays $800-$1,500/month for their full stack: Slack Pro ($72.50), Zoom Pro ($133.30), Linear ($80), 1Password ($79.90), and one HR tool.
