Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ahrefs or Semrush better?

Semrush has more features — PPC research, content tools, social. Ahrefs has the stronger backlink database preferred by link builders. For most SEO professionals, Semrush provides better value per dollar. Note: Ahrefs has no affiliate program, which means independent reviews tend to be less biased.

How much does Semrush cost in 2026?

Semrush Pro starts at $140/month (monthly) or $117/month (annual). The Guru plan for historical data and content tools is $250/month. Watch for the 10-project and 3,000-reports/day limits on Pro.

What is the best free SEO tool?

Google Search Console is the most useful free SEO tool — it shows exactly which queries drive traffic for your site. Semrush and Ahrefs both offer limited free tiers for keyword research.
