Methodology

Pricing data was collected from vendor pricing pages and verified against plan changelog records for 1,041 B2B SaaS products. Collection dates: January–April 2026. Where pricing is quote-only, the lowest available published tier or publicly reported base price was used. All prices are USD per month, billed monthly unless noted. Seat minimums were confirmed by attempting to initiate checkout flows at single-seat quantities.

  • n = 1,041 products across 40 categories
  • Categories required minimum 10 products to be included
  • Year-over-year comparisons use publicly archived pricing from January 2023
  • Enterprise-only products without published pricing are excluded from median calculations
