Common questions

Salesforce pricing history FAQ

Has Salesforce raised prices recently?
Yes — Salesforce raised Enterprise and Unlimited pricing by 9% in June 2024, the first significant increase since 2020. They also bundled AI features into higher tiers, effectively increasing costs for many customers.
What does Salesforce really cost for 10 users?
On Starter Suite at $25/user/mo, 10 users costs $3,000/year. Add implementation ($5,000–20,000), admin (0.5–1 FTE), and add-ons — you're looking at $20,000–50,000/year total.
Can you negotiate Salesforce pricing?
Yes — significantly. Salesforce salespeople have large discount authority, especially at end of quarter. 20–40% off list price is achievable for multi-year deals.
Is there a cheaper alternative to Salesforce?
HubSpot Professional at $890/mo for 5 users is ~70% cheaper than equivalent Salesforce Enterprise. Pipedrive is even cheaper at $14–99/user/mo with no hidden implementation costs.
Does Salesforce have a free plan?
No — Salesforce's cheapest option is Starter Suite at $25/user/mo, billed annually. There is no free tier.
