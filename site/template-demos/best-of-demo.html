Common questions

CRM buyer FAQ

Which CRM is best for small businesses in 2026?
HubSpot CRM is the best starting point — genuinely unlimited free tier, fast setup, strong email and deal tracking. For tight budgets, Freshsales ($9/user) or Zoho (free up to 3 users) are excellent alternatives.
What is the cheapest CRM with a free plan?
HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Freshsales all offer free plans. HubSpot's is the most generous — no seat cap, no contact limit.
Is Salesforce worth it for small teams?
Rarely. Salesforce demands dedicated admin resources. True TCO is 2–3× the licence cost. HubSpot or Pipedrive will be faster and far cheaper for teams under 50 seats.
