The fine print

Hidden costs to watch

⚠
Onboarding fee
$3,000+ mandatory for Professional and above — not optional, rarely disclosed on the pricing page.
🔒
Seat minimums
Professional requires 3 paid seats minimum — even if you only need 1 power user.
👥
Annual contract lock-in
Monthly billing is available but costs ~25–30% more. Most buyers get locked in annually without realising.
📦
Hub add-ons
Marketing, Sales, Service, and CMS Hubs are each billed separately. A full stack easily reaches $3,000+/mo.
📈
Marketing contact tiers
Marketing Hub charges per marketing contact you email — a large list drives significant additional cost on top of seat pricing.

Common questions

HubSpot pricing FAQ

Does HubSpot have a free plan?
Yes — HubSpot CRM is free with no seat cap and no contact limit. The free plan includes deal pipeline, contact management, and basic email marketing.
How much does HubSpot actually cost for 10 users?
For a team of 10 on Starter (CRM only), expect around $150/mo billed annually. Add Marketing Hub and you're looking at $400–800/mo depending on contact tier.
Is HubSpot's onboarding fee mandatory?
Yes for Professional and Enterprise — HubSpot charges $3,000–$6,000 in mandatory onboarding fees. This is not optional and is rarely disclosed prominently.
Does HubSpot charge per contact?
Marketing Hub charges per marketing contact. CRM contacts are unlimited on all plans. A large email list can significantly increase your Marketing Hub cost.
Is there a HubSpot discount for startups?
Yes — HubSpot for Startups offers 30–90% off for qualifying startups under $2M ARR. Apply at hubspot.com/startups.
