What happens when it ends

⏱
Account stays active
Your HubSpot account doesn't disappear — it reverts to the Free plan. All your contacts and data are preserved.
💳
No auto-charge
Because no credit card is required for the free trial, you won't be charged automatically when it ends.
📁
Pro features lock
Workflows, sequences, and automation you built during the trial will pause. You'll need a paid plan to reactivate them.
🔔
Data is retained
All contacts, deals, and notes remain accessible on the Free plan — nothing is deleted when the trial ends.
Common questions

HubSpot trial FAQ

Does HubSpot free trial require a credit card?
No — HubSpot's free trial requires only a work email. No payment details are collected upfront, so there's no risk of accidental charges.
How long is HubSpot's free trial?
The Professional tier trial is 14 days. The free CRM plan is unlimited — you can use it forever at no cost.
Can I extend my HubSpot trial?
In some cases yes — contact your HubSpot sales rep and ask for an extension. Extensions of 7–14 days are sometimes granted if you're actively evaluating.
What's the difference between HubSpot Free and the trial?
The trial gives you full Professional access (automation, sequences, custom reports). The Free plan is the permanently free CRM with unlimited contacts but no automation.
Can I trial multiple HubSpot Hubs at once?
Yes — you can activate trials for CRM, Marketing, Sales, and Service Hubs simultaneously. Each has its own 14-day window.

Ready to try HubSpot free for 14 days?

Instant access. No payment required. Downgrade to free anytime.
