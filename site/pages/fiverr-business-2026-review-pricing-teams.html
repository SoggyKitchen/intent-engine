What Is Fiverr Business?

Fiverr Business is a dedicated workspace for teams that hire freelancers regularly. Unlike a standard Fiverr account, it centralises your team's spending under one dashboard, lets managers approve or assign work, and gives access to a curated pool of Fiverr Pro sellers — vetted for quality across 500+ categories.

No subscription required. You pay only for the projects you commission. The platform fee is the same as regular Fiverr (up to 5.5% service fee per order). The business features — dashboard, invoicing, collaboration — are included at no extra charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fiverr Business free?
Yes — Fiverr Business has no subscription fee. You pay only for projects you commission. A service fee (up to 5.5%) applies to each order, the same as regular Fiverr. The business dashboard, team management, and invoicing features are included at no extra charge.
What is Fiverr Business vs regular Fiverr?
Fiverr Business adds a centralized team dashboard, consolidated invoicing, priority access to vetted Pro sellers, a dedicated customer success manager, and per-user spending controls. Regular Fiverr is a solo account without these team management features.
Is Fiverr Pro worth it for businesses?
Fiverr Pro sellers are vetted by Fiverr's quality team — they represent roughly the top 1% of sellers. For businesses needing reliable, high-quality output on a deadline, the vetted pool reduces trial-and-error time. The Pro premium is typically 2–5× the regular gig price, which is often justified for mission-critical work.
How does Fiverr Business pricing work?
Fiverr Business is free to activate. You pay per project at the seller's listed gig price, plus Fiverr's service fee. There's no monthly subscription. All orders from your team are consolidated on one monthly invoice, making it easy to track and reconcile spending.
What types of work is Fiverr Business best for?
Fiverr Business excels for well-defined, recurring tasks: graphic design, copywriting, video editing, social media content, SEO audits, and web development sprints. It's less suited to long-term embedded roles or complex projects requiring daily team collaboration.
