Frequently Asked Questions

Is SE Ranking better than SpyFu?

It depends on your needs. SE Ranking is superior for comprehensive SEO management, including site audits and keyword tracking. SpyFu wins in competitive intelligence, especially for PPC and historical keyword data. For most teams, SE Ranking offers broader functionality.

Which is cheaper, SE Ranking or SpyFu?

At entry-level, both start at $39/month. However, SE Ranking offers more features per tier, making it more cost-effective for SEO-heavy use cases. SpyFu becomes significantly more expensive at higher usage levels, especially for large teams.

Can I switch from SpyFu to SE Ranking?

Yes, switching is straightforward. SE Ranking supports CSV imports for keywords and domains, and its interface is intuitive for users familiar with SEO tools. Most teams can migrate within a week with minimal data loss.

Does SE Ranking or SpyFu have a free plan?

Neither offers a permanent free plan. SE Ranking provides a 14-day free trial with full access. SpyFu offers a 7-day trial but no free tier. Both require credit card details to start the trial.

Which has better customer support, SE Ranking or SpyFu?

SE Ranking offers 24/7 live chat, email, and a comprehensive knowledge base with faster response times (under 2 hours). SpyFu support is limited to email and help center, with average response times of 24+ hours, making SE Ranking the clear winner in support quality.

Is SE Ranking or SpyFu better for small teams?

SE Ranking is better for small teams due to its intuitive workflow, white-label reporting, and scalable pricing. It supports collaboration and client management, while SpyFu is more suited for individual analysts focused on competitive data.

Does SE Ranking integrate with SpyFu?

No, there is no native integration between SE Ranking and SpyFu. However, both allow CSV exports, enabling manual data transfer. Users often run both tools in parallel for competitive validation but must manage data syncing externally.

Which tool has more features, SE Ranking or SpyFu?

SE Ranking has more features overall, including site audits, rank tracking, backlink monitoring, content optimizer, and white-label reporting. SpyFu focuses on competitive analysis with tools like Kombat and Fili, offering depth in rivalry insights but fewer on-page SEO capabilities.

Feature Deep Dive

SE Ranking offers a full SEO suite including Rank Tracker, Site Audit, Backlink Checker, and Content Analyzer with optimization suggestions. Its White Label Center allows agencies to automate client reporting. SpyFu counters with Kombat (domain vs. domain keyword comparison), Fili (historical keyword data), and ad history tracking for Google Ads. While SpyFu excels in revealing competitors’ past and present strategies, SE Ranking provides more actionable on-site tools for improving organic performance. For teams needing both competitive insight and execution tools, SE Ranking delivers greater end-to-end value.

Pricing Breakdown

SE Ranking starts at $39/month (Starter) for 10 projects and 500 keywords, scaling to $299/month (Business) for 100 projects and 5,000 keywords. SpyFu starts at $39/month (Basic) for 1,000 keyword checks, then $79 (Standard), $199 (Professional), and $399 (Team) for larger volumes. SE Ranking includes more features like site audits and backlink analysis even in lower tiers, while SpyFu charges more for high-volume competitive data access. SE Ranking also allows unlimited reports and users in higher plans, making it more scalable for agencies.

Who Should Use SE Ranking

SE Ranking is ideal for digital marketing agencies, in-house SEO teams, and consultants managing multiple websites. It suits teams of 2–20 users who need white-label reporting, technical SEO diagnostics, and collaborative workflows. With transparent pricing and strong automation, it’s perfect for businesses scaling their SEO operations and requiring a single platform for tracking, auditing, and optimizing performance.

Who Should Use SpyFu

SpyFu is best for PPC specialists, competitive intelligence analysts, and solo marketers focused on reverse-engineering competitors’ search strategies. It’s particularly valuable for ad agencies crafting Google Ads campaigns or businesses entering competitive markets. While less robust for on-page SEO, its historical data and ad copy insights make it a niche powerhouse for strategic planning.

Migration & Setup

Migrating from SpyFu to SE Ranking takes 3–7 days, with both platforms supporting CSV exports for keywords, domains, and rankings. SE Ranking’s onboarding is smoother, featuring guided setup, video tutorials, and 24/7 chat support. While no direct integration exists, most data can be manually transferred, and SE Ranking’s API allows for partial automation in larger deployments.
