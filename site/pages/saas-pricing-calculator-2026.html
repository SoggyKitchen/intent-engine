How to Calculate Your SaaS Spend

Four-step framework:

  1. Inventory: List every paid SaaS tool. Pull credit card statements - you'll find tools nobody remembers signing up for.
  2. Per-seat math: For seat-based tools, multiply monthly cost x number of seats x 12 (or annual price).
  3. Add overage estimates: Usage-based tools (Datadog, Stripe, Twilio) can vary 2-3x. Average last 6 months.
  4. Add hidden fees: Implementation, premium support, integrations, sandbox environments often cost extra.

How to Reduce SaaS Spend by 20-40%

  1. Audit annually. 30-40% of seats are typically unused. Reclaim them.
  2. Switch to annual billing. Average 20% savings vs monthly.
  3. Negotiate at renewal. Mention competitor. Average 15-30% discount triggered.
  4. Right-size plans. Most teams pay for Professional features but only use Starter capabilities.
  5. Consolidate duplicates. Notion + Confluence + Coda is wasteful. Pick one.
