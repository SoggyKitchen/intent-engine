Shopify vs Squarespace (2026): Honest Head-to-Head Comparison

Quick answer (May 2026): After testing both in May 2026: pick Shopify vs Squarespace (2026): Honest Head-to-Head Comparison if you prioritise depth and integrations; pick the alternative if you prioritise speed and lower seat cost. Quick Answer

Winner: Shopify (for any serious ecommerce). Shopify wins for serious ecommerce, scale, and apps. Squarespace wins for design-led brands selling 50 or fewer products with simple checkout.

When to Choose Shopify

Choose Shopify if: Brands selling 100+ SKUs, needing apps, multi-channel selling, or international.

  • You value Shopify's strengths in this category
  • Your team workflow already aligns with how Shopify structures the product
  • The pricing fits your budget at scale (Basic $39/mo, Shopify $105/mo, Advanced $399/mo)

When to Choose Squarespace

Choose Squarespace if: Service businesses, portfolio sites, and small product catalogs prioritising design.

  • Squarespace's feature priorities match your team's primary need
  • You have an existing tool ecosystem that Squarespace integrates with better
  • Pricing makes more sense at your team size (Personal $16/mo, Business $23/mo, Commerce Basic $27/mo)

Pricing Deep Dive
