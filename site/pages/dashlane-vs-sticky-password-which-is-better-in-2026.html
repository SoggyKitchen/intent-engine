Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dashlane better than Sticky Password?

Dashlane offers more advanced security features, seamless integrations, and robust customer support, making it the better choice for teams seeking a comprehensive password manager.

Which is cheaper, Dashlane or Sticky Password?

Sticky Password is cheaper than Dashlane, with a premium plan starting at $2.99/month (billed annually).

Can I switch from Sticky Password to Dashlane?

Yes, you can switch from Sticky Password to Dashlane. Dashlane offers a secure data export feature, making it easy to transfer your data to the new password manager.

Does Dashlane or Sticky Password have a free plan?

Dashlane offers a free plan, but it has limited features. Sticky Password does not offer a free plan.

Which has better customer support, Dashlane or Sticky Password?

Dashlane offers 24/7 customer support, while Sticky Password offers 24/5 customer support.

Is Dashlane or Sticky Password better for small teams?

Sticky Password is ideal for small teams or individuals requiring a simple, user-friendly password manager.

Does Dashlane integrate with Sticky Password?

No, Dashlane does not integrate with Sticky Password. However, you can export your data from Sticky Password and import it into Dashlane.

Which tool has more features, Dashlane or Sticky Password?

Dashlane has more features than Sticky Password, including advanced security features, seamless integrations, and robust customer support.

Feature Deep Dive

Dashlane offers advanced security features, including a password generator, auto-fill feature, and two-factor authentication. It also offers seamless integrations with over 200 applications, including Google Drive, Dropbox, and Slack. Sticky Password, on the other hand, offers a simple and intuitive interface, as well as a password generator and auto-fill feature. However, it has fewer integrations and limited advanced security features.

Pricing Breakdown

Dashlane offers a personal plan starting at $3.33/month (billed annually), a premium plan starting at $4.99/month (billed annually), and a family plan starting at $6.66/month (billed annually). Sticky Password offers a premium plan starting at $2.99/month (billed annually) and a business plan starting at $4.99/month (billed annually).

Who Should Use Dashlane

Dashlane is ideal for teams seeking advanced security features, seamless integrations, and robust customer support. It is suitable for teams of all sizes, from small startups to large enterprises.

Who Should Use Sticky Password

Sticky Password is ideal for small teams or individuals requiring a simple, user-friendly password manager. It is suitable for teams with limited technical expertise or those who require a basic password manager.

Migration & Setup

Dashlane offers a secure data export feature, making it easy to transfer your data to the new password manager. Sticky Password also offers a secure data export feature, but it may require more technical expertise to set up. The typical onboarding time for Dashlane is 1-2 hours, while Sticky Password may take 2-3 hours to set up.
