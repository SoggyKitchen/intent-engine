Frequently Asked Questions

Is Netsuite better than Airbase?

Yes, Netsuite is a more comprehensive ERP system with stronger customer support, but Airbase is a better option for mid-sized companies with growing finance teams.

Which is cheaper, Netsuite or Airbase?

Airbase is significantly cheaper, with a starting price of $5/user/month compared to Netsuite's $99/user/month.

Can I switch from Airbase to Netsuite?

Yes, it's possible to switch from Airbase to Netsuite, but it may require significant customization and training for your finance team.

Does Netsuite or Airbase have a free plan?

No, neither Netsuite nor Airbase offers a free plan, but Airbase offers a 14-day free trial.

Which has better customer support, Netsuite or Airbase?

Netsuite has stronger customer support, with 24/7 support available, compared to Airbase's 24/5 support.

Is Netsuite or Airbase better for small teams?

Airbase is a better option for small teams, providing a user-friendly expense management tool with automated tracking and approval features.

Does Netsuite integrate with Airbase?

Yes, Netsuite integrates with Airbase, allowing for seamless data transfer between the two systems.

Which tool has more features, Netsuite or Airbase?

Netsuite has more features, including a comprehensive ERP system, supply chain management, and customer relationship management tools, compared to Airbase's expense management features.

Feature Deep Dive

Netsuite offers a comprehensive ERP system with features such as financial management, supply chain management, and customer relationship management. Airbase, on the other hand, provides a user-friendly expense management tool with automated tracking and approval features. While Netsuite has more features, Airbase is a better option for mid-sized companies with growing finance teams.

Pricing Breakdown

Netsuite offers a tiered pricing model with four plans: Core ($99/user/month), Select ($109/user/month), Premier ($129/user/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). Airbase offers a tiered pricing model with three plans: Starter ($5/user/month), Pro ($10/user/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing).

Who Should Use Netsuite

Netsuite is ideal for large enterprises with complex financial operations, requiring a comprehensive ERP system with strong customer support. This includes companies with multiple locations, complex supply chains, and large finance teams.

Who Should Use Airbase

Airbase is ideal for mid-sized companies with growing finance teams, requiring a user-friendly expense management tool with automated tracking and approval features. This includes companies with small to medium-sized finance teams and limited budget.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Airbase to Netsuite may require significant customization and training for your finance team. Netsuite offers a dedicated onboarding team to help with the migration process, which can take several weeks to complete.
