Speed Comparison

In independent 2026 speed tests, Surfshark averages 500–700 Mbps download via WireGuard on nearby servers. ProtonVPN averages 400–600 Mbps — fast enough for 4K streaming and low-latency gaming, but slightly behind. For most users on standard broadband, both are effectively as fast as your ISP connection.

ProtonVPN's Secure Core routing adds meaningful latency (50–120ms extra) because traffic bounces through a hardened server first. If you use standard servers, speeds are comparable to Surfshark.

Privacy & Security Comparison

ProtonVPN holds the privacy crown. Open source apps, multiple independent audits, Swiss jurisdiction, no-logs verified in court, and a free tier that earns them trust rather than money from your data. This is a VPN built specifically for whistleblowers, journalists, and activists — and it shows in every design decision.

Surfshark is not far behind. RAM-only servers (all data wiped on reboot), a Netherlands jurisdiction (EU law, strong privacy protections), and a passed no-logs audit from Deloitte in 2023. It merged with Nord Security in 2022 but operates independently.

Streaming Performance

Both unblock Netflix US, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, and most major streaming services. Surfshark has historically had a slight edge for streaming reliability — their server infrastructure is optimised for geo-unblocking. ProtonVPN's Plus plan includes Streaming servers designated specifically for streaming, which perform very well in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ProtonVPN better than Surfshark?

Surfshark wins for value: unlimited devices and cheaper at ~$2.19/month on a 2-year plan. ProtonVPN wins for privacy: open source, court-proven no-logs, and run by the team behind ProtonMail. For budget buyers or families, Surfshark is the better pick. For privacy-first users, ProtonVPN is stronger.

Which is faster, ProtonVPN or Surfshark?

Both are fast in 2026. Surfshark is slightly faster on average in independent speed tests thanks to WireGuard by default. ProtonVPN is close behind and fast enough for 4K streaming and gaming on most servers.

Does ProtonVPN have a free plan?

Yes. ProtonVPN is the only VPN we recommend with a genuinely free plan — no data cap, no ads, no selling your data. It is limited to 1 device and slower servers. Surfshark does not have a free plan.

Is Surfshark safe?

Yes. Surfshark has passed independent no-logs audits, uses RAM-only servers, and supports WireGuard and OpenVPN. It is a trustworthy VPN for everyday use, streaming, and travel.

Can I use Surfshark on unlimited devices?

Yes — Surfshark allows unlimited simultaneous device connections, making it the best VPN for households or small teams. ProtonVPN Plus allows 10 devices.
