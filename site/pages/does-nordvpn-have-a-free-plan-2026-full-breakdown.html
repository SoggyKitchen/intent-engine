The 7-Day iOS & macOS Free Trial

If you use iPhone, iPad, or Mac, NordVPN offers a genuine 7-day free trial via the Apple App Store:

Yes — it is broadly considered genuine. Users across Reddit and review platforms report successful refunds with minimal friction. Contact live chat within 30 days of purchase, state your reason (or no reason), and the refund is processed.
