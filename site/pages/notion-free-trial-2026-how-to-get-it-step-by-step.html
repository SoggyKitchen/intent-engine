Frequently Asked Questions

Does Notion have a free trial?

Yes — Free plan available. Generous free plan.

Is a credit card required for the Notion free trial?

It depends on the plan. For most Notion free tiers, no credit card is required. Paid trials may require a card but you won't be charged until the trial ends.

What happens after the Notion free trial ends?

After the trial, your account moves to the free tier (if available) or pauses until you add payment. You won't lose your data. Notion will email you before the trial expires.

Can I extend the Notion free trial?

Yes — contact Notion support before your trial expires and ask for an extension. Most companies grant 7-14 extra days, especially if you explain you need more time to evaluate with your team.

How do I cancel Notion during the trial?

Cancel via your account settings before the trial ends to avoid charges. Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Notion typically confirms cancellation by email.

Is the Notion free tier good enough?

Generous free plan. For solo users or very small teams, the free tier may be sufficient. For professional use, paid plans unlock features like advanced reporting, team collaboration, and API access.

What the Notion Free Trial Includes

The Notion free trial gives full access to all features — including features on the paid plan — for Free plan available. This is your chance to test the tool with real data, not a demo.

How to Get the Most From Your Trial

Week 1 checklist: Set up your real workflow (not a test project), invite your whole team, connect your integrations, run a real project end-to-end. By Day 7 you should know if it fits.

After the Trial: Notion Pricing

Notion paid plans start at $0 (Free). Annual billing saves ~20%. If you're on the fence, ask sales for a 30-day extension — most will say yes.

Alternatives If the Trial Doesn't Convert You
