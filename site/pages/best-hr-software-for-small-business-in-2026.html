Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best hr software for small business for small business owners and HR managers?

Gusto is our top pick for small business owners and HR managers — excellent features.

Is there a free hr for small business?

Yes — Rippling, Deel have free plans. Gusto also offers a free trial.

How do I choose the right tool for my team?

Consider: team size, budget, must-have features, and current tools you use. We recommend trialling Gusto and BambooHR side-by-side since both offer free access.

What should I look for when comparing these tools?

Key criteria: pricing per user at your team size, free trial length and completeness, integration with your existing stack, mobile app quality, and customer support response time.

How We Ranked These Tools

We evaluated each tool on: pricing value, feature depth, ease of onboarding, reliability, customer support quality, and integration ecosystem. Rankings reflect best fit for small business owners and HR managers, not raw feature count.
