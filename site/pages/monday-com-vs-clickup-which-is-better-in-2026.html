Frequently Asked Questions

Is Monday.com better than ClickUp?

It depends on your priorities. Monday.com excels in usability, design, and enterprise readiness, making it better for teams that value simplicity and scalability. ClickUp offers more features out of the box and is more cost-effective, but with a steeper learning curve.

Which is cheaper, Monday.com or ClickUp?

ClickUp is generally cheaper, especially at the mid-tier. Its Unlimited plan at $7/user/month includes more features than Monday.com’s $10/user/month Standard plan. However, Monday.com’s free plan is more limited, while ClickUp’s Free Forever plan supports unlimited tasks and members.

Can I switch from ClickUp to Monday.com?

Yes, you can migrate from ClickUp to Monday.com using CSV exports or third-party tools like Zapier and Make. While not fully automated, most task, project, and comment data can be transferred with moderate effort and cleanup.

Does Monday.com or ClickUp have a free plan?

Both offer free plans. Monday.com’s free plan supports up to two users and basic features. ClickUp’s Free Forever plan includes unlimited tasks, members, and 1,000 automation runs per month, making it significantly more generous.

Which has better customer support, Monday.com or ClickUp?

Monday.com offers 24/7 support on its Pro plan and above, with faster response times and dedicated customer success managers for Enterprise. ClickUp provides 24/7 chat and email on all paid plans, but phone support is limited to Enterprise. Monday.com edges out in support quality and responsiveness.

Is Monday.com or ClickUp better for small teams?

Small teams on a budget may prefer ClickUp due to its rich free plan and low-cost scaling. However, teams valuing ease of use and quick onboarding may find Monday.com more effective despite the higher cost.

Does Monday.com integrate with ClickUp?

There is no native two-way integration between Monday.com and ClickUp. However, you can connect them using third-party automation platforms like Zapier, Make, or PieSync to sync tasks, updates, or status changes.

Which tool has more features, Monday.com or ClickUp?

ClickUp has more built-in features, including docs, goals, chat, time tracking, and workload management, all in one app. Monday.com focuses on core project management with strong automations and dashboards, but requires integrations for some functionality like chat or docs.

Feature Deep Dive

Monday.com shines with its Workload view, Timeline (Gantt), and Forms—ideal for managing capacity and client intake. Its Automations Center allows rule-based triggers without coding. ClickUp counters with ClickUp Docs, Goals with KPIs, and native time tracking via ClickUp Time. While ClickUp offers more features, Monday.com’s features are more polished and easier to adopt. ClickUp’s Views (List, Board, Calendar, etc.) are highly flexible, but configuration complexity can slow adoption.

Pricing Breakdown

Monday.com starts at $8/user/month (Basic) and goes to $16/user/month (Pro), with Enterprise plans requiring a quote. The Standard plan at $10 is typically needed for most teams. ClickUp’s Unlimited plan at $7/user/month includes features like custom fields, integrations, and 100MB file uploads—comparable to Monday.com’s Standard. ClickUp’s Business plan at $12 adds advanced reporting and admin controls. For teams under 10, ClickUp is more cost-effective; for larger teams needing simplicity, Monday.com justifies its premium.

Who Should Use Monday.com

Monday.com is ideal for mid-sized to large organizations that need a scalable, visually intuitive platform for managing complex workflows across departments. Teams in marketing, operations, and professional services benefit from its clean UI and automation. It suits companies with budgets to invest in ease of use and long-term adoption, especially those prioritizing security, compliance, and support.

Who Should Use ClickUp

ClickUp is best for small to mid-sized teams seeking an all-in-one workspace without paying for multiple tools. Startups and tech teams that want docs, tasks, goals, and chat in one place will appreciate its depth. It’s also ideal for budget-conscious buyers who can dedicate time to configuration and training to unlock its full potential.

Migration & Setup

Switching between ClickUp and Monday.com is feasible but requires planning. Both support CSV imports/exports for tasks and projects. ClickUp’s data export is more comprehensive, including comments and attachments. Onboarding typically takes 1–2 weeks, depending on customization. Third-party tools like Zapier can automate partial syncs, but full migration often needs manual cleanup for accuracy.

Evaluation Criteria

When choosing between Monday.com and ClickUp, prioritize pricing structure, onboarding effort, integration needs, support responsiveness, and team fit. Consider whether your team values simplicity over feature density. Avoid decisions based solely on feature count—usability and long-term adoption are more critical for ROI.
