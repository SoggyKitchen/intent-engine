When to Use Elementor vs WPBakery

Use Elementor when:

  • You are starting a new WordPress website from scratch
  • You need WooCommerce product pages, cart, or checkout design control
  • You want full control over headers, footers, and archive templates
  • Performance and clean HTML output matters for your SEO strategy
  • You want a free plan to start without paying upfront

Use WPBakery when:

  • Your existing WordPress theme (typically from ThemeForest/Envato) was built with WPBakery and rebuilding is not feasible
  • Your client or development team is deeply familiar with WPBakery's backend editor
  • You need lifetime pricing and Elementor's annual model doesn't fit your budget model

Switching from WPBakery to Elementor

Migrating from WPBakery to Elementor requires manually rebuilding your pages — there is no automated migration tool that reliably converts WPBakery shortcodes to Elementor widgets. The typical process:

  1. Install Elementor alongside WPBakery (both can coexist temporarily)
  2. Rebuild each page in Elementor — typically 1–3 hours per page
  3. Switch the page template from WPBakery to Elementor once rebuilt
  4. Deactivate WPBakery once all pages are migrated

For large sites, use a staging environment and migrate in batches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Elementor better than WPBakery?
Yes, for most users. Elementor scores 8.6/10 vs WPBakery's 7.4/10. Elementor offers a free plan, a live frontend editor with real-time previews, WooCommerce integration, and far faster page loading than WPBakery. WPBakery is still widely installed (often bundled with ThemeForest themes) but its backend editor and shortcode-heavy output are considered legacy by 2026 standards.
Is WPBakery free?
No. WPBakery has no free plan. It costs $69/year for a single-site license or $299/year for unlimited sites. It often comes bundled with ThemeForest themes but requires a separate purchase to use independently. Elementor, by contrast, offers a genuinely free version (Elementor Free) with no time limit.
Does Elementor replace WPBakery?
Elementor can fully replace WPBakery for new sites. If you already have a WPBakery-built site, switching requires rebuilding your pages in Elementor because WPBakery uses proprietary shortcodes that don't transfer. For new projects, Elementor is the recommended choice: better performance, active development, and a free tier.
Is WPBakery still worth using in 2026?
Only if your existing WordPress theme is built around WPBakery (common with ThemeForest themes). For new builds, WPBakery is not recommended — its shortcode-based rendering creates page bloat, it lacks a proper theme builder, and its frontend editor lags behind Elementor's real-time experience. Elementor is the better long-term investment.
Which is faster, Elementor or WPBakery?
Elementor is generally faster, especially with its Optimized DOM and asset lazy-loading features introduced in recent versions. WPBakery's shortcode output adds hidden HTML and scripts that inflate page size. Both require a good hosting setup and caching plugin to perform well, but Elementor's output is cleaner.
Can I use Elementor and WPBakery on the same site?
Technically yes, but it is not recommended. Running both page builders simultaneously slows down your site, creates CSS/JS conflicts, and makes maintenance harder. If you are migrating from WPBakery to Elementor, rebuild pages one at a time and then deactivate WPBakery once all pages are migrated.
