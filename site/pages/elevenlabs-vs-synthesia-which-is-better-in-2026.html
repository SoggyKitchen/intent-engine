Tool Overviews

ElevenLabs
Free · Starter $5/mo · 29+ languages · Voice cloning

ElevenLabs is the leading AI voice platform, known for producing the most natural-sounding synthetic speech available. Its key differentiators are voice cloning (replicate any voice from a short audio sample), multilingual dubbing that preserves the original speaker's vocal characteristics, and a developer API used by podcast platforms, audiobook publishers, and game studios. The free tier (10,000 characters/month, no credit card required) is one of the most generous in the AI voice space and is enough to evaluate quality before committing. Paid plans scale from $5/mo (Starter, 30K chars) to $330/mo (Scale, 2M chars), with Pro at $99/mo covering most production workflows.

Synthesia
Starter $22/mo · 160+ avatars · 120+ languages · No camera

Synthesia is an AI video creation platform designed to produce talking-head presenter videos without a camera, studio, or on-screen talent. Its 160+ stock AI avatars speak 120+ languages, making it the go-to tool for multinational L&D and corporate communications teams producing training content at scale. The workflow is slide-based, import a script or a PowerPoint, pick an avatar, and render a polished video. Starter ($22/mo) includes 120 videos per year (roughly 10/month); Creator ($67/mo) scales to 360 videos/year. Synthesia has no affiliate program on SaaSpare, the link goes directly to synthesia.io.

Pricing in Detail

ElevenLabs prices by the character: Free (10,000 chars/mo), Starter ($5/mo, 30,000 chars), Creator ($22/mo, 100,000 chars), Pro ($99/mo, 500,000 chars), and Scale ($330/mo, 2,000,000 chars). All paid plans include commercial use rights. Voice cloning is available from the Creator plan upward; the Starter plan gives access to the voice library but limits cloning. For audio-only content, podcast narration, audiobook production, e-learning voiceovers, ElevenLabs is the most cost-effective AI voice tool available.

Synthesia prices by video output: Starter ($22/mo, 120 videos/year, roughly 10 per month), Creator ($67/mo, 360 videos/year, roughly 30 per month). Enterprise pricing is custom. There is no ongoing free plan; a limited demo lets you generate a sample video to evaluate quality. For teams producing more than 10 videos per month, the Creator plan is typically required. The per-video framing can feel restrictive for high-volume use; unlimited edits within a video are included, so reworking content doesn't count against the cap.

Head-to-head on price: ElevenLabs Starter ($5/mo) is significantly cheaper than Synthesia Starter ($22/mo), but they produce different outputs. If you need both audio narration and video, you'd either use ElevenLabs audio inside a video editor, or use Synthesia as your all-in-one video tool. Running both simultaneously is common in enterprise content teams.

Where They Overlap (and When to Use Both)

ElevenLabs and Synthesia solve different problems, ElevenLabs outputs audio files, Synthesia outputs video with an on-screen presenter. But they overlap in one scenario: AI-narrated video where you want to control the voice separately from the avatar.

Some teams use ElevenLabs to clone a specific speaker's voice, export the audio track, and import it into Synthesia to sync with a custom avatar. This gives you the most natural-sounding voice (ElevenLabs) combined with a professional on-screen presenter (Synthesia), a workflow increasingly common in large-scale multilingual content production.

If your use case is purely audio (podcasts, audiobooks, IVR, game dialogue, e-learning narration without video), ElevenLabs is all you need. If your use case is talking-head video, training modules, corporate communications, product demos, Synthesia is the dedicated tool. Only if you need both the best voice and the best avatar does combining them make sense.

Dubbing Existing Video vs Creating New Video

ElevenLabs Dubbing takes an existing video file and re-records the speech in a new language, preserving the original speaker's vocal characteristics. This is transformative for content creators who record once and need to reach 10+ language markets. The translated audio replaces the original track; lip sync quality has improved significantly through 2025–2026. This is a workflow Synthesia does not offer.

Synthesia creates new video from a script, you never need existing footage. The avatar is always AI-generated. This is ideal for teams who have no original on-camera content and need a polished video presenter regardless. Synthesia doesn't dub existing content; it generates from scratch.

If your workflow is "I have a recorded English video and I need it in 12 languages," use ElevenLabs. If your workflow is "I have a script and I need a professional-looking video without filming anything," use Synthesia.

Final Verdict

ElevenLabs (9.1/10) is the best AI voice platform available. If your work is audio-first, podcasts, audiobooks, e-learning narration, game dialogue, video dubbing, or any product that speaks to users, ElevenLabs is the clear choice. The free tier makes it risk-free to evaluate, and the voice quality is measurably ahead of alternatives. Start on the Free plan, upgrade to Starter ($5/mo) when you need more characters.

Synthesia (8.6/10) wins when you need to put a presenter on screen without filming. For corporate L&D teams, internal communications, and multilingual product walkthroughs, Synthesia's 160+ avatar library and 120+ language support make it the most capable camera-free video studio available. It scores lower than ElevenLabs primarily because it occupies a narrower use case and has no free plan.

If you're comparing these tools trying to find one that does everything, the honest answer is they don't overlap as much as people expect. Buy ElevenLabs for voice. Buy Synthesia for avatar video. Some teams run both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ElevenLabs better than Synthesia?
It depends on your output. ElevenLabs (9.1/10) produces superior voice audio, voice cloning, dubbing, and multilingual narration. Synthesia (8.6/10) creates AI talking-head video with on-screen presenters. They solve different problems; ElevenLabs wins for audio, Synthesia wins for presenter video.
How much does ElevenLabs cost vs Synthesia?
ElevenLabs starts at $5/mo (Starter, 30,000 chars/mo) and has a free tier with 10,000 chars/mo. Synthesia starts at $22/mo (Starter, 120 videos/year, ~10/month). There is no free Synthesia plan. ElevenLabs is significantly more affordable for audio-only work.
Can I use ElevenLabs to create videos?
ElevenLabs produces audio files (MP3/WAV), not video. You can use ElevenLabs audio as the voiceover track in any video editor, or pair it with Synthesia's avatars. If you need an AI presenter visible on screen, Synthesia is the right tool, ElevenLabs alone will not create a video file.
Does Synthesia have voice cloning?
Synthesia does not offer standalone voice cloning. ElevenLabs lets you clone any voice from a short audio sample and reuse it across unlimited content. Synthesia's avatars use built-in AI voices; Enterprise plans allow a custom avatar with your likeness, but that's a visual clone, not a voice clone in the ElevenLabs sense.
Which is better for corporate training videos?
Synthesia is the stronger choice for corporate training at scale. Its 160+ AI avatars, 120+ language support, and slide-to-video workflow are purpose-built for L&D teams producing multilingual training content without a film crew. ElevenLabs adds value as an audio layer but cannot produce a video with a talking presenter on its own.
Can I try ElevenLabs for free?
Yes, ElevenLabs has a free tier with 10,000 characters per month, no credit card required. That's roughly 7–8 minutes of finished audio, enough to fully evaluate voice quality. Synthesia has no ongoing free plan, they offer a limited one-time demo video. ElevenLabs is substantially easier to try without commitment.
