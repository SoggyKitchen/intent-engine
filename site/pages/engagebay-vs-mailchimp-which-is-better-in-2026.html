Overview: EngageBay vs Mailchimp

EngageBay
All-in-one CRM platform

Founded 2017 and built for small businesses priced out of HubSpot, EngageBay bundles CRM, email marketing, marketing automation, sales pipeline, helpdesk ticketing, and live chat into one platform. Its free plan supports up to 250 contacts — no credit card required. 30% recurring affiliate commissions make it an active earner for referred customers.

Mailchimp
Email marketing platform

Founded 2001 and used by 12M+ businesses globally, Mailchimp is the dominant email marketing platform. Its free plan covers 500 contacts and 1,000 sends/month. Strong on e-commerce integrations, template design, A/B testing, and deliverability. Weak on built-in CRM, helpdesk, or live chat — you pay for those separately.

Who Should Choose EngageBay?

EngageBay is the better choice if:

  • You're a small business (under 50 people) that needs CRM + marketing + support in one platform
  • You're switching from Mailchimp because you've outgrown its email-only focus and need deal tracking
  • You want to stop paying for separate helpdesk (Zendesk, Freshdesk) and live chat (Intercom, Crisp) tools
  • Your list is under 10,000 contacts — EngageBay is significantly cheaper at this tier
  • You're an agency managing multiple client accounts and need an affordable all-in-one stack

Who Should Choose Mailchimp?

Mailchimp is the better choice if:

  • Email marketing is your primary channel and you need the most powerful template editor and analytics
  • You run an e-commerce store on Shopify or WooCommerce and rely on revenue attribution and abandoned cart flows
  • You send to large lists (50K+ contacts) where Mailchimp's deliverability reputation and dedicated IPs matter
  • You need advanced multivariate A/B testing across subject lines, send times, and content variants
  • Your team already uses separate CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot) and just needs a best-in-class email tool to integrate with it

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay better than Mailchimp?
EngageBay scores 8.6/10 vs Mailchimp's 8.4/10 in our editorial scoring. EngageBay is better for all-in-one SMB use — it includes CRM, helpdesk, and live chat that Mailchimp doesn't offer. Mailchimp is better for pure email marketing teams that need advanced templates, revenue attribution, and dedicated IP deliverability.
How much does EngageBay cost vs Mailchimp?
EngageBay: Free forever (250 contacts), paid from $14.99/mo. Mailchimp: Free (500 contacts, 1,000 sends/mo), paid from $13/mo. At 10,000 contacts, EngageBay Growth costs $49.99/mo while Mailchimp Standard costs ~$100/mo. EngageBay includes CRM and helpdesk in every plan; Mailchimp charges separately for those tools.
Does EngageBay have a free plan?
Yes. EngageBay's free plan covers up to 250 contacts, 1,000 branded emails per month, CRM access, live chat, and basic helpdesk — with no credit card required. Mailchimp's free plan covers 500 contacts and 1,000 sends/month but limits you to a single audience and removes some branding features on lower tiers.
What does Mailchimp have that EngageBay does not?
Mailchimp has deeper e-commerce integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce revenue attribution), a larger template library (100+ professionally designed), more advanced multivariate A/B testing, dedicated IP options on Premium, and 20+ years of established send reputation. For DTC brands and high-volume email marketers, Mailchimp is the stronger pure email tool.
Can I migrate from Mailchimp to EngageBay?
Yes. EngageBay supports direct CSV import from Mailchimp and has a migration team that moves contacts, tags, and segments. Most small businesses complete the switch in under a week. EngageBay's migration tool can import Mailchimp audience exports directly.
