Watch out

Hidden cost traps

⚠
Subscription-only — no permanent license
Parallels discontinued one-time permanent licenses for new purchases. You now pay annually every year or lose access. If you skip a year, you must re-subscribe to get future macOS compatibility updates.
🔄
New version required with each major macOS upgrade
Each new major macOS version typically requires a new Parallels version. With a subscription you get updates automatically — but one-time buyers of older versions have had to pay again for compatibility.
💻
Windows license sold separately
Parallels runs Windows but does not include a Windows license. You need a separate Windows 11 Pro or Home license ($139–$199 one-time) unless you already have one from your organisation.
👥
Business tier requires 5+ seats minimum
Parallels Business is a volume product — you cannot buy just 1 or 2 seats. Small teams of 1–4 Mac users must use Pro edition even if they need centralized management features.

When to pick VMware Fusion over Parallels

VMware Fusion went free for personal use in 2024 — a major shift. For individuals who need basic Windows compatibility and don't need Parallels' Coherence mode or tight macOS integration, Fusion is now a compelling $0 option. Parallels remains faster on Apple Silicon, gets macOS compatibility updates sooner, and has better Retina/display scaling. For IT-managed enterprise fleets, compare Fusion Pro ($199/yr) against Parallels Business before committing.

Common questions

Parallels Desktop pricing FAQ

How much does Parallels Desktop cost in 2026?

Standard is $99.99/year, Pro is $119.99/year, and Business is $149.99/user/year. All plans are subscription-only — there is no longer a one-time permanent license for new purchases.

Is there a free version of Parallels Desktop?

No — Parallels Desktop has no free plan. There is a 14-day free trial. For a free alternative on Mac, consider UTM (open source) or VMware Fusion (free for personal use since 2024).

What is the difference between Parallels Standard and Pro?

Pro adds more vCPU and vRAM (up to 32 vCPUs, 128 GB RAM), GPU acceleration for ML/AI workloads, Visual Studio and developer tool integrations, and priority phone support. Most developers should pick Pro.

Is Parallels Desktop worth it for businesses?

For IT teams managing Mac fleets that need Windows app compatibility, yes — the Business edition adds centralized deployment, Intune/Jamf integration, and volume licensing (5+ seat minimum). For individuals, Pro is sufficient.

Parallels vs VMware Fusion — which is better in 2026?

VMware Fusion is now free for personal use. For individuals on a budget, Fusion is compelling. Parallels is faster on Apple Silicon, updates sooner for new macOS versions, and has better Coherence mode and Retina support. For enterprise, compare Fusion Pro ($199/yr) vs Parallels Business ($149.99/user/yr).

Does Parallels include a Windows license?

No — Parallels Desktop is virtualization software only. You need a separate Windows 11 license ($139 Home, $199 Pro) unless you already have one from your organisation or Microsoft 365 subscription.
