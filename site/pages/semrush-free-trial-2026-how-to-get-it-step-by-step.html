Frequently Asked Questions

Does Semrush have a free trial?

Yes — 7-day free trial. Free plan (10 queries/day).

Is a credit card required for the Semrush free trial?

It depends on the plan. For most Semrush free tiers, no credit card is required. Paid trials may require a card but you won't be charged until the trial ends.

What happens after the Semrush free trial ends?

After the trial, your account moves to the free tier (if available) or pauses until you add payment. You won't lose your data. Semrush will email you before the trial expires.

Can I extend the Semrush free trial?

Yes — contact Semrush support before your trial expires and ask for an extension. Most companies grant 7-14 extra days, especially if you explain you need more time to evaluate with your team.

How do I cancel Semrush during the trial?

Cancel via your account settings before the trial ends to avoid charges. Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Semrush typically confirms cancellation by email.

Is the Semrush free tier good enough?

Free plan (10 queries/day). For solo users or very small teams, the free tier may be sufficient. For professional use, paid plans unlock features like advanced reporting, team collaboration, and API access.

What the Semrush Free Trial Includes

The Semrush free trial gives full access to all features — including features on the paid plan — for 7-day free trial. This is your chance to test the tool with real data, not a demo.

How to Get the Most From Your Trial

Week 1 checklist: Set up your real workflow (not a test project), invite your whole team, connect your integrations, run a real project end-to-end. By Day 7 you should know if it fits.

After the Trial: Semrush Pricing

Semrush paid plans start at $117/mo. Annual billing saves ~20%. If you're on the fence, ask sales for a 30-day extension — most will say yes.

Alternatives If the Trial Doesn't Convert You
