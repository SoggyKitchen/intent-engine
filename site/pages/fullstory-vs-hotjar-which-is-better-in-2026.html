Feature Deep Dive

FullStory's advanced session replay feature allows you to record and replay user sessions, providing a detailed understanding of how users interact with your website or application. Hotjar's heatmap feature, on the other hand, provides a visual representation of how users interact with your website, highlighting areas of high engagement and drop-off. Additionally, FullStory's user feedback feature allows you to collect feedback from users, while Hotjar's form analytics feature provides insights into how users interact with forms on your website.

Pricing Breakdown

FullStory's pricing starts at $79/user/month (billed annually) for the Standard plan, while Hotjar's pricing starts at $39/month (billed annually) for the Basic plan. FullStory's Pro plan costs $149/user/month (billed annually), while Hotjar's Standard plan costs $69/month (billed annually). FullStory's Enterprise plan costs $249/user/month (billed annually), while Hotjar's Business plan costs $129/month (billed annually).

Who Should Use FullStory

FullStory is best suited for businesses that need advanced session replay and user feedback features, such as e-commerce companies, software as a service (SaaS) providers, and financial institutions. FullStory is also a good choice for businesses with large teams and complex analytics needs.

Who Should Use Hotjar

Hotjar is best suited for businesses that need basic heatmaps and form analytics, such as small e-commerce companies, blogs, and websites with simple analytics needs. Hotjar is also a good choice for businesses with limited budgets and simple analytics needs.

Migration & Setup

FullStory offers a seamless onboarding process, allowing you to easily switch from Hotjar. FullStory also provides a free trial, allowing you to test the tool before committing to a paid plan. Hotjar's onboarding process is also relatively easy, but it may take some time to set up and configure the tool.
