Performance: Why SiteGround Leads

SiteGround's Google Cloud infrastructure gives it a meaningful edge over traditional shared hosting. Key advantages include: distributed data centres worldwide, built-in full-page caching, Cloudflare CDN reducing latency globally, and SSD NVMe storage on all plans. Independent speed benchmarks consistently rank SiteGround among the top 3 shared hosting providers.

HostPapa's performance is adequate for low-to-moderate traffic sites but does not match SiteGround's infrastructure depth.

Final Verdict: HostPapa vs SiteGround

🏆 Our pick: SiteGround for performance; HostPapa for first-timers

SiteGround is the superior technical host — better infrastructure, better performance, better backups, and better support. If you're building a site you intend to grow, SiteGround's premium is well spent. HostPapa wins for new users who need personal hand-holding during setup, want green credentials, and need email hosting included.

Choose HostPapa if you:

  • Are a first-time website owner who wants personal onboarding support
  • Want green/eco-friendly hosting credentials
  • Need email hosting included without extra cost
  • Are on a strict budget and need predictable renewal pricing
  • Want a free domain included with your plan

Choose SiteGround if you:

  • Are building a business site that needs real performance
  • Want Google Cloud infrastructure and built-in caching
  • Need a staging environment for testing before going live
  • Want daily backups with meaningful retention
  • Prioritise technical support quality above all else

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HostPapa better than SiteGround?
SiteGround is the better technical host by most measures — faster infrastructure, better caching, staging, and support. HostPapa is better for new users who need personal onboarding and want eco-friendly hosting at a predictable price.
Does SiteGround have better support than HostPapa?
SiteGround's technical support is consistently rated best-in-class for shared hosting. HostPapa's unique differentiator is one-on-one onboarding sessions — personal training for new users — which SiteGround doesn't offer. For ongoing technical support, SiteGround wins; for initial setup guidance, HostPapa wins.
Is SiteGround worth the price over HostPapa?
For a growing business site, yes — SiteGround's Google Cloud infrastructure, built-in caching, staging, and daily backups are meaningful advantages that become more valuable as your site grows. For a personal site or hobby project, HostPapa's lower price and included email may make more sense.
Does HostPapa or SiteGround include a free domain?
HostPapa includes a free domain name with annual plans. SiteGround does not include a free domain — you purchase it separately. This gives HostPapa a cost advantage for first-year setup.
