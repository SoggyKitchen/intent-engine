Free Plan Comparison

Both free plans are genuinely usable, but they solve different problems. Zoho CRM Free gives 3 users full CRM access — contacts, leads, accounts, deals, and standard reports — with no time limit. It is the right choice for a tiny sales team that only needs to manage a pipeline.

EngageBay Free supports unlimited users but limits storage to 500 contacts. In exchange, it includes live chat, email marketing (1,000 branded emails/month), and helpdesk ticketing on the free plan — features Zoho CRM Free doesn't include at all. For a solo founder who wants CRM + email marketing in one tool, EngageBay Free wins.

CRM Depth: Where Zoho Leads

Zoho CRM is purpose-built around sales pipeline management. Its advantages over EngageBay in pure CRM functionality include: territory management for regional sales teams; Zia, an AI assistant that surfaces deal insights and flags at-risk opportunities; advanced forecast modules with accuracy tracking; and canvas-style custom views for pipeline dashboards. These features matter to scaling B2B sales teams but are overkill for most small businesses.

EngageBay's CRM is fully capable for small teams — contact management, deal pipelines, activity tracking, and email sequences are all solid. But if your primary use case is managing a complex, multi-rep sales pipeline, Zoho CRM's depth is a meaningful advantage.

All-in-One Bundling: Where EngageBay Leads

This is EngageBay's clearest win. For a team that needs CRM + email marketing + live chat + helpdesk, EngageBay bundles everything from $11.99/user/mo. Zoho offers each as a separate product: Zoho CRM, Zoho Campaigns, Zoho SalesIQ (live chat), and Zoho Desk. Each has its own subscription and its own login — meaning you're managing four separate tools, four billing cycles, and four onboarding processes.

Zoho's products do integrate well together (especially in the Enterprise tier), but the overhead of managing multiple Zoho subscriptions is real. EngageBay's single-subscription approach reduces that friction significantly.

Ease of Use

EngageBay scores 8.5/10 on ease of use vs Zoho CRM's 7.8/10. Zoho CRM's interface has improved significantly but remains complex — particularly for users setting up automation workflows for the first time. EngageBay's unified interface (no switching between apps) and simpler automation builder give it a clear edge for teams without a dedicated CRM admin.

Integration Ecosystem

Zoho CRM connects natively with the full Zoho suite — Zoho Books, Zoho Analytics, Zoho Projects, Zoho Sign, and more — making it highly attractive for teams already invested in Zoho. It also integrates with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, and key telephony tools. EngageBay integrates with Zapier (opening thousands of indirect connections), Stripe, Gmail, Twilio, and the main social platforms. For most small business needs, both are sufficient.

  • Teams on a tight budget where paying for Zoho CRM + Zoho Campaigns + Zoho Desk separately would exceed $50/user/mo total

  • Field sales teams that need a strong mobile app — Zoho CRM's mobile experience is superior to EngageBay's

Verdict: EngageBay vs Zoho CRM 2026

Our recommendation

For most small businesses: Start with EngageBay Free if you need CRM + email marketing + live chat without separate subscriptions. Upgrade to Basic ($11.99/user/mo) once you hit the 500-contact limit. The all-in-one value is hard to match at this price point.

For pure sales teams: Zoho CRM Standard ($14/user/mo) is the better choice if your primary need is pipeline management and you're comfortable adding Zoho Campaigns separately for email. The CRM features are materially deeper than EngageBay at the same price tier.

For solo founders / teams of 3: Zoho CRM Free is genuinely useful for basic pipeline tracking. EngageBay Free is better if you also want email marketing and live chat included at $0.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay better than Zoho CRM?
It depends on what you need. EngageBay (8.4/10) wins if you want CRM, email marketing, live chat, and helpdesk bundled in one subscription — it scores 9.1/10 on value. Zoho CRM (8.5/10) wins on pure CRM depth, sales automation, and has a better free plan for up to 3 users. For an all-in-one tool for small teams, EngageBay is the stronger pick. For pure CRM with deep sales features, Zoho CRM edges ahead.
How much does EngageBay cost vs Zoho CRM?
EngageBay Basic is $11.99/user/mo (annual); Zoho CRM Standard is $14/user/mo. At mid-range, EngageBay Growth is $45.99/user/mo vs Zoho CRM Professional at $23/user/mo. However, to get email marketing and helpdesk with Zoho you need to add Zoho Campaigns and Zoho Desk — separate subscriptions that can make the total cost higher than EngageBay for a team needing the full stack.
Does Zoho CRM have a free plan?
Yes. Zoho CRM Free supports up to 3 users with contacts, leads, accounts, deals, and standard reports — no time limit. It does not include email marketing or live chat. EngageBay Free has a 500-contact limit but includes live chat and basic email marketing (1,000 emails/month), making it a better fit for teams that need more than just a CRM.
Can I migrate from Zoho CRM to EngageBay?
Yes. Export your Zoho CRM contacts, leads, accounts, and deals as CSV and import via EngageBay's import wizard. Custom fields need to be recreated in EngageBay before importing. Automation workflows are not portable — they need to be rebuilt manually. Expect 1–2 days of migration work for a team of 5–10.
What does Zoho CRM have that EngageBay does not?
Zoho CRM's main advantages: Zia AI assistant for lead scoring and anomaly detection; territory management for regional sales teams; advanced sales forecasting with accuracy tracking; stronger mobile app for field sales; native integration with the full Zoho suite (Books, Analytics, Desk, Projects); more granular user permission controls for larger teams.
