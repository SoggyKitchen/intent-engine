Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lever better than Deel?

Yes, Lever offers a more comprehensive recruitment platform with robust features and excellent customer support. However, Deel excels in international hiring and onboarding.

Which is cheaper, Lever or Deel?

Deel is generally cheaper, especially for small teams or startups.

Can I switch from Deel to Lever?

Yes, but it may require some time and effort to migrate data and set up the new platform.

Does Lever or Deel have a free plan?

Both tools offer a free trial, but Lever's free plan is not available for purchase.

Which has better customer support, Lever or Deel?

Lever's customer support is generally considered better, with 24/7 support available.

Is Lever or Deel better for small teams?

Deel is a better fit for small teams or startups due to its affordable pricing and streamlined international hiring features.

Does Lever integrate with Deel?

No, Lever and Deel do not integrate directly. However, you can use Zapier or other integration tools to connect the two platforms.

Which tool has more features, Lever or Deel?

Lever has a more comprehensive set of features, including applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and candidate scoring. Deel excels in international hiring and onboarding, but its feature set is more limited for domestic hiring.

Feature Deep Dive

Lever's applicant tracking system allows you to post jobs, manage applications, and score candidates. Deel's automated employment contracts simplify international hiring and onboarding. Lever's interview scheduling feature helps you coordinate interviews with candidates, while Deel's benefits administration feature streamlines benefits for international employees.

Pricing Breakdown

Lever's pricing starts at $99/month for the 'Essential' plan, $199/month for the 'Growth' plan, and $299/month for the 'Pro' plan. Deel's pricing starts at $49/month for the 'Essential' plan, $99/month for the 'Growth' plan, and $199/month for the 'Pro' plan.

Who Should Use Lever

Lever is ideal for mid-to-large-sized companies seeking a comprehensive recruitment platform with robust features and excellent customer support. This includes companies with 50+ employees, a budget of $100,000+ per year, and a need for advanced recruitment features.

Who Should Use Deel

Deel is perfect for small teams or startups looking for a more affordable, streamlined solution for international hiring. This includes companies with 10-50 employees, a budget of $10,000-$50,000 per year, and a need for international hiring and onboarding features.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Deel to Lever may require some time and effort to migrate data and set up the new platform. Lever offers a free trial and onboarding support to help you get started. Deel also offers onboarding support, but its migration process may be more complex due to its automated employment contracts and benefits administration features.
