Features: Where Each Tool Wins

Mailchimp Wins On

  • Analytics: Click maps, revenue attribution, benchmark reports — best-in-class at this price point
  • Ecommerce: Native Shopify/WooCommerce sync, abandoned cart, product recommendations
  • Free plan: 500 contacts + 1,000 emails/month permanently — unique in this category
  • A/B testing: Subject lines, send times, content — available on Essentials ($13/mo)
  • Integrations: 300+ native integrations vs Constant Contact's 100+

Constant Contact Wins On

  • Event management: Built-in event registration, ticketing, and attendee management — Mailchimp has nothing comparable
  • Phone support: Available on all paid plans — Mailchimp gates this behind $350/mo Premium
  • SMS marketing: Included in Standard ($35/mo) — adds meaningful channel without extra tools
  • Ease of use: Slightly simpler interface — better for non-technical users who just need to send newsletters
  • Template library: 200+ professional email templates vs Mailchimp's ~100

Neither Wins On: Automation

If automation depth is your deciding factor, skip both. Mailchimp's automation (Standard, $20/mo) is better than Constant Contact's basic drips — but both are outclassed by ActiveCampaign ($15/mo) which includes visual automations, CRM, and lead scoring at a lower starting price.

Our Verdict: Who Should Pick Which

Choose Mailchimp if: You need a free plan, you run ecommerce, you care about analytics, or you want 300+ integrations. Mailchimp is the default-correct choice for most digital-first small businesses.

Choose Constant Contact if: You host in-person events regularly, you need phone support on an affordable plan, or you want email + SMS in one tool without paying extra.

Choose ActiveCampaign instead if: Automation depth, CRM, or lead scoring matters. ActiveCampaign at $15/mo beats both tools on automation at a price that undercuts Constant Contact's Standard plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Constant Contact better than Mailchimp?
For most small businesses, Mailchimp edges ahead — it scores 8.3/10 vs Constant Contact's 7.8/10. Mailchimp wins on free plan, analytics, and ecommerce. Constant Contact wins on event management, phone support, and SMS.
How much does Constant Contact cost vs Mailchimp?
Constant Contact: Lite from $12/mo (500 contacts), Standard from $35/mo. No free plan — 60-day trial only. Mailchimp: Free (500 contacts, 1K emails/month), Essentials from $13/mo. Mailchimp is cheaper at every comparable tier and has a permanent free plan.
Does Constant Contact have a free plan?
No. Constant Contact offers a 60-day free trial but no ongoing free plan. Mailchimp's free plan is permanent for up to 500 contacts and 1,000 emails per month.
Which is better for automation?
Neither is the best choice for automation. Mailchimp's Standard plan ($20/mo) offers customer journeys and behavioral triggers. Constant Contact only has basic drip sequences. For serious automation, ActiveCampaign ($15/mo) beats both with visual builders, CRM, and lead scoring.
What is the best alternative to Constant Contact and Mailchimp?
For automation: ActiveCampaign ($15/mo) — far deeper automation at a competitive price. For email + webinars + landing pages: GetResponse ($15/mo). For ecommerce-first: Klaviyo — tighter Shopify integration than both. For simplicity: Brevo (free plan for 300 emails/day).
Can you switch from Constant Contact to Mailchimp easily?
Yes — Mailchimp accepts CSV imports of contacts from Constant Contact. You'll need to rebuild any automations, templates, and audience segments. The switch typically takes 1–2 hours of setup for a small list. Export your Constant Contact list as a CSV, clean it, then import to Mailchimp's free account to test before committing.
