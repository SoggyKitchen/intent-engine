Frequently Asked Questions

How much does DocuSign cost per month?

DocuSign starts at $15 per user per month for the Starter plan (billed annually), which includes 5 sent documents per month. The most popular plan, Business Pro, is $40 per user per month with unlimited documents, in-person signing, and payment integrations. Enterprise pricing is custom and typically requires direct negotiation.

Does DocuSign have a free plan?

Yes, DocuSign offers a free plan for individuals that allows up to 5 documents per month with basic e-signature functionality. No credit card is required. This plan includes mobile access and simple tracking but lacks team controls, branding, and advanced features.

Is DocuSign worth the price?

For most B2B teams, yes — especially if you operate in regulated industries or rely on integration with platforms like Salesforce or NetSuite. The ROI comes from faster deal cycles, reduced printing/shipping costs, and fewer errors. However, smaller teams may find better value in alternatives like PandaDoc, which offer similar features at a lower price point.
