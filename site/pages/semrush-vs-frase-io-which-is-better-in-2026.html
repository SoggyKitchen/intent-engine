Frequently Asked Questions

Is Semrush better than Frase.io?

Semrush is better for comprehensive SEO, competitive analysis, and enterprise use, while Frase.io excels in AI-driven content creation and optimization. The 'better' tool depends on your team's primary goals—broad SEO intelligence or fast, high-quality content output.

Which is cheaper, Semrush or Frase.io?

Frase.io is significantly cheaper, with its top-tier Business plan at $114.99/mo versus Semrush’s $599.95/mo Business plan. Even Semrush’s entry-level Pro plan ($139.95) is more expensive than Frase.io’s highest tier.

Can I switch from Frase.io to Semrush?

Yes, switching from Frase.io to Semrush is feasible. While no direct migration tool exists, you can export content briefs and keyword data manually. Semrush offers robust onboarding and import tools for SEO data, making the transition manageable within 1–2 weeks.

Does Semrush or Frase.io have a free plan?

Neither tool offers a permanent free plan. Semrush provides a limited free account with basic features, while Frase.io only offers a 7-day free trial with no ongoing free tier.

Which has better customer support, Semrush or Frase.io?

Semrush offers superior support with 24/7 live chat, email, and a comprehensive knowledge base. Frase.io provides email and chat support during business hours, with typical response times of 12–24 hours, making Semrush better for urgent needs.

Is Semrush or Frase.io better for small teams?

Frase.io is better for small content teams focused on writing efficiency and SEO optimization on a budget. Semrush suits small teams that need full SEO capabilities but requires more training and investment.

Does Semrush integrate with Frase.io?

There is no native integration between Semrush and Frase.io. However, users often export keyword and SERP data from Semrush to inform content briefs in Frase.io, creating a manual but effective workflow.

Which tool has more features, Semrush or Frase.io?

Semrush has significantly more features—over 50 core tools spanning SEO, PPC, social media, and content marketing. Frase.io focuses on 5–6 core AI content features, offering depth in content optimization but less breadth overall.

Feature Deep Dive

Semrush offers tools like Position Tracking, Site Audit, Backlink Analytics, and Market Explorer, giving users deep visibility into technical SEO and competitive landscapes. Frase.io counters with AI Content Generator, Content Briefs, and Answer Engine that analyze top SERP results to suggest headings and answers. While Semrush’s Keyword Gap tool helps identify untapped opportunities across competitors, Frase.io’s AI Optimizer scores content in real time against SERP leaders. For content teams, Frase.io’s editor is more intuitive, but Semrush’s SEO Writing Assistant provides broader context with keyword difficulty and traffic potential.

Pricing Breakdown

Semrush’s pricing starts at $139.95/month (Pro) for 1 user, 10 projects, and 10,000 results per report. The Guru plan ($289.95) adds templates and branding, while Business ($599.95) includes API access and white-label reporting. Frase.io starts at $14.99/month (Basic) for 5 reports and 1 user, Team at $44.99 for 2 users and 25 reports, and Business at $114.99 for 5 users, 100 reports, and custom templates. Semrush costs more but delivers far more data and functionality.

Who Should Use Semrush

Semrush is ideal for marketing agencies, enterprise SEO teams, and companies running complex, multi-channel campaigns. It suits organizations with budgets over $150/month and teams of 3+ needing collaboration, workflow management, and deep analytics. If you manage PPC, social, and SEO together—or need competitive intelligence across markets—Semrush is the clear choice.

Who Should Use Frase.io

Frase.io is best for content marketers, SEO writers, and small teams focused on producing high-ranking content quickly. It’s perfect for bloggers, SaaS content teams, and agencies prioritizing content efficiency over broad SEO analytics. With a lower entry cost and AI-first interface, it’s accessible for solopreneurs and teams under tight deadlines.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Frase.io to Semrush requires manual data export of content briefs and keywords, as no direct integration exists. Onboarding in Semrush typically takes 1–2 weeks due to its complexity, though guided tours and templates help. Frase.io can be set up in under a day. Both tools allow CSV exports, enabling partial data portability for keyword and content performance history.
