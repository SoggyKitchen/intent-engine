Hidden Costs & Gotchas

What to budget for beyond the base plan price.

📈
Monthly billing premium
Paying month-to-month costs ~20% more than annual. On a 5-person team on Growth, that's ~$45/month extra — $540/year. Commit to annual only if you're confident in the platform.
📧
Email volume caps (Free & Basic)
Free plan caps at 1,000 emails/month; Basic at 3,000. If you're doing regular campaigns to even a 500-person list weekly, you'll hit Basic limits fast and need Growth ($45/user/mo).
🔗
Integration gaps
EngageBay's native integrations are fewer than HubSpot's. You may need Zapier ($20+/mo) to connect tools like Slack, Shopify, or Calendly — factor this into total cost of ownership.
🆙
Contact-limit upgrades
Basic caps at 1,000 contacts. Growing from 1,000 to 25,000 jumps you from Basic to Growth — a $32+/user/month increase. Plan your list-building timeline before committing to Basic.
☎️
Phone support is Pro-only
If your team relies on phone support, you need Pro (~$100/user/mo). Free, Basic, and Growth users get email and chat support only.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does EngageBay cost?

EngageBay All-in-One starts at $0 (Free, 250 contacts). Paid plans run approximately $13/user/month (Basic), $45/user/month (Growth), and $100/user/month (Pro) on annual billing. Monthly billing adds ~20%. All prices are per user — verify current rates at engagebay.com as they can change.

Does EngageBay have a free plan?

Yes. The free plan supports up to 250 contacts with basic CRM, 1 landing page, and limited email sends. It is permanent — not a trial — but the 250-contact ceiling is very restrictive for any team doing active outreach.

Is EngageBay cheaper than HubSpot?

Yes — typically 70–85% cheaper for equivalent functionality. A 5-person team on EngageBay Growth pays roughly $2,700/year vs. $4,800+/year for HubSpot Starter Suite. The trade-off is fewer integrations and less enterprise polish.

What does EngageBay All-in-One include?

EngageBay bundles three products: Marketing (email campaigns, automation, landing pages, forms), CRM (contacts, deals, pipelines, tasks), and Service Bay (help desk, ticketing, live chat). Paid plans include all three in one subscription.

Can I upgrade or downgrade EngageBay plans?

Yes. EngageBay allows plan changes at any time. Upgrades are prorated. Downgrades typically apply at the next billing cycle. Annual plans may have refund restrictions — check with EngageBay support before committing.
