Feature Deep Dive

ActiveCampaign offers a range of features, including email marketing, automation, and analytics, making it a more comprehensive marketing automation platform. Marketo, on the other hand, focuses on advanced analytics and customization options, making it better suited for large enterprises with complex marketing setups. ActiveCampaign's automation features, such as its visual workflow builder and conditional logic, make it easier to create and manage targeted campaigns. Marketo's advanced analytics, including its predictive analytics and attribution modeling, provide deeper insights into customer behavior and campaign performance.

Pricing Breakdown

ActiveCampaign's pricing plans start at $9/month (billed annually) for up to 500 contacts, while Marketo's plans start at $1,295/month (billed annually) for up to 10,000 contacts. ActiveCampaign's Pro plan costs $49/month (billed annually) for up to 10,000 contacts, while Marketo's Pro plan costs $2,495/month (billed annually) for up to 50,000 contacts. ActiveCampaign's Enterprise plan costs $129/month (billed annually) for up to 50,000 contacts, while Marketo's Enterprise plan costs $4,995/month (billed annually) for up to 100,000 contacts.

Who Should Use ActiveCampaign

ActiveCampaign is ideal for mid-sized businesses looking for a user-friendly, affordable marketing automation platform with robust features. It's perfect for teams with 10-50 employees, a budget of $1,000-$5,000 per month, and a focus on email marketing, automation, and analytics.

Who Should Use Marketo

Marketo is ideal for large enterprises with complex marketing setups and require advanced analytics and customization options. It's perfect for teams with 50+ employees, a budget of $5,000-$20,000 per month, and a focus on advanced analytics, attribution modeling, and predictive analytics.

Switching from Marketo to ActiveCampaign is relatively easy, and ActiveCampaign offers a free migration service to help with the transition. The migration process typically takes 1-3 days, depending on the complexity of the setup. ActiveCampaign also offers a range of resources, including documentation and support, to help with the setup and onboarding process.
