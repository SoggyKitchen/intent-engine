Mixpanel Review

Mixpanel is a product analytics platform designed for B2B SaaS companies and digital product teams to track user behavior, analyze feature adoption, and optimize customer journeys. It’s ideal for product managers, growth teams, and data analysts who need deep insights into how users interact with web and mobile apps.

Pros

  • Powerful behavioral analytics with funnel, retention, and segmentation tools
  • Intuitive visual interface for non-technical users
  • Flexible pricing based on monthly tracked users (MTUs), making it scalable

Cons

  • Pricing can spike quickly with user growth or unexpected data spikes
  • Limited support and advanced features in lower tiers

Pricing: Free plan available; Pro plan starts at $25/month (billed annually) for up to 10,000 MTUs. Higher tiers scale with usage: $50/month (50K MTUs), $150/month (250K MTUs), and custom pricing beyond 1M MTUs. Enterprise plans include dedicated support, SSO, and audit logs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Mixpanel cost per month?

Mixpanel starts at $25/month (billed annually) for up to 10,000 monthly tracked users (MTUs). Prices scale with usage: $50 for 50K MTUs, $150 for 250K MTUs. Beyond that, pricing is custom. Most mid-sized B2B teams pay between $200–$800/month depending on user volume and feature needs.

Does Mixpanel have a free plan?

Yes. Mixpanel offers a free plan that includes up to 50,000 monthly tracked users (MTUs), core analytics features like funnels and segmentation, and up to 3 projects. No credit card is required to sign up.

Is Mixpanel worth the price?

For product-led growth teams that need fast, actionable insights without heavy engineering support, Mixpanel delivers strong ROI. However, costs can escalate if user counts grow unexpectedly. Teams with predictable usage or those needing advanced experimentation may find better value in alternatives like Amplitude.
