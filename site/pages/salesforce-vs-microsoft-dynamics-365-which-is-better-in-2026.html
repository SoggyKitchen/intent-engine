Microsoft Dynamics 365

Salesforce vs Dynamics 365: The Core Choice

This is fundamentally a question of ecosystem: Are you a Microsoft shop?

If your business runs on Microsoft 365, Azure, and Teams, Dynamics 365 offers genuinely tight integration that Salesforce connectors can't match. Your reps work from Outlook, meeting summaries sync via Copilot in Teams, and Power BI dashboards embed natively.

If you're not locked into the Microsoft ecosystem — or if you need the richest standalone CRM on the market — Salesforce wins. App Exchange alone (6,000+ apps) gives Salesforce unmatched extensibility. Einstein AI is mature. Trailhead training is the industry standard.

Our Final Verdict (July 2026)

Salesforce is our recommendation for most sales-focused organizations. Here is the breakdown:

  • Choose Salesforce if: you want the most feature-rich standalone CRM on the market, need access to the App Exchange ecosystem, use a heterogeneous tech stack (not Microsoft-centric), or are building a world-class sales operation with complex automation needs.
  • Choose Dynamics 365 if: your company is a deep Microsoft 365 user (Teams, Outlook, Azure), you need CRM and ERP in one vendor relationship, you're in manufacturing/finance/government where Microsoft dominates, or your existing Microsoft licensing creates bundle discounts.
  • Budget-conscious mid-market: Model both. Dynamics 365 bundled with Microsoft 365 E3/E5 can be dramatically cheaper than standalone Salesforce — get quotes from both.
  • SMBs: Salesforce Starter ($25/user) is more affordable and accessible than Dynamics' entry point ($65/user).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Salesforce better than Microsoft Dynamics 365?
Salesforce scores 9.0/10 vs Dynamics 365's 8.4/10. Salesforce wins on CRM depth and App Exchange ecosystem. Dynamics 365 wins for Microsoft 365 users — native Teams, Outlook, and Power BI integration is genuinely superior. The "better" choice depends entirely on your existing tech stack.
How much does Salesforce cost vs Microsoft Dynamics 365?
Salesforce Sales Cloud: Starter $25/user/mo, Professional $80/user/mo, Enterprise $165/user/mo. Dynamics 365 Sales: Professional $65/user/mo, Enterprise $95/user/mo, Premium $135/user/mo. Salesforce is cheaper at entry; Dynamics can be cheaper at scale for Microsoft 365 customers with bundle discounts.
Can I try Salesforce and Dynamics 365 for free?
Yes — both offer 30-day free trials with no credit card required. Salesforce's trial is at salesforce.com/form/signup; Dynamics 365 trial is at dynamics.microsoft.com. We recommend running both simultaneously with your actual use cases for 2 weeks each.
Which is better for Microsoft Office users?
Microsoft Dynamics 365 is built for Microsoft 365 users. Native Teams integration means meeting summaries and action items sync directly to CRM. The Outlook add-in is more powerful and stable than Salesforce's connector. If your team lives in Teams and Outlook, Dynamics 365 is the natural fit.
Is Salesforce or Dynamics 365 easier to implement?
Salesforce is generally easier for mid-market — the Flow/admin tools are more accessible, and there are more certified admins in the market. Dynamics 365 typically requires a Microsoft partner for implementation and Power Platform expertise. Both need significant configuration time for enterprise deployments.
Which CRM has better AI features in 2026?
Both are strong. Salesforce Einstein AI covers lead scoring, opportunity insights, and next-step recommendations on Enterprise+ plans. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Copilot — meeting summaries in Teams, email drafting in Outlook, predictive scoring — is included across all plans. If you already have Microsoft Copilot M365, Dynamics provides the most seamless AI-across-everything experience.
