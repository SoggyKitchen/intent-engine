Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gusto better than Lattice?

Yes, Gusto offers more features and better customer support, but Lattice is a more affordable option for smaller teams.

Which is cheaper, Gusto or Lattice?

Lattice is generally cheaper, with prices starting at $5 per employee, per month, compared to Gusto's $6 per employee, per month.

Can I switch from Lattice to Gusto?

Yes, it's possible to switch from Lattice to Gusto, but you may need to export your data and re-import it into Gusto.

Does Gusto or Lattice have a free plan?

Neither Gusto nor Lattice offers a free plan, but both offer free trials.

Which has better customer support, Gusto or Lattice?

Gusto has better customer support, with 24/7 support available, compared to Lattice's 24/5 support.

Is Gusto or Lattice better for small teams?

Lattice is a better option for small teams, with more affordable pricing and a user-friendly interface.

Does Gusto integrate with Lattice?

No, Gusto and Lattice do not integrate directly, but you can use Zapier or other integration tools to connect the two platforms.

Which tool has more features, Gusto or Lattice?

Gusto has more features, including payroll, benefits, and HR management tools, compared to Lattice's focus on employee engagement and performance management.

Feature Deep Dive

Gusto offers a range of features, including payroll, benefits, and HR management tools, as well as performance management and employee engagement tools. Lattice, on the other hand, focuses on employee engagement and performance management, with features like goal-setting and feedback tools. While both platforms offer robust features, Gusto's comprehensive approach makes it a better choice for mid-sized to large businesses.

Pricing Breakdown

Gusto offers a range of plans, including a Core plan ($6 per employee, per month) and an Advanced plan ($12 per employee, per month). Lattice offers a range of plans, including a Core plan ($5 per employee, per month) and an Advanced plan ($10 per employee, per month). While both platforms offer affordable pricing, Gusto's prices are slightly higher, especially for larger teams.

Who Should Use Gusto

Gusto is ideal for mid-sized to large businesses that need a comprehensive HR platform with robust features and excellent customer support. This includes businesses with 50 or more employees, or those that require advanced HR features like payroll and benefits management.

Who Should Use Lattice

Lattice is ideal for small teams or startups that want a user-friendly solution with a focus on employee engagement and performance management. This includes businesses with fewer than 50 employees, or those that prioritize employee experience and engagement over advanced HR features.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Lattice to Gusto can be a bit complex, as you'll need to export your data and re-import it into Gusto. However, both platforms offer robust onboarding processes and customer support to help you get started. Typical onboarding time is around 2-4 weeks, depending on the size of your team and the complexity of your HR needs.
