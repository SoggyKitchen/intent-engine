NordLayer Review

NordLayer is a business-grade Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) and SASE solution designed for remote teams, hybrid workplaces, and distributed enterprises. It enables secure, encrypted access to internal resources without traditional VPNs, offering identity-based access control, device verification, and network segmentation. Ideal for IT managers, security officers, and growing companies needing scalable, compliant network security.

Pros

  • Enterprise-grade security with Zero Trust principles
  • Easy deployment and centralized admin dashboard
  • Transparent, competitive pricing with volume discounts

Cons

  • Higher cost for full feature access in larger deployments
  • Limited customization in starter plans

Pricing: Starter: $6/user/month (billed annually), Pro: $9/user/month, Enterprise: Custom pricing based on scale and requirements. Annual billing required for best rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does NordLayer cost per month?

NordLayer starts at $6 per user per month for the Starter plan (billed annually, minimum 5 users). The Pro plan is $9 per user per month and includes advanced features like SSO, device posture checks, and network segmentation.

Does NordLayer have a free plan?

NordLayer offers a free tier for up to 3 users, ideal for small teams or testing. It includes core ZTNA functionality but lacks advanced admin controls and compliance reporting.

Is NordLayer worth the price?

Yes, for most B2B buyers. The Pro plan delivers strong ROI through reduced breach risk, simplified compliance (GDPR, HIPAA), and seamless remote access. Enterprises often negotiate 10–20% discounts on annual contracts, improving value further.
