Elementor vs Wix: The Core Difference

These tools solve the same problem (building a website) in fundamentally different ways. Elementor is a plugin that runs on WordPress — you need separate hosting, a domain, and WordPress installed first. Wix is a closed, hosted platform — everything is bundled in one monthly fee.

This difference shapes every other comparison: flexibility, SEO, cost, and lock-in.

When to choose Elementor

  • You want full SEO control via plugins like Yoast or RankMath
  • You plan to run a WooCommerce store and need deep eCommerce customisation
  • You want to own your data and switch hosts freely at any time
  • You need advanced integrations (CRM, email, custom code, ACF fields)
  • You're building on WordPress and want the most powerful page builder

When to choose Wix

  • You are a true beginner who wants a website live in an afternoon
  • You don't want to manage hosting, updates, or WordPress security
  • You run a small business website (not a large eCommerce operation)
  • You want AI-powered design generation (Wix ADI builds a starter site automatically)
  • Your budget is tight and you want everything in one predictable monthly fee

Our Final Verdict

Bottom line

Elementor is the better long-term choice for anyone who cares about SEO, growth, and flexibility. The plugin ecosystem (60,000+ WordPress plugins), WooCommerce integration, and zero platform lock-in make it the right tool for serious website owners — even if the setup is more involved.

Wix is genuinely the right choice for someone who wants a website up in a day, never wants to think about hosting or security updates, and runs a simple small-business site or portfolio. The ADI (AI Design Intelligence) can build a starter site in minutes.

The migration warning: Wix does not export to WordPress. If you start on Wix and later want to move to Elementor, you're rebuilding from scratch. Factor this in before choosing Wix.

Choose Elementor if:

  • You want the best SEO capability (Yoast + RankMath on WordPress)
  • You plan to run WooCommerce now or in the future
  • You care about owning your data and avoiding platform lock-in
  • You want to extend functionality with 60,000+ WordPress plugins
  • You're comfortable managing WordPress hosting (or willing to learn)

Choose Wix if:

  • You've never built a website and want the simplest possible experience
  • You're building a portfolio, restaurant site, or simple service business
  • You want everything managed (hosting, SSL, CDN) in one monthly fee
  • You want AI to generate a starter design instantly (Wix ADI)
  • You don't anticipate needing to migrate to another platform

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Elementor better than Wix in 2026?
Elementor scores 8.4/10 vs Wix's 8.1/10. Elementor is better for SEO control, WooCommerce, and advanced flexibility — but requires WordPress + hosting. Wix is better for absolute beginners who want an all-in-one hosted builder with no technical setup.
Is Wix or Elementor cheaper?
Wix starts at $17/mo all-inclusive. Elementor Pro is $59/yr ($4.92/mo) but you also need WordPress hosting ($3–10/mo), bringing the real total to ~$8–15/mo. They're similarly priced, but Wix includes hosting; Elementor does not.
Can I move from Wix to Elementor?
Not automatically — Wix does not export to WordPress. You would need to manually rebuild your site on WordPress with Elementor. This is a significant project. Consider this lock-in risk before choosing Wix if long-term flexibility matters.
Which is better for SEO — Wix or Elementor?
Elementor on WordPress has better SEO control. You can use Yoast SEO, RankMath, or other dedicated plugins to manage meta tags, schema markup, sitemaps, and technical SEO fully. Wix has improved its SEO capabilities significantly and is adequate for most small business sites, but lacks the depth of WordPress SEO plugins.
Is Elementor free?
Yes — Elementor has a free version on WordPress.org with 40+ widgets and the core drag-and-drop builder. You need WordPress + hosting, but the Elementor plugin itself is free. Wix also has a free plan, but it shows Wix branding, displays ads, and does not allow a custom domain.
