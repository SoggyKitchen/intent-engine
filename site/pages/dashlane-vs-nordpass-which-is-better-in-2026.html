Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dashlane better than NordPass?

Dashlane offers more advanced features like dark web monitoring, detailed password health reports, and stronger team administration tools, making it better for businesses with higher security needs. NordPass is simpler and cheaper but lacks some enterprise capabilities.

Which is cheaper, Dashlane or NordPass?

NordPass is significantly cheaper, starting at $2.49/user/month for Premium, while Dashlane starts at $7.99/user/month. For teams, NordPass Business is $4.99/user/month versus Dashlane Business at $10/user/month.

Can I switch from NordPass to Dashlane?

Yes, Dashlane supports importing data from NordPass via CSV export. The process takes under 15 minutes, and Dashlane’s setup wizard guides you through migration, including secure re-encryption of passwords.

Does Dashlane or NordPass have a free plan?

Both offer free plans. Dashlane’s free plan allows one user and unlimited passwords on one device. NordPass free plan supports one device and basic password storage but no sharing or sync across devices.

Which has better customer support, Dashlane or NordPass?

Dashlane offers faster response times with 24/7 live chat and phone support for enterprise customers, typically responding within 1 hour. NordPass provides solid email and chat support with average response times of 2–4 hours.

Is Dashlane or NordPass better for small teams?

NordPass is better for very small teams on a tight budget due to its lower pricing and simple setup. However, Dashlane offers more value as teams grow, with superior admin controls and security auditing.

Does Dashlane integrate with NordPass?

No, Dashlane does not directly integrate with NordPass. However, both allow CSV import/export, enabling easy one-time migration of passwords and secure notes between platforms.

Which tool has more features, Dashlane or NordPass?

Dashlane has more features, including dark web monitoring, form filling, secure sharing with expiration, password health scoring, and detailed audit logs. NordPass focuses on core functionality with fewer advanced tools.

Feature Deep Dive

Dashlane excels with features like Password Health, which scans for reused, weak, or compromised passwords and suggests fixes. It includes Dark Web Monitoring that alerts admins if employee credentials appear in breaches. Secure Sharing allows time-limited and permission-based access to logins, ideal for IT teams. NordPass offers Essentials Mode for simplified access, Biometric Lock, and Data Breach Scanner, but lacks real-time monitoring and detailed reporting. Dashlane also supports automated password changing for select sites like PayPal and LinkedIn, a feature NordPass doesn’t offer.

Pricing Breakdown

Dashlane’s individual Premium plan costs $7.99/month (billed annually at $95.88), including unlimited devices and dark web monitoring. The Business plan is $10/user/month (min 2 users), adding SSO, SCIM, and audit logs. NordPass Premium is $2.49/month ($29.88/year), with unlimited passwords on unlimited devices only in paid tiers. NordPass Business is $4.99/user/month (min 3 users), offering team folders and admin controls but fewer compliance features than Dashlane.

Who Should Use Dashlane

Dashlane is ideal for mid-sized to large businesses that require strong security compliance, audit trails, and centralized user management. Teams in regulated industries like finance, healthcare, or legal services benefit from its dark web monitoring and SSO integration. It suits companies with budgets prioritizing long-term scalability and advanced threat detection over initial cost savings.

Who Should Use NordPass

NordPass is best for small businesses, startups, or remote teams needing a simple, affordable password solution. Its clean interface and fast performance make it accessible for non-technical users. Organizations already using NordVPN may appreciate the unified ecosystem, though they should be aware of its limited enterprise-grade reporting and controls.

Migration & Setup

Switching from NordPass to Dashlane is straightforward using CSV export/import, preserving passwords, notes, and folders. Dashlane’s onboarding takes under 30 minutes per user, with guided setup and browser extension installation. Both tools support two-factor authentication setup during migration. Team admins can automate user provisioning via SCIM in Dashlane, a feature not available in NordPass.
