Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sucuri have a free trial?

Sucuri does not offer a traditional free trial on its paid plans. Instead it provides: (1) a completely free malware scanner (SiteCheck) that requires no account, and (2) a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. The guarantee is unconditional, no reason required within 30 days of purchase.

What does the Sucuri free scanner actually check?

Sucuri SiteCheck checks for externally visible malware, blacklist status on Google Safe Browsing, McAfee SiteAdvisor, and others, and outdated CMS/plugin versions. It does not scan your server's file system, that requires a paid plan with the server-side agent installed.

Is Sucuri worth it vs. a free WordPress security plugin?

Sucuri's WAF is positioned upstream of your server, it blocks traffic before it reaches WordPress. Free plugins like Wordfence operate on the server, meaning your server still processes the attack before blocking it. For high-traffic or revenue-critical sites, a WAF-first approach is more effective. For small blogs on a tight budget, Wordfence Free covers the basics.

Can I trial Sucuri on multiple sites?

No, each Sucuri plan covers one domain. If you want to trial it on multiple sites, you'd need to purchase separate plans, each with their own 30-day guarantee window. The Business and Platform plans can cover additional domains at extra cost.

What happens to my site's DNS if I cancel after the trial?

Your site keeps working, you just need to update your DNS records back to point directly to your origin server (instead of Sucuri's WAF). Sucuri provides the original DNS values in your dashboard. This takes effect within 24–48 hours of updating your registrar records.
