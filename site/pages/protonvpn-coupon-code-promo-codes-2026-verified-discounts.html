Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a working ProtonVPN promo code in 2026?

No — ProtonVPN does not issue public promo codes. The 2-year plan discount (up to 60% off) is applied automatically at checkout. Any code you find on coupon sites is expired or fake. The best legitimate deal is the multi-year plan directly on ProtonVPN's site.

Does ProtonVPN have a free trial?

ProtonVPN does not offer a traditional time-limited free trial. Instead, it has a forever-free plan with unlimited data and 1 device connection. Paid plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee, which effectively functions as a risk-free trial.

How much does ProtonVPN cost with the best deal?

As of June 2026, the 2-year ProtonVPN Plus plan starts at approximately $3.59–$4.99/month (billed every 2 years). The monthly plan costs $9.99/month. Annual plan falls between the two at around $5.99/month. Always check the current pricing on ProtonVPN's site as rates may vary by region.

Can I get ProtonVPN for free forever?

Yes. ProtonVPN's free plan is permanently free with no data cap, no ads, and no tracking. It includes 1 device, access to servers in 3 countries, and medium speeds. It's the only major free VPN we recommend without reservations.

Is ProtonVPN better than NordVPN?

It depends on your priorities. ProtonVPN wins on privacy (Swiss law, open source, audited), free plan availability, and ethical track record. NordVPN wins on price per month on the 2-year plan, server count, and streaming unblocking. For privacy-first users, ProtonVPN is the better pick.
