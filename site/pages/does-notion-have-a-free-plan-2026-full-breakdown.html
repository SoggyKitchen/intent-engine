Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion free forever?

Yes, Notion offers a free plan that you can use indefinitely. It includes unlimited pages and blocks, making it ideal for personal use, though with some feature and file size restrictions.

What do you get with Notion's free plan?

You get unlimited pages and blocks, basic collaboration with limited guests, mobile and desktop apps, and template access. File uploads are capped at 5MB, and advanced features like version history, API access, and full team permissions require a paid plan.

What happens when the Notion free trial ends?

Notion does not require a free trial — the free plan is always available without a time limit. If you're on a trial of a paid plan (e.g., Team or Enterprise), you’ll simply revert to the free plan unless you upgrade.
