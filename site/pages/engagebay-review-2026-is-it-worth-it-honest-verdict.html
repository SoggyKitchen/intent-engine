Feature Deep Dive

CRM & Contact Management

EngageBay's CRM covers the essentials well: contacts, companies, deals pipeline (drag-and-drop Kanban), tasks, and notes. Deal stages are customisable and the pipeline view is clean. Where it falls short is depth — custom objects, complex role-based permissions, and advanced forecasting require jumping to Salesforce or HubSpot Enterprise territory. For a team managing a straightforward B2B sales pipeline under 500 active deals, it's more than enough.

Email Marketing & Automation

Drag-and-drop email editor with pre-built templates — functional, not beautiful. Sequences and automation workflows are available on Pro. The automation builder handles linear flows (welcome series, lead nurture, deal-stage triggers) reliably. Multi-branch conditional logic is possible but can feel awkward compared to Klaviyo or ActiveCampaign. For most SMB email sequences, it covers 90% of use cases.

Helpdesk & Live Chat

The helpdesk module (ticketing, macros, canned responses, SLA policies on Pro) is a genuine differentiator — it's included in every paid plan rather than a separate add-on. Live chat widget installs with a single script. For a startup replacing Zendesk lite + Intercom free tier, this alone justifies the cost.

Landing Pages & Forms

Landing page builder is included from Starter. Templates are passable and the drag-and-drop editor works. Not Unbounce quality, but eliminates the need for a separate landing page tool for lead capture campaigns. Forms embed cleanly on external sites via a snippet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay actually free? +

Yes — EngageBay has a permanent free plan capped at 500 contacts and 250 emails/month. It includes basic CRM, email marketing, and live chat. For most solo founders and tiny teams it is genuinely usable, not a crippled trial.

How does EngageBay compare to HubSpot? +

EngageBay is far cheaper — Starter is $15/user/mo vs HubSpot Starter at $20/user/mo — and bundles CRM, email, helpdesk, and landing pages in one plan. HubSpot wins on integrations (1,500+ native), reporting depth, and brand trust. EngageBay wins on price for small teams that need all modules without paying separately for each Hub.

Does EngageBay have marketing automation? +

Yes, from the Pro plan ($49/user/mo). It includes email sequences, workflow automation, lead scoring, and A/B testing. The automation builder is drag-and-drop and handles most small-business use cases, though it lacks the depth of Klaviyo or ActiveCampaign for complex conditional flows.

Is EngageBay good for small businesses? +

Yes — it's one of the strongest all-in-one platforms for small businesses. If you need CRM + email marketing + helpdesk for a team under 20 and can't justify HubSpot's full-suite price, EngageBay delivers the most functionality per dollar without gutting key features.

What are EngageBay's biggest weaknesses? +

Three main weaknesses: (1) The interface is cluttered — many modules competing for screen space creates a real learning curve. (2) Native integrations are limited — you'll often need Zapier. (3) Reporting is basic on Starter and Free — no custom dashboards until Pro. If deep analytics or complex automation matter, consider HubSpot or Zoho CRM.
