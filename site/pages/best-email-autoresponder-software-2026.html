What Is an Email Autoresponder?

An email autoresponder automatically sends pre-written emails in a sequence based on a trigger — a sign-up, a purchase, a time delay, or a link click. Unlike one-off broadcast campaigns, autoresponders run on autopilot: a new subscriber gets welcome email #1 immediately, email #2 after 3 days, email #3 after 7 days — without any manual action.

The key difference between platforms is how many messages you can sequence, how granular the triggers are, and how reliably the emails land in the inbox.

How to Choose an Email Autoresponder

The right tool depends on your sequence complexity and budget:

  • You want the most powerful dedicated autoresponder: GetResponse — unlimited sequences, best deliverability, visual builder
  • You're starting out and want free: AWeber (500 contacts) or MailerLite (1,000 contacts)
  • You need behavioural triggers + CRM: ActiveCampaign — lead scoring, deal stage triggers
  • You're a creator or blogger: ConvertKit — simplest creator-focused sequence tool
  • You need a large free contact list: Brevo — unlimited contacts on free, 300 emails/day
  • You want brand recognition + integrations: Mailchimp — most widely used, 300+ integrations

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best email autoresponder software in 2026?

GetResponse is the best dedicated email autoresponder for most businesses — visual drag-and-drop sequence builder, unlimited messages, and a 99% deliverability guarantee from $15/month. AWeber is the best simple alternative with a full-featured free plan. ActiveCampaign wins for complex behavioural automation.

What is an email autoresponder?

An email autoresponder automatically sends pre-written emails based on a trigger — sign-up, purchase, time delay, or user action. Unlike broadcast campaigns, autoresponders are personalised sequences that run on autopilot without manual sending.

Is GetResponse good for autoresponders?

Yes — GetResponse was originally built as an autoresponder service and remains the category leader. It includes unlimited autoresponder messages on all paid plans, a visual sequence builder, AI subject line optimisation, and time-zone delivery. Starts at $15/month for 1,000 contacts.

What is the cheapest email autoresponder?

AWeber offers a free plan for up to 500 subscribers with full autoresponder access. MailerLite is free for up to 1,000 subscribers. GetResponse starts at $15/month and is the cheapest option that includes unlimited sequences, visual builder, and webinar hosting together.

Does Mailchimp have a good autoresponder?

Mailchimp's Customer Journeys builder handles basic autoresponders but restricts advanced triggers on the free plan. For sequence-heavy use cases, GetResponse or AWeber offer better value per contact. Mailchimp is stronger as a broadcast email tool than a dedicated autoresponder.

What's the difference between email marketing and an autoresponder?

Email marketing sends one-off broadcasts (newsletters, promotions) to your whole list at a scheduled time. An autoresponder sends automated emails triggered by a user action or time delay after sign-up — they are personalised, automated sequences, not manual campaigns.
