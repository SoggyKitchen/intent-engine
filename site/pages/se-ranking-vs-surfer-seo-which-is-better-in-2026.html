When to Choose SE Ranking

Choose SE Ranking if you need a complete SEO platform without the Semrush/Ahrefs price tag. It handles rank tracking, keyword research, site audits, backlink monitoring, and competitor analysis — all the core SEO workflows in one tool.

  • You're an SEO freelancer or small agency tracking rankings for multiple clients
  • You need a full-stack SEO platform (rank tracking + backlinks + audit + keywords) under $100/mo
  • You want white-label reports for client deliverables (Pro plan and above)
  • You're price-sensitive but need professional-grade data

When to Choose Surfer SEO

Choose Surfer SEO if content production is your primary bottleneck and you already have a rank tracker. Surfer's Content Editor is genuinely the best AI-driven on-page optimization tool — it analyses the top 20 results for your target keyword and gives precise guidance on headings, word count, and NLP terms.

  • You have writers who need AI-guided briefs and real-time content scoring
  • You already use Ahrefs or Semrush for rank tracking/research and just need a content layer
  • Your SEO strategy is primarily content-driven (blog, thought leadership, informational pages)
  • You're optimizing existing articles to recapture lost rankings

Pricing Compared

SE Ranking pricing

SE Ranking offers three plans on annual billing: Essential (~$44/mo), Pro (~$87/mo), Business (~$191/mo). Monthly billing costs approximately 25% more. The Essential plan covers 10 projects and 2,000 keywords for rank tracking — enough for a solo SEO or small site owner. Pro unlocks unlimited projects and white-label reports.

Surfer SEO pricing

Surfer SEO plans start at ~$79/mo (annual, Essential) and scale to ~$119/mo (Scale) and ~$199/mo (Scale AI). The Scale AI plan includes Surfer AI article generation, which can produce full SEO-optimized drafts. The Essential plan includes the Content Editor and SERP Analyzer but has limited article credits.
