Frequently Asked Questions

Is Brevo better than ConvertKit?

Brevo is better for teams needing CRM, sales alignment, and multi-channel automation. ConvertKit excels for creators who want simplicity and fast email campaign setup. The 'better' tool depends on your use case.

Which is cheaper, Brevo or ConvertKit?

ConvertKit is cheaper at lower subscriber volumes (e.g., $15 for 1K subs vs Brevo's $25). However, Brevo's usage-based pricing can be more cost-effective for high-volume, low-subscriber campaigns using automation and SMS.

Can I switch from ConvertKit to Brevo?

Yes, you can export subscribers, tags, and sequences from ConvertKit and import them into Brevo via CSV or Zapier. Brevo offers migration guides and API access to streamline the process.

Does Brevo or ConvertKit have a free plan?

Both offer free plans. Brevo allows 300 emails/day to unlimited contacts. ConvertKit allows up to 1,000 subscribers with full automation. Neither requires a credit card to start.

Which has better customer support, Brevo or ConvertKit?

Brevo offers 24/7 live chat and email on Premium plans, with faster response times (<2 hours). ConvertKit provides email support with 24–48 hour replies; priority support is limited to higher-tier plans.

Is Brevo or ConvertKit better for small teams?

ConvertKit is easier for small, solo teams focused on newsletters and courses. Brevo suits small teams planning to scale with CRM and sales tools, though it requires more setup.

Does Brevo integrate with ConvertKit?

There is no native two-way integration, but you can connect Brevo and ConvertKit via Zapier or Make to sync contacts or trigger actions across platforms during migration or hybrid use.

Which tool has more features, Brevo or ConvertKit?

Brevo has more features overall, including built-in CRM, transactional emails, SMS, and chat. ConvertKit focuses on email automation, landing pages, and tagging, with fewer enterprise capabilities.

Feature Deep Dive

Brevo’s Automation Builder supports multi-step workflows with conditions, delays, and actions across email, SMS, and CRM stages. It includes real-time analytics and A/B testing. ConvertKit’s Visual Automations are simpler, using subscriber tags and goals to trigger emails, ideal for course drip sequences. Brevo offers.Transactional Email API and dedicated IPs, while ConvertKit lacks transactional sending. Brevo’s built-in CRM with deal pipelines supports sales teams; ConvertKit has no CRM, limiting B2B use.

Pricing Breakdown

Brevo’s pricing starts with a free tier (300 emails/day). The Starter plan is $25/month for 10K emails, basic automation, and CRM. The Business plan ($65/month) adds A/B testing, dynamic content, and API access. ConvertKit’s free plan supports 1K subscribers. The Creator plan ($15/month) includes automation and landing pages. The Pro plan ($39/month) unlocks visual automation and broadcasts. Brevo charges by email volume, while ConvertKit scales by subscriber count, making Brevo more flexible for high-volume senders.

Who Should Use Brevo

Brevo is ideal for B2B SaaS companies, e-commerce brands, and marketing teams of 5+ people needing CRM integration, lead scoring, and multi-channel campaigns. It suits mid-market businesses with budgets over $25/month seeking scalability. Teams running complex automations with SMS, email, and sales follow-ups will benefit most from Brevo’s unified platform.

Who Should Use ConvertKit

ConvertKit is best for solopreneurs, bloggers, course creators, and indie hackers who prioritize ease of use and content-led marketing. It’s perfect for small teams with under 5K subscribers running newsletters, digital product launches, and automated content sequences. Its low entry cost and intuitive design make it a top choice for non-technical users.

Migration & Setup

Migrating from ConvertKit to Brevo takes 2–5 hours depending on list size and automation complexity. Both platforms allow CSV export of subscribers, tags, and segments. Brevo provides import templates and Zapier integration for seamless transfer. Onboarding in Brevo takes longer due to feature depth, while ConvertKit can be set up in under an hour.
