Frequently Asked Questions

Is Monday.com worth it in 2026?

Yes, for teams that need a flexible, visually-driven project management platform with strong automation and collaboration tools. However, its value diminishes if your needs are basic or your budget is tight.

What are the main complaints about Monday.com?

Users frequently cite rising costs as teams grow, a learning curve for new adopters, and the need to upgrade to higher plans to unlock essential features like time tracking and advanced reporting.

Who should NOT use Monday.com?

Small teams with simple task management needs, solopreneurs, or organizations prioritizing all-in-one functionality at a low cost should consider simpler or more feature-rich alternatives.
