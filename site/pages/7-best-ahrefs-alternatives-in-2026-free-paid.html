Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Ahrefs?

Mangools offers a generous free plan with access to KWFinder and SERP checker, making it the best free-tier option for keyword research.

Why are people switching from Ahrefs?

Users are leaving Ahrefs due to rising prices, complex reporting, and the availability of more intuitive, affordable tools with similar data accuracy.

How hard is it to migrate from Ahrefs?

Migration is generally easy—most tools support CSV import for keywords and backlinks. Use API access or built-in migration guides in Semrush or SE Ranking for a smooth transition.
