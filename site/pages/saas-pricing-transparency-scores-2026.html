How we score

Ten tests, two points each, twenty points total, reported out of ten. Nothing weighted, nothing subjective. A vendor who fixes their pricing page scores higher on the next run automatically.

Publishes a real priceEvery tier shows a number instead of a contact-sales form.
No mandatory setup feeNo required one-time onboarding or implementation charge.
Will sell you one seatNo minimum seat count standing between you and the advertised price.
Lets you pay monthlyPaid tiers can be bought month to month, not annual commitment only.
Has a free tierA genuinely free plan you can stay on, not just a countdown trial.
Trial without a cardYou can try it without handing over card details first.
Monthly billing isn't punishedThe premium for paying monthly instead of yearly stays under 25%.
Extra seats at list priceAdding a colleague costs the advertised per-seat price, with no surcharge.
The price is the whole billNo usage or transaction charges metered on top of the published plan price.
No intro-price cliffThe advertised entry price is the real one, not a promotion that expires.
