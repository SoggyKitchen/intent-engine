Editorial ratings

SaaS pricing transparency scores

Not user reviews. Our own score for one thing: how straight a vendor's pricing page is with the buyer. Eight tests, two points each, sixteen points total. Every point traces back to a price we read off the vendor's own page, and every tool gets a written verdict.

Pricing verified 6 September 2026 · 15 vendors · 8 criteria · recomputed nightly, never hand-set

What the rankings say

Asana tops the table on 16/16. Ahrefs comes last on 9/16, and not for being expensive: no free tier, no trial, and $40–$80 a month for each extra seat.

HubSpot publishes $100 a seat and charges a required $1,500 onboarding fee on top, making year one $2,700 for a single seat.

Categories with only one tracked vendor are left out. A field of one has no winner.

#1

Asana

Project management

5.0 · 16/16 points

The pricing page does everything right

Free for up to ten people, then $13.49 a seat. No minimum, no setup fee, no card to start, and the annual discount is a modest 23% rather than a penalty for staying flexible. There is nothing hidden here, which is rarer than it should be.

Best for Teams who want to grow into a paid plan on their own schedule.

Watch for The jump from Starter to Advanced is more than double, at $30.49 a seat.

#15

Ahrefs

SEO tools

3.0 · 9/16 points

Lowest score here, and not because it is expensive

No free tier and no trial, so there is no way to evaluate it without paying. Each plan includes exactly one user and extra seats cost $40 to $80 a month on top. Best for Solo operators who already know they want it.
