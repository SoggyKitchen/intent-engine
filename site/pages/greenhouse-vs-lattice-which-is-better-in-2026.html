Feature Deep Dive

Greenhouse offers a wide range of features, including applicant tracking, interview management, and reporting tools. Lattice, on the other hand, focuses on employee engagement and offers features such as performance reviews and goal setting. While both platforms have their strengths, Greenhouse is a better choice for companies seeking a comprehensive recruitment platform.

Pricing Breakdown

Greenhouse offers a custom pricing plan for each customer, but typical prices range from $150 to $300 per user per month. Lattice, on the other hand, offers a free plan, as well as several paid plans starting at $5 per user per month. While Greenhouse is more expensive, Lattice's pricing is more transparent and easier to understand.

Who Should Use Greenhouse

Greenhouse is best suited for mid-to-large-sized companies seeking a robust, scalable recruitment platform with advanced features. This includes companies with 100+ employees, a complex hiring process, and a need for advanced reporting and analytics.

Who Should Use Lattice

Lattice is best suited for smaller teams seeking an affordable, user-friendly solution with a focus on employee engagement. This includes companies with 10-50 employees, a simple hiring process, and a need for basic recruitment and performance management features.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Lattice to Greenhouse may require some time and effort to migrate your data and set up the new platform. However, both platforms offer a range of resources and support to help with the transition.
