Frequently Asked Questions

Is SE Ranking better than Rankmath Pro?

It depends on your needs. SE Ranking offers broader functionality including rank tracking, backlink analysis, and competitor research, making it better for comprehensive SEO management. Rankmath Pro excels in on-page optimization within WordPress but lacks external analytics.

Which is cheaper, SE Ranking or Rankmath Pro?

Rankmath Pro is generally cheaper, especially for individuals or small businesses. Its Pro plan costs $99/year (about $8.25/month), while SE Ranking starts at $39/month. However, SE Ranking’s higher cost reflects its broader enterprise-grade features.

Can I switch from Rankmath Pro to SE Ranking?

Yes, you can switch, though the tools serve different purposes. You’d use SE Ranking for external SEO analytics while potentially keeping Rankmath for on-page optimization. Data migration is limited since Rankmath doesn’t export rank or backlink data, but SEO reports can be manually transferred.

Does SE Ranking or Rankmath Pro have a free plan?

Neither offers a permanent free plan. SE Ranking provides a 14-day free trial with full access. Rankmath offers a powerful free version on WordPress.org, while Rankmath Pro requires a paid license for premium features.

Which has better customer support, SE Ranking or Rankmath Pro?

SE Ranking offers 24/7 live chat and email support with average response times under 2 hours. Rankmath Pro provides ticket-based support with 24–48 hour response times. SE Ranking wins for urgent agency needs, while Rankmath’s support is sufficient for most small users.

Is SE Ranking or Rankmath Pro better for small teams?

Small teams focused on WordPress content may prefer Rankmath Pro for its ease of use and low cost. However, small SEO agencies or growth-focused teams needing rank tracking and client reporting will benefit more from SE Ranking despite the higher price.

Does SE Ranking integrate with Rankmath Pro?

There is no direct integration between SE Ranking and Rankmath Pro. However, both can be used together—Rankmath for on-page SEO within WordPress, and SE Ranking for external monitoring, keyword tracking, and competitor analysis.

Which tool has more features, SE Ranking or Rankmath Pro?

SE Ranking has more features overall, including keyword rank tracking, backlink analysis, competitor research, and white-label reporting. Rankmath Pro focuses on on-page SEO, schema markup, and content optimization, offering fewer but highly specialized tools within WordPress.

Feature Deep Dive

SE Ranking offers real-time keyword tracking with location-based results across Google, Bing, and regional search engines, while Rankmath Pro lacks native rank tracking. SE Ranking’s Website Audit tool scans for 70+ technical SEO issues and integrates with Google Search Console and Analytics. Rankmath Pro shines with its AI-powered content analyzer, schema generator, and built-in redirection manager. However, SE Ranking’s competitor analysis, backlink gap tool, and white-label reporting make it far more suitable for strategic SEO planning across multiple domains.

Pricing Breakdown

SE Ranking’s pricing starts at $39/month (billed annually) for the Starter plan, allowing 10,000 tracked keywords and 200 daily searches. The Pro plan at $119/month increases limits to 35,000 keywords and adds competitor research. The Business plan at $299/month supports 100,000 keywords and unlimited reports. Rankmath Pro starts at $59/year for one site (Basic), $99/year for three sites (Pro), and $199/year for ten sites (Agency). Lifetime plans are also available, making Rankmath Pro more cost-effective for long-term WordPress users.

Who Should Use SE Ranking

SE Ranking is ideal for digital marketing agencies, in-house SEO teams, and enterprises managing multiple websites. It suits teams needing detailed competitor intelligence, client reporting, and scalable keyword tracking. With support for collaboration, role-based access, and API access, it fits organizations with budgets for robust SEO infrastructure and a need for data-driven decision-making.

Who Should Use Rankmath Pro

Rankmath Pro is best for WordPress users—bloggers, small businesses, and freelancers—who want to optimize on-page SEO without complexity. It’s perfect for those already using WordPress and seeking schema markup, readability analysis, and technical SEO improvements directly in the editor. Its low annual cost and lifetime options make it a smart pick for budget-conscious individuals.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Rankmath Pro to SE Ranking is straightforward for analytics purposes, though not a direct replacement. SE Ranking requires domain verification and API setup with Google services, typically taking under 30 minutes. Rankmath Pro data (like SEO scores) cannot be exported, but content optimizations remain in place. Onboarding into SE Ranking takes 1–2 hours for full configuration, including keyword lists and competitor sets.
