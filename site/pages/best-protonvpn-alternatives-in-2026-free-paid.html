Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to ProtonVPN?

Windscribe is the best free ProtonVPN alternative — 10GB/month free across 14 server countries, vs ProtonVPN's unlimited bandwidth but only one server location on the free tier. For a paid alternative, NordVPN at $3.39/mo is the top pick.

Is NordVPN better than ProtonVPN?

For most users, yes. NordVPN has 6,400+ servers vs ProtonVPN's ~3,000, faster speeds, and a PwC-audited no-logs policy. ProtonVPN's advantage is Swiss jurisdiction and fully open-source apps — stronger for activists in high-risk regions. For everyday privacy, NordVPN delivers equal protection at a lower price.

Why would someone switch from ProtonVPN?

The most common reasons: ProtonVPN is expensive ($4.99–$9.99/mo paid), the free tier is slow with only 3 server countries, and streaming performance is weaker than NordVPN or Surfshark. Users switching for price go to Surfshark ($2.19/mo unlimited devices).

Which VPN is cheapest in 2026?

Surfshark at $2.19/month (2-year plan, unlimited devices) and CyberGhost at $2.03/month (3-year plan) are the cheapest mainstream VPNs in 2026. ProtonVPN's cheapest paid plan is $4.99/month — more than double. For a free option, Windscribe gives 10GB/month at no cost.
