Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rippling better than Greenhouse?

Rippling offers a more comprehensive platform with a wider range of features, making it a better choice for large enterprises with complex HR needs. Greenhouse, on the other hand, is specialized in recruitment and talent management, making it a better choice for mid-sized teams with a focus on these areas.

Which is cheaper, Rippling or Greenhouse?

Rippling is generally cheaper than Greenhouse, especially for large teams. However, Greenhouse offers a more affordable plan for small teams.

Can I switch from Greenhouse to Rippling?

Yes, it's possible to switch from Greenhouse to Rippling. However, the migration process may take some time, and you may need to export data from Greenhouse and import it into Rippling.

Does Rippling or Greenhouse have a free plan?

Neither Rippling nor Greenhouse offers a free plan. However, both platforms offer a free trial and a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Which has better customer support, Rippling or Greenhouse?

Rippling offers 24/7 support, while Greenhouse offers 24/5 support. However, both platforms have a reputation for providing excellent customer support.

Is Rippling or Greenhouse better for small teams?

Greenhouse is a better choice for small teams, as it offers a more affordable plan and specialized recruitment tools. However, Rippling may be a better choice for small teams with complex HR needs.

Does Rippling integrate with Greenhouse?

No, Rippling and Greenhouse do not integrate directly. However, you can export data from Greenhouse and import it into Rippling.

Which tool has more features, Rippling or Greenhouse?

Rippling has a wider range of features, including HR, finance, and IT operations. Greenhouse, on the other hand, specializes in recruitment and talent management, but offers a range of features to streamline the hiring process and improve candidate experience.

Feature Deep Dive

Rippling offers a comprehensive suite of features to manage HR, finance, and IT operations, including employee onboarding, payroll, benefits, and compliance management. Greenhouse, on the other hand, specializes in recruitment and talent management, offering features such as applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and candidate scoring. While both platforms offer a range of features, Rippling's comprehensive platform makes it a better choice for large enterprises with complex HR needs.

Pricing Breakdown

Rippling offers a custom pricing plan for large enterprises, starting at $8 per employee per month. Greenhouse offers a custom pricing plan for mid-sized teams, starting at $15 per user per month. Both platforms offer a free trial and a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Who Should Use Rippling

Rippling is ideal for large enterprises with complex HR needs, including employee onboarding, payroll, benefits, and compliance management. It's also suitable for teams with a wide range of HR, finance, and IT operations.

Who Should Use Greenhouse

Greenhouse is ideal for mid-sized teams with a focus on recruitment and talent management, including applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and candidate scoring. It's also suitable for teams with a limited HR, finance, and IT operations.

Migration & Setup

The migration process from Greenhouse to Rippling may take some time, and you may need to export data from Greenhouse and import it into Rippling. However, both platforms offer a range of resources and support to help with the migration process.
