Overview: GetResponse vs Campaign Monitor

GetResponse Founded 1998 · Poland-based

GetResponse started as a pure email tool and evolved into a full marketing platform. It now includes webinar hosting, landing page builder, paid ads integration, and visual marketing automation — all in one subscription. The free plan (500 contacts, basic email) is genuinely usable for small lists.

Campaign Monitor Founded 2004 · Sydney, Australia

Campaign Monitor has always prioritised design quality and deliverability. It's popular with agencies because of multi-client account management and branded reporting. The drag-and-drop email builder produces some of the cleanest rendering across email clients — but it lacks a free-forever plan and webinar features.

Who Should Pick GetResponse?

  • You want email + webinars + landing pages in one tool without Zoom add-ons
  • You need a visual automation builder at a mid-tier price ($49/mo)
  • You're starting out and want a free plan (500 contacts) before committing
  • You sell online courses, coaching, or digital products via email funnels

Who Should Pick Campaign Monitor?

  • You're an agency managing email campaigns for multiple clients
  • Pixel-perfect, premium-designed emails matter more than automation depth
  • You need deep Shopify, Salesforce, or Magento integrations
  • Your team values world-class deliverability tools and inbox preview testing

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GetResponse better than Campaign Monitor?
GetResponse scores 8.4/10 vs Campaign Monitor's 7.8/10. GetResponse wins for automation, webinars, landing pages, and pricing. Campaign Monitor wins for premium email design quality and agency multi-client tools.
How much does GetResponse cost vs Campaign Monitor?
GetResponse: free plan (500 contacts) then $15/mo for Email Marketing, $49/mo for Marketing Automation. Campaign Monitor: Basic from $9/mo (500 contacts), Unlimited from $29/mo, Premier from $149/mo. Campaign Monitor has no free-forever tier.
Which has better email automation — GetResponse or Campaign Monitor?
GetResponse wins on automation depth. GetResponse includes visual workflow automation on the $49/mo Marketing Automation plan. Campaign Monitor's Journey automation is available on Unlimited ($29/mo) and above, but has fewer trigger types.
Does Campaign Monitor have a free plan?
No. Campaign Monitor does not offer a free-forever plan. There is a limited free trial (up to 5 emails to 5 subscribers). Paid plans start at $9/mo for 500 contacts. GetResponse offers a genuine free plan for up to 500 contacts with no time limit.
Who should use Campaign Monitor instead of GetResponse?
Campaign Monitor suits agencies managing multiple client accounts, teams who prioritise premium email design quality and inbox rendering, and businesses needing native Salesforce or Shopify integration. For solo marketers and SMBs needing automation plus webinars, GetResponse is the better value.
