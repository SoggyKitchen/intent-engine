Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best crm for startups for startup founders and early sales teams?

HubSpot CRM is our top pick for startup founders and early sales teams — excellent features.

Is there a free crm for startups?

Yes — Pipedrive, Notion have free plans. HubSpot CRM also offers a free trial.

How do I choose the right tool for my team?

Consider: team size, budget, must-have features, and current tools you use. We recommend trialling HubSpot CRM and Pipedrive side-by-side since both offer free access.

What should I look for when comparing these tools?

Key criteria: pricing per user at your team size, free trial length and completeness, integration with your existing stack, mobile app quality, and customer support response time.

How We Ranked These Tools

We evaluated each tool on: pricing value, feature depth, ease of onboarding, reliability, customer support quality, and integration ecosystem. Rankings reflect best fit for startup founders and early sales teams, not raw feature count.

Our #1 Pick and Why

HubSpot CRM tops our list for startup founders and early sales teams because it offers the best combination of features and value. It's the tool we'd recommend to most teams starting out.

Budget Pick

If budget is tight, Close offers the most value at the lowest price point. You trade some features for a significantly lower monthly cost — a reasonable trade for smaller teams.
