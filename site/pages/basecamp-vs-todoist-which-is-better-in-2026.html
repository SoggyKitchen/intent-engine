Frequently Asked Questions

Is Basecamp better than Todoist?

It depends on your needs. Basecamp is better for team collaboration and managing full project lifecycles, while Todoist excels at personal task management and speed. For teams, Basecamp offers more comprehensive features.

Which is cheaper, Basecamp or Todoist?

Todoist Business ($6/user/month) is cheaper than Basecamp ($15/user/month). However, Basecamp includes more built-in features like messaging and file storage, making it more cost-effective for teams needing an all-in-one solution.

Can I switch from Todoist to Basecamp?

Yes, you can export tasks from Todoist as CSV and manually import them into Basecamp. While no direct integration exists, third-party tools like Zapier can automate partial data migration, though full context like comments may be lost.

Does Basecamp or Todoist have a free plan?

Both offer free plans. Basecamp’s free plan supports up to 3 projects with full feature access. Todoist’s free plan includes core task management for individuals with limited collaboration features.

Which has better customer support, Basecamp or Todoist?

Basecamp offers email support with typical 24-hour response times and a robust help center. Todoist provides faster email and chat support (under 12 hours) for Business users, giving it an edge in responsiveness.

Is Basecamp or Todoist better for small teams?

Basecamp is better for small teams (5–20 people) managing client work or internal projects requiring chat, docs, and deadlines. Todoist suits micro-teams or solopreneurs prioritizing task speed over collaboration.

Does Basecamp integrate with Todoist?

There is no native integration, but you can connect Basecamp and Todoist using automation platforms like Zapier or Make. This allows syncing tasks or notifications between the two tools.

Which tool has more features, Basecamp or Todoist?

Basecamp has more built-in features including message boards, schedules, docs, and group chat. Todoist focuses on task depth—like natural language input, recurring due dates, and priority levels—but lacks collaboration tools.

Feature Deep Dive

Basecamp’s ‘Hill Charts’ help visualize project progress toward goals, while ‘Campfire’ offers real-time team chat. Todoist’s ‘Natural Language Input’ lets users type ‘Call John every Monday at 9am’ to auto-create recurring tasks. Basecamp includes ‘Schedule’ for deadlines and ‘Pings’ for direct messaging, whereas Todoist relies on third-party tools for communication. Todoist supports ‘Labels’ and ‘Filters’ for advanced task organization, while Basecamp uses simpler to-do lists within Projects. For holistic project oversight, Basecamp wins; for granular task control, Todoist leads.

Pricing Breakdown

Basecamp charges $15 per user per month (billed annually) or $24 monthly, with a free plan limited to 3 projects. Todoist offers a free tier, Pro at $4/month (individual), and Business at $6/user/month (team features, admin controls, and shared projects). While Todoist Business is cheaper, Basecamp’s flat rate includes unlimited projects, file storage, and collaboration tools—making it more comprehensive at a higher price point.

Who Should Use Basecamp

Basecamp is ideal for small to mid-sized agencies, consultancies, or remote teams managing client projects. Teams of 5–50 people who value simplicity and all-in-one functionality will benefit most. It’s perfect for those avoiding tool sprawl and needing built-in chat, docs, and schedules. Companies with tight budgets but complex collaboration needs should consider Basecamp’s value despite the higher per-user cost.

Who Should Use Todoist

Todoist is best for freelancers, solopreneurs, or small teams focused on personal productivity and task execution. It suits individuals who want fast, reliable to-do management across devices. Teams under 10 with minimal collaboration needs but high task volume will appreciate its speed and automation. Budget-conscious users will value its low-cost Pro and Business tiers.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Todoist to Basecamp requires manual effort or third-party automation via Zapier. Todoist allows CSV exports of tasks, but context like comments or labels won’t transfer directly. Basecamp’s setup is intuitive—teams can be onboarded in under an hour. Most users report full migration taking 1–3 days depending on project complexity and historical data volume.
