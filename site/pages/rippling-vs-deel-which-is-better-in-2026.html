Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rippling better than Deel?

Rippling offers more comprehensive HR features and scalability, making it a better choice for large enterprises. Deel is ideal for smaller teams and startups that require affordable and easy-to-use global employment capabilities.

Which is cheaper, Rippling or Deel?

Deel is generally cheaper than Rippling, especially for small teams and startups. However, Rippling's custom pricing plan can be more cost-effective for large enterprises.

Can I switch from Deel to Rippling?

Yes, it's possible to switch from Deel to Rippling. However, the migration process can be complex, and it's recommended to consult with a Rippling expert to ensure a smooth transition.

Does Rippling or Deel have a free plan?

Neither Rippling nor Deel offers a free plan. However, Deel offers a 14-day free trial, while Rippling offers a custom demo.

Which has better customer support, Rippling or Deel?

Rippling offers 24/7 support, while Deel provides email and chat support. Rippling's customer support is generally faster and more responsive.

Is Rippling or Deel better for small teams?

Deel is a better choice for small teams due to its affordable pricing and easy-to-use interface. However, Rippling's custom pricing plan can be more cost-effective for small teams with complex HR needs.

Does Rippling integrate with Deel?

No, Rippling and Deel do not integrate directly. However, both platforms offer integrations with other HR tools and services.

Which tool has more features, Rippling or Deel?

Rippling offers more comprehensive HR features, including payroll, benefits, and compliance management. Deel focuses on global employment capabilities and international hiring.

Feature Deep Dive

Rippling offers a comprehensive suite of HR features, including payroll, benefits, compliance, and time-off management. Deel, on the other hand, focuses on global employment capabilities and international hiring. While Deel's features are more limited, it's easier to use and more affordable.

Pricing Breakdown

Rippling's custom pricing plan starts at $8 per employee per month for the basic plan and $12 per employee per month for the premium plan. Deel's pricing plans start at $49 per month for the basic plan and $99 per month for the premium plan. While Deel is generally cheaper, Rippling's custom pricing plan can be more cost-effective for large enterprises.

Who Should Use Rippling

Rippling is ideal for large enterprises with complex HR needs. It's suitable for companies with 100+ employees, multiple locations, and complex payroll and benefits requirements. Rippling's comprehensive features and scalability make it an ideal choice for large enterprises.

Who Should Use Deel

Deel is suitable for small teams and startups that require affordable and easy-to-use global employment capabilities. It's ideal for companies with 10-50 employees, international hiring needs, and limited HR resources. Deel's user-friendly interface and affordable pricing make it an ideal choice for small teams and startups.

Migration & Setup

The migration process from Deel to Rippling can be complex and time-consuming. It's recommended to consult with a Rippling expert to ensure a smooth transition. Rippling offers data exports and onboarding support to help with the migration process.
