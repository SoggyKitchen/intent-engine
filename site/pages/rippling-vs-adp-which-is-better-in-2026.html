Rippling vs ADP: Verdict at a Glance

The core difference: Rippling is built for how modern companies work — one login for HR, payroll, benefits, and IT. ADP is built for compliance and scale — it's the safe choice when you need iron-clad tax filing, union payroll, or enterprise-grade SLAs.

For most teams under 500 employees switching from spreadsheets or a dated payroll provider, Rippling wins on ease-of-use, speed of setup, and total cost. ADP wins when you're running 50-state payroll with union contracts, or when your existing ERP (SAP, Oracle) has deep ADP integrations.

Our Final Verdict (July 2026)

Rippling is our recommendation for the majority of growing businesses. Here is the breakdown:

  • Choose Rippling if: you have 10–500 employees, want HR and IT in one platform, need a modern employee experience, or are transitioning from manual processes. Particularly strong for tech companies and remote teams.
  • Choose ADP if: you're a large enterprise (500+ employees) with complex union/prevailing wage payroll, need the deepest ACA compliance tools available, or already have ADP deeply integrated into your ERP stack.
  • Price-sensitive small businesses: Rippling wins. ADP Run's pricing ($59/mo base + $4/employee) adds up fast and you still get less than what Rippling offers.
  • Global teams: Rippling wins. ADP Global Payroll exists but is a patchwork of regional products vs. Rippling's unified global platform covering 50+ countries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rippling better than ADP?
Rippling scores 8.8/10 vs ADP's 7.9/10 in our editorial scoring. Rippling wins on modern UX, automation, and unified HR+IT. ADP wins on enterprise compliance depth and union payroll. For most businesses under 500 employees, Rippling is the better choice.
How much does Rippling cost vs ADP?
Rippling starts at $8/user/month for the payroll module (full platform is custom-priced). ADP Run starts at $59/month base + $4/employee/month. For 25 employees, ADP Run would cost ~$159/mo vs Rippling's estimated $200–$300/mo all-in. At scale the gap narrows — get custom quotes from both.
Can I try Rippling and ADP for free?
Rippling offers a free live demo — no self-serve trial due to payroll data setup requirements. ADP offers a 3-month free trial for ADP Run (small business), and a demo for Workforce Now (mid-market). We recommend requesting both demos to compare the experience firsthand.
Which is better for small businesses — Rippling or ADP?
Rippling wins for small businesses. Cleaner interface, faster setup, better employee self-service, and more affordable at low headcounts. ADP Run is functional but the UI is dated and setup often requires help from ADP's support team.
Does ADP integrate with Rippling?
No — Rippling and ADP are competing platforms and don't integrate with each other. You choose one. Rippling offers 500+ integrations with tools like Slack, QuickBooks, Google Workspace, Greenhouse, and more.
Which handles international payroll better?
Rippling wins on modern global payroll — 50+ countries with one unified login. ADP offers global payroll through ADP Celergo and global products, but the experience varies by country/product. For companies expanding internationally, Rippling's single-platform approach is significantly easier to manage.
