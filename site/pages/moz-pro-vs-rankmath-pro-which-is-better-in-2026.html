Frequently Asked Questions

Is Moz Pro better than Rankmath Pro?

Moz Pro excels in enterprise SEO with advanced analytics, rank tracking, and team collaboration, making it better for large-scale operations. Rankmath Pro is more suited for WordPress users focused on on-page SEO and content optimization at a lower cost.

Which is cheaper, Moz Pro or Rankmath Pro?

Rankmath Pro is significantly cheaper, starting at $59/year, while Moz Pro starts at $99/month ($1,188/year). For budget-conscious users, especially WordPress site owners, Rankmath Pro offers better value.

Can I switch from Rankmath Pro to Moz Pro?

Yes, you can switch, though the tools serve different purposes. You’ll need to export keyword data and rankings manually or via API, then import into Moz Pro. Onboarding takes 1–2 weeks depending on site complexity.

Does Moz Pro or Rankmath Pro have a free plan?

Moz Pro offers a 30-day free trial but no permanent free plan. Rankmath Pro also offers a 7-day trial and has a robust free version of the Rank Math plugin available in the WordPress repository.

Which has better customer support, Moz Pro or Rankmath Pro?

Moz Pro offers superior support with 24/7 chat, email, and a comprehensive help center, typically responding within 2 hours. Rankmath Pro provides email and community support with average response times of 24–48 hours.

Is Moz Pro or Rankmath Pro better for small teams?

For small teams using WordPress, Rankmath Pro is more cost-effective and easier to adopt. However, if the team needs advanced SEO analytics and scalability, Moz Pro is worth the investment despite the higher cost.

Does Moz Pro integrate with Rankmath Pro?

There is no direct integration between Moz Pro and Rankmath Pro. However, both can coexist on a WordPress site—Rankmath Pro for on-page SEO and Moz Pro for external analytics, rank tracking, and backlink monitoring.

Which tool has more features, Moz Pro or Rankmath Pro?

Moz Pro offers more features overall, especially in rank tracking, site audits, and backlink analysis. Rankmath Pro focuses on depth in on-page SEO, schema, and content optimization, but lacks the breadth of enterprise tools found in Moz Pro.

Feature Deep Dive

Moz Pro offers flagship features like Keyword Explorer, Rank Tracker, Site Crawl, and Link Explorer, which provide deep insights into keyword performance, technical SEO issues, and domain authority. Rankmath Pro counters with real-time content scoring, AI-driven optimization suggestions, and automatic schema markup generation directly in the WordPress editor. While Moz Pro delivers broader, data-rich analytics across platforms, Rankmath Pro excels in contextual, on-page guidance for content creators. Moz’s Campaigns feature enables multi-client reporting, ideal for agencies, whereas Rankmath Pro’s bulk editing and SEO checks streamline WordPress site management.

Pricing Breakdown

Moz Pro pricing starts at $99/month for the Starter plan (10 campaigns, 1 user), moves to $249/month (Medium: 25 campaigns, 3 users), and tops out at $599/month (Large: 100 campaigns, 10 users). All plans require annual billing. Rankmath Pro offers three tiers: Basic ($59/year for 1 site), Pro ($79/year for 3 sites), and Agency ($199/year for 100 sites). The annual pricing model of Rankmath Pro makes it dramatically more affordable, especially for small publishers and freelancers managing multiple WordPress sites.

Who Should Use Moz Pro

Moz Pro is ideal for mid-sized to large businesses, digital marketing agencies, and SEO teams that need scalable, data-driven insights. It suits organizations with dedicated SEO budgets and cross-functional collaboration needs. Teams that manage multiple domains, require detailed backlink analysis, or integrate SEO data into broader marketing platforms will benefit most from Moz Pro’s ecosystem and reporting depth.

Who Should Use Rankmath Pro

Rankmath Pro is best for WordPress-based businesses, bloggers, and small agencies focused on content optimization and technical SEO within the WordPress environment. It’s perfect for users who want an intuitive, all-in-one SEO plugin with AI-powered suggestions and schema tools without the overhead of enterprise software. Its low cost and ease of setup make it a top pick for SMBs and solo marketers.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Rankmath Pro to Moz Pro is straightforward but involves different use cases—Moz Pro doesn’t replace Rankmath’s on-page features but complements them with external analytics. You can export keyword lists and rankings via CSV or API and import them into Moz Pro. Onboarding typically takes 1–2 weeks, including crawl setup and campaign configuration. No direct plugin migration tool exists, but both can run concurrently during transition.
