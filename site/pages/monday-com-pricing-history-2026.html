Monday.com price timeline

Every verified pricing change since we started tracking. Sources documented.

November 2025 Price Increase
All tiers raised 10-14% — AI features cited
Monday.com raised pricing across all tiers: Basic from $10 to $12/seat, Standard from $14 to $17/seat, Pro from $24 to $28/seat (all per seat, 3-seat minimum, annual). The increase was tied to AI features (AI Automations, AI Column, AI Summarise) added to all paid plans.
Source: Monday.com Pricing Page (verified Nov 2025)
🆕
May 2025 New Feature
Monday AI tools added to all paid plans
Monday.com added AI Automations, AI Column (data enrichment), and AI Summary to Standard plans and above. Pro added advanced AI formulas. These features were cited in the November 2025 price increase.
Source: Monday.com Product Blog — AI Features
→
January 2024 No Change
Pricing held despite WorkOS platform expansion
Monday.com maintained 2022 pricing levels through 2024 despite launching monday dev (for software teams) and monday sales CRM as separate products.
Source: Monday.com Pricing Archive 2024
↑
June 2022 Price Increase
Plans restructured — 3-seat minimum enforced on all paid tiers
Monday.com formalised the 3-seat minimum across all paid plans, effectively removing the ability to purchase 1-2 seats at the basic rate. This represented a minimum spend increase for very small teams.
Source: Monday.com Pricing Page (verified Jun 2022)
→
January 2022 No Change
Annual pricing stable — 3-seat minimum structure maintained
Monday.com held annual pricing at Basic $8/seat, Standard $10/seat, Pro $16/seat through 2022 (3-seat minimums). The November 2025 increase was the most significant in recent history.
