Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Jira?

ClickUp offers the most robust free plan with unlimited tasks, custom views, and integrations, making it the best free alternative to Jira.

Why are people switching from Jira?

Teams leave Jira due to its complexity, high cost, poor usability for non-developers, and fragmented experience across Jira Software, Service Management, and Confluence.

How hard is it to migrate from Jira?

Migration difficulty varies: tools like ClickUp and Trello offer Jira importers. Plan for 1–2 weeks of data cleanup, user training, and workflow recreation to ensure a smooth transition.
