Verdict at a Glance

Both tools convert text to lifelike speech, but they serve different masters. ElevenLabs is the voice quality leader — its models produce audio that's consistently mistaken for human speech. Play.ht competes on value: its Unlimited plan ($99/mo) removes character caps entirely, making it attractive for teams generating high volumes of basic audio.

The gap is most obvious on cloning. ElevenLabs instant voice cloning (from Creator, $22/mo) is faster and more natural than Play.ht's equivalent. If you're cloning a voice for a podcast or brand, the quality difference matters enough to justify ElevenLabs' premium.

Our verdict: For most users — content creators, developers, marketers — ElevenLabs is the better tool. Start free (10K chars/month, no card required) and upgrade as you scale. Only choose Play.ht if you need unlimited characters for a budget below $99/mo and voice realism isn't critical.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ElevenLabs better than Play.ht?

ElevenLabs scores 9.3/10 vs Play.ht's 8.2/10. ElevenLabs wins on voice realism, instant cloning, and developer API depth. Play.ht wins on raw character volume at $99/mo (unlimited vs ElevenLabs' 500K). For most professional use cases, ElevenLabs is the stronger choice.

How much does ElevenLabs cost vs Play.ht?

ElevenLabs: Free (10K chars/mo), Starter $5/mo (30K chars), Creator $22/mo (100K chars + instant cloning), Pro $99/mo (500K chars). Play.ht: Starter $31/mo annual (100K chars), Pro $47/mo annual (300K chars), Unlimited $99/mo annual (unlimited chars). ElevenLabs is much cheaper to start and try.

Which is better for podcasters — ElevenLabs or Play.ht?

ElevenLabs is better for high-quality podcast voiceovers and cloning your own voice. Play.ht has a native podcast RSS feed integration and more podcast-focused workflows. If you're producing a lot of episodes and need unlimited audio cheaply, Play.ht Unlimited ($99/mo) has an edge. For a natural-sounding host voice, ElevenLabs wins.

Does Play.ht have a free plan?

Play.ht offers a limited free trial — not a permanent free plan with a monthly character allowance. ElevenLabs has a genuine free tier: 10,000 characters per month, no credit card required. If you want to test before paying, ElevenLabs is the easier starting point.

Can ElevenLabs clone voices better than Play.ht?

Yes — ElevenLabs instant voice cloning (from the Creator plan at $22/mo) requires only a 1-minute audio sample and produces very natural results. Play.ht also offers voice cloning but ElevenLabs consistently produces more human-sounding output. For cloning your own voice for content creation, ElevenLabs is the better choice.
