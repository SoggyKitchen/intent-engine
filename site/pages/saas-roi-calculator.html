How to use this calculator

  • Tool Cost: Enter the per-seat price from the tool's pricing page. Include setup only if there's real onboarding spend.
  • Time Savings: Be conservative — even 1 saved hour/week per employee adds up fast at scale.
  • Revenue Impact: Hard to estimate — leave at 0 if unsure. Don't inflate it.
  • A positive ROI with a payback period under 6 months is almost always a no-brainer.
