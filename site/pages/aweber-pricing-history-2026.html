2023 Restructure
AWeber restructured to Free / Lite / Plus / Unlimited tiers
AWeber moved from a single "Pro" plan model to a tiered structure. The Free plan (introduced 2021) was retained. A new "Lite" tier was added below Plus, targeting users who wanted more features than Free but didn't need unlimited subscribers. "Plus" became the main paid offering at ~$20/mo for unlimited subscribers billed annually. An "Unlimited" tier ($899/mo) was added for large senders wanting priority support and migration assistance.
Source: AWeber public pricing page (archived — general market knowledge, not independently scrape-verified by SaaSpare)
✓
Late 2021 Price Drop
AWeber launches Free plan — biggest pricing shift in company history
AWeber introduced a permanent Free plan in late 2021 supporting up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails per month. This was a significant competitive response to Mailchimp's dominant free tier and ConvertKit's growing free offering. Prior to this, AWeber had no free plan — all users paid from the first subscriber. The move made AWeber genuinely accessible to bootstrapped founders and micro-businesses for the first time.
Source: AWeber company announcement (public sources — not independently verified by SaaSpare scraper)
📋
Pre-2021 Paid-Only Era
AWeber Pro plan: $19/mo for up to 500 subscribers
Before the free plan launch, AWeber offered a single "Pro" tier priced by subscriber count: approximately $19/mo for up to 500 subscribers, scaling to $29/mo (501–2,500), $49/mo (2,501–5,000), and upward. There was no free tier — every user paid from subscriber one. This was AWeber's standard pricing model from its founding in 1998 through 2021, making the free-plan launch a major strategic shift.

AWeber pricing history FAQ

Has AWeber raised prices recently?

No verified price increases since we began tracking. AWeber's Free, Lite, Plus, and Unlimited tiers have all been stable through our monitoring period as of August 2026. We update this page immediately on any change.

When did AWeber add a free plan?

AWeber launched its free plan in late 2021. Before that, AWeber was entirely paid-only — starting at approximately $19/month for up to 500 subscribers. The free plan (500 subscribers, 3,000 emails/month) was a major competitive response to Mailchimp and ConvertKit.

What does AWeber cost in 2026?

AWeber 2026 pricing: Free ($0, up to 500 subscribers), Lite ($12.50/mo annually, up to 500 subscribers, removes AWeber branding), Plus ($20/mo annually, unlimited subscribers, full features), Unlimited ($899/mo, priority support and migration). Annual billing saves approximately 25-33% vs monthly billing.

Is AWeber cheaper than Mailchimp in 2026?

Depends on your list size. For under 500 subscribers: both are free. For larger lists, AWeber Plus at $20/mo covers unlimited subscribers — Mailchimp charges per contact tier, which scales more expensively for lists above 2,000-3,000. AWeber's flat-rate unlimited model is generally cheaper for fast-growing lists.

Will AWeber raise prices?

We have no evidence of an imminent AWeber price increase as of August 2026. AWeber has maintained stable pricing through the period we monitor. Annual billing locks your rate for 12 months — recommended while pricing is favourable.
