Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Notion cost per month?

Notion starts at $0 for the Free plan. The Plus plan is $8/user/month (billed annually) or $10/month (monthly). The Business plan is $15/user/month (annual) or $18/month (monthly). Enterprise pricing is custom and requires a sales consultation.

Does Notion have a free plan?

Yes, Notion offers a Free plan with unlimited pages and blocks for individuals and small teams. It supports up to 10 guests, making it ideal for early-stage startups or personal use. File uploads are limited to 5MB for guests, but full users have higher limits.

Is Notion worth the price?

For most B2B teams, yes — especially those prioritizing knowledge management and cross-functional collaboration. The Business plan pays for itself through improved onboarding, reduced tool sprawl, and centralized documentation. However, if your team needs heavy automation or CRM-like workflows, a cheaper alternative like ClickUp may offer better ROI.
