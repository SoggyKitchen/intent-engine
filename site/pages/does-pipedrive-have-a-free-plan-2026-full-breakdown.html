Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pipedrive free forever?

Yes, Pipedrive offers a free forever plan, but it's limited to one user and only includes three custom fields. Additional users and features require a paid plan.

What do you get with Pipedrive's free plan?

The free plan includes unlimited contacts and deals, basic pipeline management, email integration, and mobile access. However, it limits you to one user, three custom fields, and no automation or advanced reporting.

What happens when the Pipedrive free trial ends?

The 14-day free trial gives you full access to the Professional plan. After it ends, you're automatically downgraded to the free plan unless you upgrade to a paid plan. No credit card is required, so you won’t be charged.
