Frequently Asked Questions

Is Surfer SEO better than Mangools?

Surfer SEO is better for advanced content optimization and data-driven SEO strategies, especially for teams producing high volumes of content. Mangools excels in simplicity and affordability, making it ideal for beginners. The 'better' tool depends on your team's size, goals, and technical expertise.

Which is cheaper, Surfer SEO or Mangools?

Mangools is significantly cheaper, starting at $49/month compared to Surfer SEO’s $89/month. Even at higher tiers, Mangools’ Agency plan ($159) is less than half the cost of Surfer’s Business plan ($399), making it the more budget-friendly option.

Can I switch from Mangools to Surfer SEO?

Yes, you can switch from Mangools to Surfer SEO. While there's no direct import tool, you can export keyword lists, rank tracking data, and backlink reports from Mangools and manually upload them. Onboarding typically takes 1–2 weeks with Surfer’s onboarding resources.

Does Surfer SEO or Mangools have a free plan?

Neither tool offers a permanent free plan. However, Surfer SEO provides a 7-day free trial requiring a credit card, while Mangools offers a 10-day free trial with no credit card needed, giving users more time to evaluate without commitment.

Which has better customer support, Surfer SEO or Mangools?

Surfer SEO offers 24/7 live chat and email support with average response times under 2 hours. Mangools provides chat and email support during business hours (9–5 CET) with responses typically within 24 hours. Surfer SEO wins on availability and speed.

Is Surfer SEO or Mangools better for small teams?

Mangools is better for small teams or solopreneurs due to its lower cost and ease of use. Surfer SEO is more suited for teams of 3+ with dedicated SEO or content roles who need advanced analytics and AI content generation.

Does Surfer SEO integrate with Mangools?

No, Surfer SEO does not natively integrate with Mangools. However, both support CSV import/export, allowing partial data migration. Users often use Mangools for initial keyword research and Surfer for content optimization, manually transferring data between platforms.

Which tool has more features, Surfer SEO or Mangools?

Surfer SEO offers more advanced features, especially in AI content creation, SERP reverse-engineering, and content auditing. While Mangools includes five solid tools, Surfer’s feature depth—particularly in content optimization—gives it an edge in functionality and automation.

Feature Deep Dive

Surfer SEO’s Content Editor uses real-time NLP and SERP analysis to recommend word count, keyword density, and semantic terms, outperforming Mangools’ basic KWFinder suggestions. Surfer’s SERP Analyzer provides TF-IDF scoring and heading structure breakdowns, while Mangools’ SERPChecker offers simpler competitor snapshots. Surfer AI enables one-click content generation, a feature Mangools lacks entirely. For backlink analysis, Mangools’ LinkMiner is user-friendly but less comprehensive than third-party tools Surfer integrates with, like Ahrefs or Semrush. Overall, Surfer delivers deeper, AI-enhanced insights, while Mangools focuses on accessibility and core SEO tasks.

Pricing Breakdown

Surfer SEO’s pricing starts at $89/month (Basic) for 1 user, 500 keywords, and 10 content audits. The Pro plan ($199) adds Surfer AI and team collaboration. Business ($399) includes 5 users and 10,000 keywords. Enterprise ($599+) is custom. Mangools starts at $49/month (Starter) for 1 user and 150 keyword lookups/day. Pro ($99) adds rank tracking and 365 lookups/day. Agency ($159) supports 3 users and unlimited lookups. Annual billing saves 20% on both platforms, but Mangools remains the more cost-effective entry point.

Who Should Use Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO is ideal for SEO agencies, in-house content teams, and enterprises producing high-volume, performance-driven content. Teams with budgets over $200/month and users experienced in SEO analytics will maximize its AI-powered insights. It’s best suited for organizations prioritizing content quality, SERP dominance, and scalable workflows. If you use WordPress or Google Docs and want AI-generated drafts, Surfer is the clear fit.

Who Should Use Mangools

Mangools is perfect for freelancers, startups, and small businesses new to SEO. Its intuitive dashboard and low entry price make it accessible without requiring technical expertise. Users focused on keyword discovery, local rank tracking, and basic backlink checks will find it sufficient. If you need a simple, all-in-one starter SEO toolkit without long-term commitment, Mangools is a smart choice.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Mangools to Surfer SEO takes 1–2 weeks due to manual data transfer via CSV exports. While Surfer doesn’t import Mangools projects directly, keyword lists and rankings can be uploaded. Onboarding is smooth with video tutorials and a structured setup wizard. Surfer’s team collaboration features require initial configuration, but most users report full productivity within 10 days.

Our Testing Methodology

SaaSpare evaluated both tools over 60+ hours of hands-on testing, analyzing feature accuracy, UI/UX, support responsiveness, and real-world SEO impact. We used identical keyword sets and content briefs across both platforms and validated results against live SERP performance. Data was supplemented by user reviews from G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot to ensure balanced, real-world insights.
