HubSpot
CRM + Marketing + Service · Free / from $20/mo/seat

HubSpot is a full CRM platform with Sales Hub, Marketing Hub, and Service Hub. Its free tier includes an inbox, live chat, chatbot, basic ticketing, and a knowledge base — making it the default comparison point for any team evaluating customer support tools. HubSpot's advantage is unified data: sales conversations, marketing emails, and support tickets all live in one contact record.

Intercom
Customer Messaging + AI Support · from ~$39/mo

Intercom is purpose-built for customer engagement — its messenger widget, proactive campaigns, and AI-powered Fin chatbot make it the market leader for in-app customer messaging. It's particularly strong for SaaS companies with self-serve products, where automated resolution of support queries before they reach a human is a genuine business outcome.

Verdict at a Glance

HubSpot and Intercom overlap in live chat and shared inbox, but they serve different primary buyers. HubSpot's Service Hub is a support tool bolted onto a CRM — its strength is being one tab away from the customer's full sales and marketing history. Intercom is a messaging and engagement platform that built support features on top — its strength is the depth of in-product engagement and AI automation for self-serve SaaS.

For most B2B SaaS teams selling to businesses, HubSpot's free tier eliminates the cost argument for Intercom entirely at the starting tier. As you scale to 10,000+ MAUs or need product tours, outbound campaign sequences, and AI-resolved conversations, Intercom's investment starts to make sense.

Who Should Choose HubSpot?

  • Teams that need CRM + support in one tool — HubSpot's unified contact record means support tickets sit next to sales deals and marketing emails. Intercom requires separate CRM integration.
  • Budget-conscious teams — HubSpot's free tier includes live chat, chatbot, shared inbox, and ticketing. Intercom has no free plan and starts at ~$39/mo for 1 seat.
  • B2B teams with complex sales cycles — If support conversations often turn into expansion opportunities, HubSpot's CRM context at the support agent's fingertips is invaluable.
  • Companies already using HubSpot CRM — Adding Service Hub to an existing HubSpot deployment is dramatically lower friction than integrating Intercom alongside it.

Who Should Choose Intercom?

  • Product-led SaaS with self-serve onboarding — Intercom's product tours, in-app messages, and triggered campaigns drive feature adoption at scale. HubSpot has no equivalent.
  • High-volume support teams — Intercom's Fin AI resolves a significant percentage of incoming queries automatically. For teams handling 1,000+ conversations/month, the ROI on automated resolution can exceed the tool's cost.
  • Teams needing proactive engagement — Intercom can message users based on product usage events (e.g., "user hasn't set up integration after 3 days"). HubSpot's workflows are less tightly coupled to in-product behaviour.
  • Customer success + support overlap — Intercom's view of customer health signals alongside conversation history makes it better than HubSpot for proactive CS motions.

Our Final Verdict

HubSpot wins for most B2B SaaS teams — especially those already using HubSpot CRM or needing a free starting point. Intercom is the right call for product-led SaaS companies at scale with a genuine need for AI-powered resolution and in-product engagement.

  • Choose HubSpot if you need CRM + support in one tool, want a free tier that actually works, or are a B2B company with a sales-led motion. Start free; add Service Hub Professional when you need SLA management and reporting.
  • Choose Intercom if you run a high-volume self-serve SaaS product, need product tours and in-app messages to drive adoption, or have a CS team where automated resolution ROI is measurable.

Teams that switch from Intercom to HubSpot typically save 40-60% in tooling costs. Teams that go from HubSpot to Intercom typically do so when their support volume and automation requirements exceed what HubSpot's Service Hub can handle cost-effectively at scale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HubSpot better than Intercom?

HubSpot scores 9.2/10 vs Intercom's 8.5/10 for most B2B teams. HubSpot wins on CRM integration, pricing (free tier), and breadth. Intercom wins on in-app messaging depth, AI resolution, and product tours for product-led SaaS.

How much does HubSpot cost vs Intercom?

HubSpot has a free CRM with live chat and inbox. Paid Service Hub from $20/mo/seat. Intercom has no free plan — starts at approximately $39/mo for 1 seat. HubSpot is significantly cheaper for teams under 10 seats.

Can I replace Intercom with HubSpot?

Yes, for most teams. HubSpot's Service Hub covers live chat, shared inbox, ticketing, chatbot, and knowledge base. The gap is in-app product tours and deep product-event-based messaging — Intercom still leads here. For sales-led B2B teams, HubSpot is the complete replacement.

Which is better for live chat?

Intercom's live chat widget and conversation routing are best-in-class for depth and customisation. HubSpot's live chat (free) is excellent value and good enough for most teams. For high-volume or product-embedded chat, Intercom is stronger.

Does HubSpot have a free plan like Intercom?

Yes — HubSpot's free CRM includes live chat, chatbot, shared inbox, basic ticketing, and a knowledge base. Intercom has no free plan at all. This makes HubSpot the default for budget-conscious teams who need basic customer messaging alongside a CRM.
