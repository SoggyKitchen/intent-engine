Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1Password better than Keeper?

Yes, 1Password offers more advanced security features, seamless integrations, and a user-friendly interface, making it a better choice for large teams and enterprises.

Which is cheaper, 1Password or Keeper?

Keeper is generally cheaper than 1Password, especially for small teams and individuals.

Can I switch from Keeper to 1Password?

Yes, you can easily switch from Keeper to 1Password by exporting your data and importing it into 1Password.

Does 1Password or Keeper have a free plan?

Both 1Password and Keeper offer free plans, but 1Password's free plan is limited to a single user, while Keeper's free plan allows for multiple users.

Which has better customer support, 1Password or Keeper?

1Password offers 24/7 support, while Keeper offers 24/5 support.

Is 1Password or Keeper better for small teams?

Keeper is a better choice for small teams due to its more affordable pricing and essential password management features.

Does 1Password integrate with Keeper?

No, 1Password and Keeper do not integrate with each other.

Which tool has more features, 1Password or Keeper?

1Password has more advanced features, including two-factor authentication, encryption, and advanced collaboration tools, while Keeper offers essential password management features, including auto-fill and two-factor authentication.

Feature Deep Dive

1Password offers advanced security features, including two-factor authentication, encryption, and password vaults, while Keeper offers essential password management features, including auto-fill and two-factor authentication. 1Password also has a more user-friendly interface and intuitive navigation, making it easier to use for large teams and enterprises.

Pricing Breakdown

1Password offers a range of plans, including a free trial, Individual ($2.99/month), Family ($4.99/month), and Team ($7.99/month). Keeper offers a range of plans, including a free plan, Personal ($2.91/month), Family ($6.00/month), and Business ($3.33/month).

Who Should Use 1Password

1Password is ideal for large teams and enterprises that require robust security features, seamless integrations, and advanced collaboration tools. It's also a good choice for individuals who want a user-friendly interface and intuitive navigation.

Who Should Use Keeper

Keeper is ideal for small teams and individuals who require essential password management features, including auto-fill and two-factor authentication. It's also a good choice for those who want a more affordable option with a free plan.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Keeper to 1Password is easy, as you can export your data and import it into 1Password. The onboarding process typically takes a few hours, and 1Password offers 24/7 support to help with any issues.
