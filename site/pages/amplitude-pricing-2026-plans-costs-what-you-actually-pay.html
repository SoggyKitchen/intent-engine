Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Amplitude cost per month?

Amplitude does not charge a simple monthly rate. The Plus plan starts at approximately $1,250/month ($15,000/year) for up to 10 million events. Most mid-market companies pay between $2,000–$5,000/month depending on usage. Enterprise pricing is custom and typically requires annual commitments.

Does Amplitude have a free plan?

Yes. Amplitude offers a free plan that includes up to 10 million events per month, access to core analytics features (funnels, retention, segmentation), and up to 2 dashboards. It’s ideal for startups or small teams testing the platform.

Is Amplitude worth the price?

For product-led B2B companies, yes — Amplitude often delivers strong ROI by uncovering insights that improve conversion, retention, and feature adoption. Teams that actively use data to drive decisions typically see value that far exceeds the cost. However, smaller teams or those with basic analytics needs may find it overpriced.
