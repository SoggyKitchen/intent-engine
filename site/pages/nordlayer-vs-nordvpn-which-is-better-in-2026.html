NordLayer vs NordVPN: The Core Difference

Both products come from Nord Security — the same Lithuanian company behind NordPass and NordLocker. They share infrastructure, but they serve fundamentally different customers.

NordVPN is a consumer VPN. You install it on your device, connect to one of 6,400+ servers in 111 countries, and your traffic is encrypted and routed through Nord's network. It's built for individuals who want privacy, streaming access, and protection on public Wi-Fi. Pricing starts at $3.39/mo on a 2-year plan.

NordLayer (formerly NordVPN Teams) is a business network security platform. It's built around team management — an admin console where IT can provision user accounts, enforce access policies, integrate with SSO providers (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace), create dedicated gateways for your office or cloud, and generate audit logs. Pricing starts at $9/user/mo with a 5-user minimum.

Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose NordVPN if:

  • You're an individual, freelancer, or household

  • You need strong privacy protection on public Wi-Fi
  • Budget matters — $3.39/mo vs $9+/user/mo for NordLayer

Choose NordLayer if:

  • You manage a team of 5 or more people
  • You need centralized user provisioning and de-provisioning
  • Your company uses Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace SSO
  • You need dedicated egress IPs for IP allowlisting with SaaS tools
  • Compliance or audit requirements demand access logs
  • You're replacing a traditional office VPN with cloud-based ZTNA

The bottom line: NordVPN is not a business tool, and NordLayer is not a consumer product. They happen to share a parent company and underlying infrastructure, but they solve different problems. Trying to use NordVPN as a company VPN (shared accounts, no centralized management) is a security anti-pattern. And paying NordLayer rates for a personal VPN is simply expensive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NordLayer the same as NordVPN?
No — they are different products from the same company (Nord Security). NordVPN is a consumer VPN for individuals from $3.39/mo. NordLayer is a business network security platform for teams from $9/user/mo with a 5-user minimum. NordLayer includes SSO, centralized admin, ZTNA, and audit logs that NordVPN does not offer.
Which is better for business — NordLayer or NordVPN?
NordLayer is designed specifically for businesses. It offers SSO integration (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace), centralized team access controls, dedicated cloud gateways, and Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA). For teams of 5 or more with real IT and compliance needs, NordLayer is the correct choice. NordVPN Business exists as a workaround for very small teams but lacks enterprise features.
How much does NordLayer cost vs NordVPN?
NordLayer starts at $9/user/mo (Lite, annual) with a 5-user minimum — so minimum $45/mo. NordVPN starts at $3.39/mo per person on a 2-year plan. For a 10-person team: NordLayer Core costs $110/mo; 10 individual NordVPN accounts would cost $33.90/mo — but NordLayer adds enterprise management features that NordVPN cannot replicate.
Can I use NordVPN for my business instead of NordLayer?
For very small teams (2-4 people), NordVPN can work as a stopgap — but you lose centralized management, SSO, audit logs, and dedicated IP options. If your company requires compliance reporting, dedicated egress IPs, or SSO integration, NordLayer is the correct choice. NordVPN Business (shared credentials) is a workaround, not an enterprise solution.
Does NordLayer include the same servers as NordVPN?
NordLayer runs on Nord Security's shared infrastructure, but the product is different. NordLayer focuses on dedicated gateways (shared or private) for business access, not the consumer server pool of 6,400+ servers optimised for streaming. If you need streaming access to international content, NordVPN is the better choice.
