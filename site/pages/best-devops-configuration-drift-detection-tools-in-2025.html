Frequently Asked Questions

Why is regex-based key filtering important in drift detection?

It allows teams to exclude dynamic or environment-specific keys (like datacenter-specific endpoints) using patterns, reducing noise in drift reports and improving signal accuracy.

Can I use these tools to compare .env files safely in CI?

Yes, StackDiff is specifically designed for this with its `--ignore-values` flag, ensuring secrets aren't exposed during comparison. Consul and Datadog require additional safeguards.
