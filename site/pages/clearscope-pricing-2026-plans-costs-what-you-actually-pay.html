Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Clearscope cost per month?

Clearscope starts at $179/month for the Starter plan (billed annually). The Pro plan is $359/month. Monthly billing is available at a slightly higher rate (~10% premium).

Does Clearscope have a free plan?

Clearscope does not offer a permanent free plan, but it provides a 14-day free trial with full access to all features — no credit card required.

Is Clearscope worth the price?

For SEO-driven content teams, yes. Clients typically see 3–6x ROI within 3–6 months through higher rankings and increased organic traffic. However, for general content creators without SEO goals, the investment may not pay off.
