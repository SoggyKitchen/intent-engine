Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Tresorit?

Snyk offers a robust free tier for developer security, though it doesn't replace file encryption. For general security, NordLayer also provides a free plan with limited seats.

Why are people switching from Tresorit?

Users are leaving Tresorit due to high pricing, limited collaboration features, and a shift toward broader security platforms that integrate access, identity, and threat protection beyond file encryption.

How hard is it to migrate from Tresorit?

Migration difficulty varies by tool. For platforms like NordLayer and 1Password, it's easy with API support and import tools. Enterprise systems like Okta and CrowdStrike require planning, but offer professional services to assist.
