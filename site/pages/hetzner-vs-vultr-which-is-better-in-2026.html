Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hetzner better than Vultr?

Yes, Hetzner offers higher-performance servers and more robust security features, making it a better choice for businesses requiring high uptime and security.

Which is cheaper, Hetzner or Vultr?

Vultr is generally cheaper than Hetzner, with their 'Vultr High Frequency' plan starting at $2.50/month compared to Hetzner's 'CX11' plan starting at $5.99/month.

Can I switch from Vultr to Hetzner?

Yes, switching from Vultr to Hetzner is relatively straightforward, and Hetzner offers a free trial to help you get started.

Does Hetzner or Vultr have a free plan?

No, neither Hetzner nor Vultr offers a free plan. However, both providers offer a free trial to help you get started.

Which has better customer support, Hetzner or Vultr?

Hetzner offers 24/7 customer support, while Vultr offers 24/5 customer support. However, both providers have a reputation for providing excellent customer support.

Is Hetzner or Vultr better for small teams?

Vultr is a better option for small teams due to its more affordable pricing and flexible infrastructure options.

Does Hetzner integrate with Vultr?

No, Hetzner and Vultr do not integrate directly. However, both providers offer APIs and other integration options to help you connect your infrastructure.

Which tool has more features, Hetzner or Vultr?

Hetzner offers more features than Vultr, including high-performance servers, robust security features, and a wider range of integrations.

Feature Deep Dive

Hetzner's high-performance servers are powered by Intel Xeon processors and offer up to 64 GB of RAM. Their robust security features include automatic backups, firewalls, and intrusion detection. In contrast, Vultr's infrastructure is designed for flexibility and scalability, with a wide range of datacenter locations and a user-friendly interface.

Pricing Breakdown

Hetzner's pricing plans start at $5.99/month for their 'CX11' plan, which includes 1 vCPU, 2 GB RAM, and 40 GB SSD. Vultr's pricing plans start at $2.50/month for their 'Vultr High Frequency' plan, which includes 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, and 30 GB SSD.

Who Should Use Hetzner

Hetzner is ideal for businesses requiring high-performance servers, robust security features, and excellent customer support. This includes e-commerce sites, gaming companies, and other businesses that require high uptime and security.

Who Should Use Vultr

Vultr is ideal for businesses requiring a flexible cloud infrastructure solution with a wide range of datacenter locations. This includes small teams, startups, and businesses that require scalability and flexibility.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Vultr to Hetzner is relatively straightforward, and Hetzner offers a free trial to help you get started. However, migrating your infrastructure can take some time, and it's recommended to plan ahead and test your setup before going live.
