Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Moz Pro?

While most robust tools are paid, Mangools offers a generous free plan with limited searches, making it the best free-tier alternative for light SEO tasks.

Why are people switching from Moz Pro?

Users are leaving Moz Pro due to stagnant feature updates, less accurate keyword volume data, and higher pricing compared to more comprehensive tools like Ahrefs and Semrush.

How hard is it to migrate from Moz Pro?

Migration is generally easy—most alternatives support CSV imports for keywords and domains. Tools like Ahrefs and SE Ranking offer direct competitor analysis to quickly benchmark your site against top rankings.
