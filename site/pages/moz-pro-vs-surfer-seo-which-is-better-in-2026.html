Frequently Asked Questions

Is Moz Pro better than Surfer SEO?

It depends on your needs. Moz Pro is better for technical SEO, backlink analysis, and enterprise reporting. Surfer SEO excels in content creation and on-page optimization. For holistic SEO strategy, Moz Pro is generally the stronger platform.

Which is cheaper, Moz Pro or Surfer SEO?

Surfer SEO's entry point is slightly cheaper at $89/month vs. Moz Pro's $99/month. However, Moz offers more features at each tier, making it more cost-effective for teams needing advanced SEO capabilities.

Can I switch from Surfer SEO to Moz Pro?

Yes, switching is straightforward. Moz Pro supports CSV imports for keywords and rankings, and you can manually transfer content strategies. Expect a 1–2 week onboarding period for full team adoption.

Does Moz Pro or Surfer SEO have a free plan?

Neither offers a permanent free plan. Moz Pro provides a 30-day free trial; Surfer SEO offers a 7-day trial. Both require credit card details to start.

Which has better customer support, Moz Pro or Surfer SEO?

Moz Pro offers superior support with 24/7 chat, dedicated resources, and a mature help center. Surfer SEO support is responsive during business hours but lacks after-hours availability.

Is Moz Pro or Surfer SEO better for small teams?

Small teams focused on content may prefer Surfer SEO for its ease of use and lower entry cost. However, teams planning to scale SEO operations should invest in Moz Pro for its long-term scalability and depth.

Does Moz Pro integrate with Surfer SEO?

There is no native integration between Moz Pro and Surfer SEO. However, both support CSV exports and API access, allowing for partial data synchronization with custom workflows or third-party tools like Zapier.

Which tool has more features, Moz Pro or Surfer SEO?

Moz Pro has more features overall, including site audits, rank tracking, backlink analysis, and domain authority. Surfer SEO focuses on content optimization with fewer but highly specialized tools like the Content Editor and SERP Analyzer.

Feature Deep Dive

Moz Pro offers robust tools like Site Crawl for technical audits, Keyword Explorer for research, and Link Explorer for backlink analysis—features critical for enterprise SEO. Surfer SEO counters with its real-time Content Editor, which analyzes top SERP pages and recommends word count, keyword placement, and heading structure. While Surfer excels in content guidance, it lacks Moz’s depth in technical and off-page SEO. Moz also provides Domain Authority (DA), a widely adopted trust metric absent in Surfer. For teams needing both on-page and off-page insights, Moz Pro delivers a more complete toolkit.

Pricing Breakdown

Moz Pro starts at $99/month (Starter) for 100 keywords and one site, scaling to $599/month (Large) for 1,000 keywords and advanced reporting. Surfer SEO starts at $89/month (Basic) for 10 documents and 5 projects, rising to $299/month (Business) for 90 documents and priority support. While Surfer appears cheaper upfront, Moz Pro includes rank tracking, site audits, and backlink data at all tiers—features Surfer either lacks or offers minimally. For teams needing full SEO coverage, Moz Pro offers better value despite the higher cost.

Who Should Use Moz Pro

Moz Pro is ideal for mid-sized to large businesses, agencies, and in-house SEO teams managing complex websites. It suits organizations that prioritize technical SEO, link building, and data-driven reporting. Teams with budgets over $200/month and a need for domain authority insights, site crawls, and competitive backlink analysis will benefit most from Moz Pro’s comprehensive suite.

Who Should Use Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO is best for content marketers, copywriters, and SEO-focused writers who want to create high-ranking content quickly. It’s ideal for startups and small teams prioritizing on-page optimization over technical SEO. With its intuitive editor and SERP reverse-engineering, Surfer is perfect for teams that produce content at scale but don’t need advanced backlink or site audit tools.

Migration & Setup

Migrating from Surfer SEO to Moz Pro is manageable but requires manual effort—neither tool offers direct import/export between platforms. Moz Pro supports CSV uploads for keywords and rankings, while Surfer allows document exports in Markdown or HTML. Onboarding typically takes 1–2 weeks, including team training and dashboard setup. Moz’s steeper learning curve is offset by extensive documentation and support resources.
