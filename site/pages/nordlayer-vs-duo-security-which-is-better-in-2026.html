NordLayer offers advanced threat protection, secure access, and robust network security features, including a firewall, VPN, and intrusion detection. Duo Security, on the other hand, provides robust user authentication and access management features, including multi-factor authentication and single sign-on. While both platforms offer a similar feature set, NordLayer's features are more flexible and customizable, making it a better choice for most businesses.
