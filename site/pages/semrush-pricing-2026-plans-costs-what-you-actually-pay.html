Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Semrush cost per month?

Semrush starts at $139.95/month for the Starter plan when billed annually. The Pro plan is $249.95/month, and the Business plan is $599.95/month. Monthly billing raises each tier by about 20%.

Does Semrush have a free plan?

Yes, Semrush offers a free account with limited access: 10 free searches per day, basic keyword data, and access to some site audit tools. No credit card required.

Is Semrush worth the price?

For most B2B marketing teams, yes — especially agencies and enterprises. The ROI comes from improved SEO rankings, faster content production, and competitive insights. However, smaller teams should evaluate usage needs carefully or consider more focused, lower-cost tools.
