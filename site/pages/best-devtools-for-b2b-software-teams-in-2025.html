Frequently Asked Questions

Which tool is best for compliance-heavy environments?

Agent Bridge is purpose-built for regulated environments with its `externalWrite:false` schema and audit trail features, making it ideal for legal and security reviews during M&A or audits.

Can WorldLoops replace Agent Bridge?

WorldLoops offers similar signal patterns but lacks enterprise-grade monitoring and support. It’s better suited for prototyping than production-critical systems.

Is Clonezilla SE free to use in enterprise?

Yes, Clonezilla SE is free under the GPL license and widely used in enterprise settings, though it requires in-house expertise to deploy and maintain.
