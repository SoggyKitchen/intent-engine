Frequently Asked Questions

Is NordLayer worth it in 2026?

Yes, for businesses that prioritize security, compliance, and scalability. With rising cyber threats and hybrid work, NordLayer’s zero-trust model and centralized management make it a future-proof investment—especially for teams over 10 users.

What are the main complaints about NordLayer?

Users report that pricing can add up quickly for small teams, and while the dashboard is intuitive, some advanced networking features require deeper IT knowledge. A few have noted slower customer support responses during onboarding.

Who should NOT use NordLayer?

Very small teams (under 5 users) with simple remote access needs or startups with tight budgets should consider lighter alternatives like Tailscale. NordLayer’s full value shines in structured, security-conscious environments—not minimal setups.
