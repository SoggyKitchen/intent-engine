Bottom Line: Solid Starter Host With a Renewal Price Trap

HostPapa is a Canadian eco-friendly web host built for small businesses and beginners. It gets a lot right: 24/7 phone support, a free one-on-one onboarding call ("Papa's Experts"), green renewable energy hosting, and a solid Business plan with unlimited websites and SSD storage. The intro prices are genuinely competitive.

The problem? Renewal rates jump 2–3× when your initial term ends. The Starter plan goes from $3.95/mo to $10.99/mo. Business Pro from $9.95/mo to $24.99/mo. If you're signing up for a 1-year plan and expecting that price to hold, you'll be surprised at renewal.

Who it's for: Small businesses and bloggers who want phone support, green credentials, and a free domain — and are prepared to either renew at the higher rate or migrate. Not for high-traffic sites, developers who need staging/Git, or anyone expecting consistent pricing.
