Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Asana cost per month?

Asana starts with a free plan for teams up to 15 users. The Premium plan is $10.99/user/month (billed annually), and the Business plan is $24.99/user/month (billed annually). Enterprise pricing is custom and typically starts at 50+ users with negotiated annual contracts.

Does Asana have a free plan?

Yes, Asana offers a free plan that supports up to 15 users and includes core features like task assignments, due dates, project timelines, and basic reporting. It’s ideal for small teams or startups testing the platform.

Is Asana worth the price?

For mid-sized and enterprise teams that value ease of use, scalability, and integration depth, Asana delivers strong ROI. The Business plan justifies its cost with advanced reporting, custom fields, and portfolio management. However, smaller teams may find better value in alternatives like ClickUp, especially if they need more features at a lower cost.
