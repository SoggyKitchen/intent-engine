Frequently Asked Questions

Does Salesforce have a free trial?

Yes — 30-day free trial. 30-day free trial.

Is a credit card required for the Salesforce free trial?

It depends on the plan. For most Salesforce free tiers, no credit card is required. Paid trials may require a card but you won't be charged until the trial ends.

What happens after the Salesforce free trial ends?

After the trial, your account moves to the free tier (if available) or pauses until you add payment. You won't lose your data. Salesforce will email you before the trial expires.

Can I extend the Salesforce free trial?

Yes — contact Salesforce support before your trial expires and ask for an extension. Most companies grant 7-14 extra days, especially if you explain you need more time to evaluate with your team.

How do I cancel Salesforce during the trial?

Cancel via your account settings before the trial ends to avoid charges. Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Salesforce typically confirms cancellation by email.

Is the Salesforce free tier good enough?

30-day free trial. For solo users or very small teams, the free tier may be sufficient. For professional use, paid plans unlock features like advanced reporting, team collaboration, and API access.

What the Salesforce Free Trial Includes

The Salesforce free trial gives full access to all features — including features on the paid plan — for 30-day free trial. This is your chance to test the tool with real data, not a demo.

How to Get the Most From Your Trial

Week 1 checklist: Set up your real workflow (not a test project), invite your whole team, connect your integrations, run a real project end-to-end. By Day 7 you should know if it fits.

After the Trial: Salesforce Pricing

Salesforce paid plans start at $25/mo. Annual billing saves ~20%. If you're on the fence, ask sales for a 30-day extension — most will say yes.

Alternatives If the Trial Doesn't Convert You

If Salesforce isn't the right fit, the top alternatives are: HubSpot CRM, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM. All offer free trials so you can compare directly.
