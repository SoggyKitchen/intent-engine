What's Included in Elementor Free (The Good Part)

Elementor Free is a capable page builder for standard WordPress pages. Here's what you actually get:

  • ✓ Drag-and-drop visual editor — live frontend editing, no page refresh needed
  • ✓ 40+ widgets — Heading, Image, Button, Text Editor, Video, Divider, Icon Box, Counter, Progress Bar, Tabs, Accordion, etc.
  • ✓ Responsive design controls — separate settings for desktop, tablet, mobile
  • ✓ 30+ free templates — full page templates you can import and customise
  • ✓ Revision history — undo changes up to a set number of revisions
  • ✓ Works with any theme — compatible with most WordPress themes
  • ✓ No time limit, no credit card — genuinely free forever via WordPress.org

The 7 Features Locked Behind Elementor Pro

These are the hard stops where Elementor Free won't be enough:

  • ✗ Popup Builder — you can't create exit-intent popups, slide-ins, or full-screen overlays without Pro. This is the #1 reason small businesses upgrade.
  • ✗ Theme Builder — custom headers, footers, single post templates, archive pages all require Pro. On Free, your header/footer is controlled by your theme, not Elementor.
  • ✗ WooCommerce Builder — custom product pages, cart, checkout, and My Account page layouts require Pro. Free gives you a basic shop with your theme's WooCommerce template.
  • ✗ Form Builder — multi-step forms, integrations with email platforms (Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, etc.), and file upload fields require Pro.
  • ✗ 100+ Pro widgets — Countdown Timer, Price Table, Flip Box, Hotspot, Motion Effects, Animated Headline, Lottie, etc. are all Pro-only.
  • ✗ Dynamic content — pulling ACF field values, post meta, or author data into your design requires Pro.
  • ✗ Custom CSS per element — the Free plan has a global custom CSS option in Customizer, but per-widget/section CSS requires Pro.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Elementor have a free plan in 2026?

Yes. Elementor Free is available on WordPress.org as a permanently free plugin with 5M+ active installs. No credit card, no expiry, no trial. The free version includes 40+ widgets, drag-and-drop editing, and 30+ templates.

What is NOT included in Elementor Free?

The 7 key Pro-only features are: Popup Builder, Theme Builder (header/footer/archive), WooCommerce Builder, Form Builder with email integrations, 100+ advanced Pro widgets, dynamic content, and custom CSS per element. All require Elementor Pro.

How much does Elementor Pro cost?

Elementor Pro starts at approximately $59/year for a single-site licence (Essential plan) as of mid-2025. Plans for 3 sites, 25 sites, and 1,000 sites are also available at higher price points. Pricing changes — verify the current rate before purchasing via the link above.

Can I build a full website with Elementor Free?

Yes, for simple sites. You can build page content with Elementor Free, but your header, footer, and post/archive templates will still come from your WordPress theme. For a fully custom design including headers, footers, and 404 pages, you need Theme Builder, which is Pro-only.

Is Elementor Free good enough for a business website?

For basic informational business sites, yes. The moment you need lead capture (popups or forms), email integration, or WooCommerce customisation, you'll need Pro. Most marketing-focused businesses find they need Pro within 6 months of using the free version.
