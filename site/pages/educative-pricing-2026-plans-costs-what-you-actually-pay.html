Verdict

Is Educative Worth the Price?

Short answer: Yes, if you have a specific technical interview or cloud cert coming up. The Grokking the Coding Interview and Grokking the System Design Interview courses alone justify the annual price for most mid-level and senior developers. The in-browser coding environments mean you never need to set up a local environment — useful for rapid sprint studying.

When to pick Monthly: If you want 2–3 months of focused study before a specific interview cycle and plan to cancel after. $50–75 total beats $149/year if your prep window is short.

When to look elsewhere: If you prefer video-based learning, Udemy courses are cheaper per-topic. If you want brute-force practice problems, LeetCode Premium is the standard. Educative wins on structured, text-based, concept-first learning.

FAQ

Educative Pricing — Frequently Asked

How much does Educative cost?
Educative offers a free tier, an Individual plan around $20–25/month (or ~$149/year billed annually), and Teams at custom pricing. Verify at educative.io/pricing — prices update regularly.
Does Educative have a free plan?
Yes. The free tier gives access to select free courses and previews. Most advanced courses — including the Grokking series — require a paid subscription.
Is Educative worth it for interview prep?
For FAANG and top-tech interview prep, Educative is highly rated. The Grokking the Coding Interview and Grokking the System Design Interview courses are industry standards. At ~$149/year, ROI is strong if you land even one better-compensated role.
What is the cheapest way to use Educative?
Annual billing gives the biggest discount (typically 30–40% off monthly). Educative also runs promotions regularly — check around Black Friday and New Year. A 7 or 14-day free trial is often available.
How does Educative compare to LeetCode?
LeetCode focuses on coding challenges and contest problems. Educative focuses on structured learning paths with explanations. Many developers use both: Educative to learn patterns, LeetCode to practice. LeetCode Premium runs ~$35/month; Educative Individual ~$20–25/month.
Can I cancel Educative anytime?
Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next billing cycle. Annual plans are typically non-refundable after the trial period closes. Set a calendar reminder before renewal.
