Security: Are They Actually Safe?

Both NordPass and 1Password are zero-knowledge password managers — your master password never leaves your device, and the company cannot access your vault even if compelled. Both have passed independent security audits from Cure53.

NordPass: XChaCha20 encryption

NordPass uses XChaCha20-Poly1305, a newer authenticated encryption algorithm recommended for software implementations where AES hardware acceleration isn't always available. It's considered at least as secure as AES-256. Cure53 audited the NordPass apps in 2023 and found no critical vulnerabilities.

1Password: AES-256-GCM + Secret Key

1Password uses AES-256-GCM encryption and adds a 128-bit Secret Key — a locally-generated key that combines with your master password to create the actual encryption key. This means even if your master password is stolen, attackers still need the Secret Key to access your vault. It's an extra layer NordPass doesn't have.

Security verdict: Both are excellent. 1Password's Secret Key gives it a marginal edge for users who worry about master password phishing. For most people, both are more than secure enough.

User Experience

This is where the difference is most noticeable in daily use.

NordPass has a clean, modern interface. Autofill works reliably on desktop and mobile. The browser extension is lightweight and fast. Some users find the organisation less flexible — you get folders but no custom vaults or tags.

1Password has the most polished password manager UX available. Vaults are highly customisable, the Quick Access feature (⌘ + Shift + Space on Mac) is genuinely faster than any competitor, and Watchtower proactively warns you about compromised, weak, or reused passwords. The iOS and macOS apps feel native and refined.

UX verdict: 1Password is better here, meaningfully so. If you're deep in the Apple ecosystem, the gap is even wider.

Travel Mode (1Password Only)

1Password's Travel Mode lets you hide specific vaults when crossing international borders — so if a border agent demands access to your phone, those vaults simply don't exist on the device. When you're through customs, you restore everything remotely.

NordPass has no equivalent feature. For security professionals, journalists, and frequent international travellers, this is a significant differentiator in 1Password's favour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NordPass better than 1Password?

NordPass wins on price ($1.99/mo vs $2.99/mo) and gives you a free tier. 1Password wins on UX, Travel Mode, and team features. Budget-conscious individuals should pick NordPass; teams and Apple power users should pick 1Password.

What is the price difference between NordPass and 1Password?

As of August 2026: NordPass Premium is $1.99/mo billed annually ($23.88/yr). 1Password Individual is $2.99/mo billed annually ($35.88/yr) — NordPass saves you $12/year. For families: both cost $4.99/mo, but NordPass includes 6 seats vs 1Password's 5.

Does NordPass have a free plan?

Yes. NordPass Free stores unlimited passwords but limits you to one active device at a time — switching devices requires logging out of the previous one. 1Password has no free plan; it offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Which is safer — NordPass or 1Password?

Both are zero-knowledge and independently audited. NordPass uses XChaCha20; 1Password uses AES-256 plus a Secret Key. In practice, both are equally safe for consumers. 1Password's Secret Key gives a marginal edge against master password phishing attacks.

Does 1Password work better for teams?

Yes. 1Password Business ($7.99/user/mo) has deeper admin controls, custom vaults, activity logs, and integrations with Azure AD, Okta, and Rippling. NordPass Business ($4.99/user/mo) covers the basics at lower cost but has fewer enterprise features. For teams over 20, 1Password is the better call.

Final Verdict

NordPass is the better pick for price-conscious individuals and families. At $1.99/mo it's 33% cheaper than 1Password, still passes independent security audits, and comes with a genuinely usable free tier. The Nord brand trust (same company as NordVPN) and XChaCha20 encryption are bonuses.

1Password is the better pick for teams, power users, and anyone deep in the Apple ecosystem. Travel Mode, the Secret Key, Watchtower, and the polished UX justify the extra dollar per month for people who will actually use those features.

If you're still deciding, start with NordPass Free — it costs nothing and lets you see if the UI works for you before committing to a paid plan.
