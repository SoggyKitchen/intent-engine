What to Look for in a Shopify Email Marketing App

Shopify Sync Depth

Native Shopify App Store apps sync orders, products, and customer segments automatically. Plugin-based tools require setup and may have data sync delays.

Revenue Attribution

The best tools (Klaviyo, Omnisend) show exactly how much revenue each email or flow generates. Essential for proving ROI and optimising sequences.

Pre-Built Ecommerce Flows

Look for ready-made flows: abandoned cart, welcome series, post-purchase, browse abandonment, and win-back. These generate revenue on day one without manual setup.

List Growth & Segmentation

Segment by purchase history, product category, order value, and customer lifetime value. Generic blasts underperform targeted sequences by 3–5x on open rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best email marketing app for Shopify in 2026?

Klaviyo is the best overall email marketing app for Shopify in 2026. It has the deepest native Shopify integration, revenue attribution per email, and the widest range of pre-built ecommerce automation flows. GetResponse is the best value pick for stores that also want landing pages and webinars in the same platform.

Does Shopify have built-in email marketing?

Yes — Shopify Email is included in all Shopify plans and is free up to 10,000 emails per month. It covers basic campaigns and automation, but lacks advanced segmentation, A/B testing, and revenue analytics. Most serious stores upgrade to a dedicated tool once their list and revenue grow.

Which Shopify email app has the best free plan?

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) has the most generous free plan: 300 emails per day to unlimited contacts with no credit card required. For ecommerce-specific features, Klaviyo and Omnisend both offer free tiers up to 250 contacts that include abandoned cart flows and order-based automation.

Is GetResponse good for Shopify?

Yes — GetResponse connects to Shopify via an official integration that syncs customers, orders, and abandoned carts. It stands out for stores that want email marketing plus landing pages, webinar hosting, and marketing funnels in one platform. The Email Marketing plan starts from $15.58/month. See our GetResponse review for a full breakdown.

What is the cheapest email marketing app for Shopify?

AWeber's Plus plan starts from $12.50/month (annual) and includes Shopify integration, unlimited email sends, and landing pages. Mailchimp Essentials starts at $13/month. For completely free usage, Brevo, Klaviyo, and Omnisend all have usable free tiers for small Shopify stores under 250–500 contacts.
