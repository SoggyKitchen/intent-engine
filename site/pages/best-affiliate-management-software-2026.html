Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best affiliate management software in 2026?

impact.com is the most complete affiliate and partner management platform in 2026, best for enterprise brands running complex programs with multiple partner types. PartnerStack leads for B2B SaaS. For smaller teams or ecommerce, Tapfiliate and Refersion offer strong value at lower price points.

How much does affiliate management software cost?

Pricing varies widely. Budget options like FirstPromoter start from around $49/month. Mid-tier platforms like Tapfiliate start around $89/month. Enterprise platforms like impact.com and PartnerStack are typically priced based on program size and revenue, expect $500+/month at scale.

What is the difference between impact.com and PartnerStack?

impact.com is a full partnership management platform covering affiliates, influencers, brand partnerships, and B2B referrals, best for large brands managing diverse partner types. PartnerStack focuses specifically on B2B SaaS partner programs (resellers, agencies, referral partners) and has a built-in marketplace of SaaS-focused affiliates.

Can small businesses use affiliate tracking software?

Yes. FirstPromoter (~$49/month) and Tapfiliate (~$89/month) are both designed for smaller SaaS or ecommerce businesses. Refersion integrates directly with Shopify and is popular with smaller ecommerce brands. Most platforms offer a free trial so you can test before committing.
