Ramp Review

Ramp is a corporate card and spend management platform designed for fast-growing startups and mid-sized businesses. It helps finance teams automate expenses, track budgets in real time, and earn cashback on purchases.

Pros

  • Real-time expense tracking with automated categorization
  • Up to 1.5% uncapped cashback on all purchases
  • No fees on cards or ACH transfers

Cons

  • Higher-tier features require negotiation for access
  • Limited international support outside the U.S.

Pricing: Ramp offers four main tiers: Starter (free), Core ($0/month), Plus ($15/month per user), and Enterprise (custom pricing). The Core plan is most popular and includes core spend management, virtual cards, and 1.5% cashback. Plus adds advanced controls, custom approval workflows, and dedicated support.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Ramp cost per month?

Ramp’s Core plan is free. The Plus plan costs $15 per user per month and includes advanced controls and priority support. Most teams use the free tier effectively, while larger organizations opt for Plus or negotiate custom Enterprise pricing.

Does Ramp have a free plan?

Yes. Ramp’s Starter and Core plans are free and include unlimited virtual and physical cards, real-time expense tracking, automated receipt matching, and 1.5% uncapped cashback on all purchases.

Is Ramp worth the price?

For most growing businesses, yes. The combination of zero fees, high cashback, and powerful automation delivers strong ROI. Teams that centralize software, travel, and vendor spend on Ramp often earn back thousands annually in rebates alone — making even the Plus plan a smart investment.
