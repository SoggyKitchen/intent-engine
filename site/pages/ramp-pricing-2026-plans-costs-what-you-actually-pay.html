Ramp Review

Ramp is a spend management platform designed for businesses to streamline their financial operations, offering features such as automated expense tracking, invoice management, and real-time budgeting insights.

Pros

  • Seamless integration with existing accounting systems
  • Robust security and compliance features

Cons

  • Steep learning curve for non-technical users
  • Customization options can be overwhelming

Pricing: Ramp offers three main plans: a free plan with limited features, the 'Pro' plan starting at $120 per month, and an 'Enterprise' plan with custom pricing based on the organization's size and needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Ramp cost per month?

Ramp's pricing starts with a free plan, then $120 per month for the Pro plan, and custom pricing for the Enterprise plan.

Does Ramp have a free plan?

Yes, Ramp offers a free plan with limited features, suitable for small businesses or those just starting out with spend management.

Is Ramp worth the price?

Ramp is worth the investment for businesses that can utilize its advanced features to streamline financial operations and reduce costs. However, for smaller businesses or those on a tight budget, the cost may outweigh the benefits.
