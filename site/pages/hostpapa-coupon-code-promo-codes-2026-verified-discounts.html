Common questions

HostPapa coupon FAQ

Does HostPapa have a coupon code?

HostPapa does not typically issue public coupon codes. Their best discount is the intro pricing available through our link, from $3.95/mo (Starter) or $5.95/mo (Business), which is up to 62% off the regular renewal rate.

What is HostPapa's cheapest plan?

The Starter plan starts at $3.95/mo (intro rate, annual billing), but it limits you to 2 websites and 100 GB SSD. For most small businesses, the Business plan at $5.95/mo intro is better value: unlimited websites, unlimited SSD, and better performance optimization.

Does HostPapa include a free domain?

Yes, HostPapa includes a free domain registration for the first year with any annual or multi-year plan. After year 1, the domain renews at the standard rate ($15–20/yr). Domain privacy (WHOIS protection) is a paid add-on at approximately $10/yr.

Is there a HostPapa free trial?

HostPapa does not offer a traditional free trial. However, they provide a 30-day money-back guarantee on most shared hosting plans, so you can test the service risk-free for the first month and request a full refund if unsatisfied.

What happens to pricing at renewal?

HostPapa's promotional introductory pricing applies only for your initial term. Renewal rates are significantly higher: Starter renews at $10.99/mo, Business at $14.99/mo, Business Pro at $24.99/mo. This renewal price hike is the #1 complaint, plan for it before you commit.

How is HostPapa vs Bluehost on price?

HostPapa and Bluehost are closely matched on intro pricing. HostPapa differentiates with 24/7 phone support and a free one-on-one onboarding session (Papa's Experts), particularly valuable for non-technical users. HostPapa also runs on 100% renewable energy. For WordPress hosting, Bluehost has deeper official integration; for small business support quality, HostPapa edges ahead.
