The 1-Device Limit: What It Really Means Day-to-Day

This is the most misunderstood aspect of NordPass free. "One device at a time" does not mean you can only install it on one device — you can install NordPass on your phone, laptop, and browser extension simultaneously.

What it means: only one of those devices can have an active login session. The moment you open NordPass on a second device and use it, the first device is automatically logged out and will require you to log back in.

Who this affects most: Anyone who regularly switches between a work laptop and a phone. If you're looking up a password on your laptop while your phone's NordPass is still "open," you'll be prompted to re-authenticate on the laptop.

Who it doesn't affect: Someone who primarily uses NordPass on one device (e.g., just the browser extension on one laptop) will rarely notice the limit.

Honest verdict: The 1-device limit is a genuine frustration for multi-device users. Bitwarden's free plan removes this limit entirely. If cost is your deciding factor, Bitwarden free is a stronger free tier. If you prefer NordPass's UI, import tools, or Nord ecosystem (NordVPN, NordPass together), the premium upgrade is worth it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NordPass have a free plan in 2026?

Yes. NordPass has a permanent free plan with no time limit. It includes unlimited passwords, passkeys, and secure notes. The key restriction is 1 active device at a time — you cannot be logged in on multiple devices simultaneously.

What is NOT included in NordPass free?

NordPass free does not include: data breach scanner, emergency access, password and secure note sharing, or email masking. These all require a paid plan. The other major missing feature is true multi-device sync — free users can only have one active session at a time.

Can I use NordPass free on multiple devices?

You can install NordPass on multiple devices, but only one can be active at a time. If you open NordPass on your phone, your laptop session is automatically ended. This is the single biggest practical limitation of the free plan for multi-device users.

Is Bitwarden better than NordPass free?

For the free tier, Bitwarden is more generous: unlimited device sync with no active-session limit, plus emergency access and basic sharing on free. NordPass free is strong for single-device users and has a more polished interface with passkey support. For teams or multi-device personal use, Bitwarden free wins on value.
