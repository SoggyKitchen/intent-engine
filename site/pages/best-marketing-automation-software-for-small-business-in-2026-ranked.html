Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best marketing automation software for small business?

HubSpot is the best overall marketing automation software for small businesses due to its free CRM, intuitive interface, and scalable features. ActiveCampaign is a close second for teams needing deeper automation.

How much does marketing automation software cost for small business?

Most small businesses can expect to pay between $13 and $50 per month for core marketing automation features. Costs increase with contact volume, automation depth, and added functionality like CRM or ads.

Is there a free marketing automation tool for small business?

Yes, both Brevo and ConvertKit offer free plans that include email marketing and basic automation. HubSpot also offers a free CRM with limited marketing tools, ideal for early-stage businesses.
