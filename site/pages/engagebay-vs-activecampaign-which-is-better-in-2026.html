Where EngageBay Wins

Best for: Small teams (under 20 people) that need CRM + email + live chat + support in one app without paying for four separate tools.

All-in-one value at $12/user. EngageBay is the only tool at this price that genuinely bundles a CRM, email marketing, live chat, appointment scheduling, and a helpdesk ticketing system. The nearest equivalent at ActiveCampaign would require Plus ($49/mo) plus a separate live chat tool (Intercom, Crisp, etc.) — easily $100+/mo for a 3-person team.

Better free tier. EngageBay's free plan includes 250 contacts, 1,000 branded emails per month, the full CRM, live chat, and helpdesk. ActiveCampaign has no free plan — just a 14-day trial. For bootstrapped founders, EngageBay free is a real starting point.

Per-user pricing is more predictable. EngageBay bills per seat regardless of contact count. ActiveCampaign bills by contact count, which creates unexpected cost jumps as your list grows. A 10,000-contact list at ActiveCampaign Plus costs significantly more than the base $49/mo.

Easier onboarding. The UI is straightforward and the learning curve is lower. Teams switching from a spreadsheet or a simple CRM will feel at home within a day.

How to Choose: The One-Question Framework

Is email automation your primary revenue driver?

Yes → ActiveCampaign. If you're running sequences for ecommerce, SaaS trial conversions, or complex lead nurturing — and your email automation ROI is measurable — ActiveCampaign's superior automation will pay for the price difference. The gap in automation sophistication is real and material.

No → EngageBay. If email is one channel among several, and you need CRM + support + chat + marketing in one app without juggling 4 subscriptions, EngageBay wins on value and simplicity. The $12/user all-in price is genuinely hard to beat.

Neither fits? Consider HubSpot Free (generous CRM, but email limits) or Zoho CRM (deeper sales CRM, separate marketing add-on).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay better than ActiveCampaign?
EngageBay (8.4/10) wins on value — it bundles CRM, email, live chat, and helpdesk from $11.99/user/mo. ActiveCampaign (8.7/10) wins on automation depth with conditional sequences, predictive sending, and 900+ integrations. Neither is universally better — the right pick depends on whether automation or all-in-one value matters more to your team.
How much does EngageBay cost vs ActiveCampaign?
EngageBay Basic is $11.99/user/mo (annual). ActiveCampaign Starter is $15/mo for 1 user (annual), Plus $49/mo for up to 3 users. For a 5-person team, EngageBay costs ~$60/mo vs ActiveCampaign Plus at $79/mo (for 3 user seats — extras cost more). EngageBay scales more predictably for small teams.
Does EngageBay have email automation?
Yes — EngageBay includes email sequences, autoresponders, and basic workflow automation on all paid plans. However, ActiveCampaign's automation builder is significantly more advanced, with conditional splits, goal-based triggers, probability branching, and ML send-time optimisation. For sophisticated sequences, ActiveCampaign is the stronger tool.
Which has a better free plan — EngageBay or ActiveCampaign?
EngageBay's free plan is more generous: 250 contacts, 1,000 branded emails/mo, full CRM, live chat, and helpdesk — all free with no time limit. ActiveCampaign has no free plan, only a 14-day trial. For startups testing the waters, EngageBay free wins clearly.
Can ActiveCampaign replace a CRM?
ActiveCampaign includes deal pipelines and contact scoring on Plus and above. It's functional for SMBs but is primarily an email automation platform. EngageBay's CRM is more fully-featured at lower cost. If CRM is your core need, EngageBay or a dedicated CRM (HubSpot Free, Zoho CRM) is a better fit.
