Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Contabo cost per month?

Contabo pricing starts from $2.99 per month for the Starter plan, $4.99 per month for the Pro plan, and $9.99 per month for the Business plan.

Does Contabo have a free plan?

Yes, Contabo offers a free plan for small businesses with limited users and storage.

Is Contabo worth the price?

Yes, Contabo is worth the price for teams that need scalable infrastructure, reliable performance, and robust security features. However, it may not be worth it for small businesses with limited needs.

Can I get a Contabo discount?

Yes, Contabo offers discounts for annual payments, startups, and non-profit organizations. You can also negotiate a custom plan with Contabo's sales team.

What's the cheapest Contabo plan?

The cheapest Contabo plan is the Starter plan, which costs $2.99 per month for 1-10 users, 10 GB storage, and 100 GB bandwidth.

Does Contabo charge per user?

Yes, Contabo charges per user, with a minimum of 1 user per plan. However, you can add more users to your plan as needed.

Are there hidden costs with Contabo?

Yes, Contabo charges extra for overages, add-ons, and implementation fees. However, these costs are clearly outlined in Contabo's pricing plans.

How does Contabo pricing compare to competitors?

Contabo pricing is competitive with other cloud hosting providers, such as Linode. However, Contabo offers more features and better performance for the same price.
