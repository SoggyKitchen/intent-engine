Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a Deel promo code for 2026?

Most Deel promo codes circulating online are expired. The real discount is annual billing, which saves 15-20% over monthly. Some affiliate links also unlock extended trials.

How do I get Deel for free?

Free for US payroll. This is the legitimate way to try Deel without paying.

Does Deel offer a student discount?

Deel offers educational discounts through their website — typically 50-75% off for verified students and nonprofits. Contact their sales team with your .edu email.

What's the cheapest Deel plan?

Deel's cheapest paid plan starts at $49/contractor/mo. On annual billing you'll pay less per month.

Does Deel have a startup program?

Yes — Deel has a startup program offering significant discounts (typically 50-90% off) for early-stage companies. Apply through their website or via accelerator partnerships like YC, Techstars.

Can I negotiate Deel pricing?

For plans over $500/mo, yes. Contact sales at end of quarter, mention competitor quotes, and ask for annual commitment in exchange for 20-30% discount. Works especially well for Salesforce, HubSpot, Datadog.

How to Get the Best Deel Price

1. Annual billing: saves 15-20%. 2. Startup program: 50-90% off if you qualify. 3. End-of-quarter negotiation: sales reps have quota pressure. 4. Extended trial via affiliate links. 5. Educational/nonprofit discount.

Deel Free Trial Guide

Free US payroll plan. During your trial, test the features your team will use daily. Most teams need 7-14 days to properly evaluate. Set up your real workflow, not a test project.

Is Deel Worth the Price?

Deel starts at $49/contractor/mo. For teams where it replaces even 2 hours/week of manual work per person, it pays for itself. Use our ROI calculator to check: https://saaspare.org/pages/saas-roi-calculator.html
