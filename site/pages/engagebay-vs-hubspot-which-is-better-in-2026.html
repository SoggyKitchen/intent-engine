Integration Ecosystem

HubSpot's integration library is one of its strongest advantages. With 1,400+ native connectors — Salesforce, Slack, Shopify, Zoom, Stripe, and hundreds more — it fits into virtually any existing tech stack without custom development.

EngageBay has ~250 integrations including Zapier (which unlocks thousands of indirect connections), Gmail, Outlook, Stripe, Twilio, and major social platforms. For most small business use cases this is sufficient. But if your stack includes niche B2B tools, verify compatibility before committing.

Ease of Use

EngageBay scores slightly higher on ease of use (8.5 vs 8.3/10) in our testing. The interface is clean and less cluttered than HubSpot's, and the learning curve is noticeably lower — a first-time CRM user can build their first email sequence within 30 minutes.

HubSpot's interface has grown complex over the years, particularly in the Marketing Hub. New users often need 1–2 weeks to feel confident. HubSpot Academy (free certification courses) helps significantly, but it's a real time investment.

Customer Support

HubSpot edges ahead on support (8.7 vs 8.1/10) with 24/7 chat and phone on Professional/Enterprise tiers and a large community forum. EngageBay offers email and live chat support, with phone support on Pro plans. Response times at EngageBay average 2–4 hours during business hours — acceptable but not exceptional.

Who Should Choose HubSpot?

  • Growth-stage companies (50+ people) with a dedicated marketing team that needs advanced automation

  • Teams deeply invested in the HubSpot ecosystem — once you've built workflows around it, switching is expensive
  • Companies that need ABM or enterprise-grade analytics that EngageBay's reporting can't match

Migration: HubSpot to EngageBay

EngageBay offers a dedicated HubSpot migration service. The process: export contacts, companies, deals, and activities from HubSpot as CSV → import directly into EngageBay via the migration wizard. Email templates and automation workflows need to be rebuilt (typically 1–3 days for a 10-person team). Custom properties and pipeline stages map cleanly.

What you'll lose: HubSpot's reporting history and any Salesforce sync. Plan a parallel-run period of 2 weeks before fully switching off HubSpot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay better than HubSpot?
For small teams under 50 people, EngageBay delivers better value — it scores 9.1/10 on value vs HubSpot's 6.2/10, and bundles CRM + email + live chat + helpdesk from $11.99/user/mo. HubSpot scores higher overall (8.9 vs 8.4/10) because of deeper automation, 1,400+ integrations, and enterprise analytics. Neither is universally "better" — the right choice depends on team size and budget.
How much does EngageBay cost vs HubSpot?
EngageBay Basic is $11.99/user/mo (annual). HubSpot Starter is $15/user/mo — but HubSpot Professional (the first tier with real automation) is $800/mo for 5 users ($160/user/mo). For a 5-person team, EngageBay Growth ($230/mo) delivers automation features comparable to HubSpot Professional ($800/mo).
Can I migrate from HubSpot to EngageBay?
Yes. EngageBay has a free HubSpot migration tool. Export contacts, companies, deals, and activities as CSV from HubSpot and import via EngageBay's migration wizard. Workflows and email sequences need to be rebuilt manually — expect 1–3 days of work for a team of 10.
Does EngageBay have a free plan?
Yes. EngageBay Free supports up to 500 contacts, 1,000 branded emails/month, live chat, and helpdesk ticketing — no time limit. It is genuinely usable for solo founders and very early-stage teams before upgrading to Basic ($11.99/user/mo annual).
What does HubSpot offer that EngageBay does not?
HubSpot's main advantages: 1,400+ native integrations (vs EngageBay's ~250), more powerful branching workflow automation, stronger ABM tools, advanced custom reporting and revenue attribution, predictive lead scoring, and a large certified consultant ecosystem. If your business relies on these capabilities, HubSpot justifies the cost.
