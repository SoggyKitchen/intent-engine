Frequently Asked Questions

Is Brevo better than Klaviyo?

It depends on your use case. Brevo is better for general marketing automation with a lower cost and broader communication tools. Klaviyo is superior for e-commerce brands needing advanced segmentation, product recommendations, and sales attribution.

Which is cheaper, Brevo or Klaviyo?

Brevo is generally cheaper, especially for startups and small businesses. Its free tier allows 300 emails/day, and paid plans start at $25/month. Klaviyo’s pricing starts at $45/month and increases significantly with contact count and message volume.

Can I switch from Klaviyo to Brevo?

Yes, you can migrate from Klaviyo to Brevo using CSV exports for contacts and segments. Brevo supports API and webhook integrations, but rebuilding complex flows may take time. Third-party tools like Zapier can assist in data transfer.

Does Brevo or Klaviyo have a free plan?

Both offer free plans. Brevo allows up to 300 emails/day and 1,000 contacts indefinitely. Klaviyo’s free plan supports up to 250 contacts and limited automation, ideal for very small lists.

Which has better customer support, Brevo or Klaviyo?

Klaviyo offers faster support with 24/7 chat and phone access on higher tiers (response under 2 hours). Brevo provides email and chat support with 24–48 hour response times, making it less ideal for urgent issues.

Is Brevo or Klaviyo better for small teams?

Brevo is better for small teams due to its intuitive UI, lower cost, and built-in CRM and chat tools. It enables lean teams to manage email, SMS, and customer service without multiple tools.

Does Brevo integrate with Klaviyo?

There is no direct two-way integration between Brevo and Klaviyo. However, you can migrate data via CSV export/import or use middleware like Zapier to sync contact lists and basic events during transition.

Which tool has more features, Brevo or Klaviyo?

Klaviyo has more depth in e-commerce-specific features like product recommendation engines, predictive analytics, and revenue reporting. Brevo offers broader communication channels (email, SMS, chat, CRM) but fewer advanced e-commerce capabilities.

Feature Deep Dive

Brevo offers robust automation workflows with visual builders, dynamic segmentation, and A/B testing. Its Marketing Studio allows multi-channel campaigns combining email and SMS, while Brevo CRM provides built-in contact management. Klaviyo counters with Flow Builder for behavior-triggered emails, Predictive Analytics for LTV forecasting, and Revenue Attribution reports linking campaigns to sales. Klaviyo’s Product Recommendations and Browse Abandonment flows are unmatched for e-commerce, while Brevo excels in general-purpose automation and customer engagement across non-retail verticals.

Pricing Breakdown

Brevo’s pricing starts with a free plan (300 emails/day, 1K contacts). The Starter plan is $25/month for 10K emails, including automation and landing pages. The Business plan ($65/month) adds CRM, advanced reporting, and API access. Klaviyo’s free plan supports 250 contacts. The Email & SMS plan starts at $45/month for 1K contacts, scaling to $500+ at 50K contacts. Klaviyo charges more as your list grows, while Brevo’s volume-based model is more predictable for growing teams.

Who Should Use Brevo

Brevo is ideal for small to mid-sized businesses across industries—SaaS, agencies, retail, and professional services—needing an affordable, all-in-one marketing platform. Teams with limited budgets and those wanting CRM, email, SMS, and chat in one place will benefit most. It’s especially strong for companies not on Shopify or those prioritizing ease of use and fast setup over deep e-commerce analytics.

Who Should Use Klaviyo

Klaviyo is best suited for e-commerce brands, especially those on Shopify, BigCommerce, or Magento, that rely on personalized, behavior-driven marketing. Mid-sized to large online stores with high order volume will leverage its predictive analytics, revenue tracking, and product recommendation features. Marketing teams focused on ROI and customer lifetime value should consider Klaviyo despite its higher cost.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Klaviyo to Brevo is feasible but requires planning. Both platforms allow CSV export of contacts and segments. Brevo’s import tool is user-friendly, and API access enables partial automation sync. Onboarding typically takes 1–2 weeks for basic setup, with additional time needed to rebuild advanced flows. Brevo’s documentation and templates help accelerate migration, though e-commerce-specific logic may need reconfiguration.
