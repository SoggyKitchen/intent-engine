Security Deep Dive

NordPass uses XChaCha20 encryption — a more modern cipher than the AES-256 standard used by most competitors. Combined with a zero-knowledge architecture, this means Nord Security employees literally cannot access your passwords even if compelled by law enforcement.

The product has been independently security-audited. NordPass also includes a built-in data breach scanner that monitors the dark web for your credentials — a feature that costs extra with many competing products.
