Stripe Top Pick

Stripe is a comprehensive financial infrastructure platform that enables businesses to accept online payments, manage subscriptions, and expand globally with robust APIs and developer tools. It supports one-time, recurring, and usage-based billing with deep ecosystem integrations.

Pros

Cons

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stripe better than Recurly?

Stripe is generally better for most businesses due to its broader feature set, lower entry cost, and integrated payment processing. However, Recurly offers superior subscription-specific automation and revenue optimization for mature recurring revenue businesses.

Which is cheaper, Stripe or Recurly?

Stripe is typically cheaper for startups and mid-market companies, charging per transaction with no monthly base fee. Recurly starts at $99/month and can cost significantly more at scale, though it may reduce churn and increase lifetime value.

Can I switch from Recurly to Stripe?

Yes, you can migrate from Recurly to Stripe. Stripe offers migration tools and APIs to import subscription data, customer histories, and billing schedules. The process requires technical effort but is well-documented and supported.

Does Stripe or Recurly have a free plan?

Stripe has no monthly fee and is effectively free to start using (you only pay per transaction). Recurly does not offer a free plan but provides a 14-day free trial; its lowest paid tier starts at $99/month.

Which has better customer support, Stripe or Recurly?

Recurly offers more consistent 24/7 support with SLAs on higher plans, while Stripe’s support response times can range from 24–48 hours for standard users. Enterprise customers on both platforms receive dedicated support.

Is Stripe or Recurly better for small teams?

Stripe is better for small teams due to its low barrier to entry, intuitive dashboard, and no monthly cost. Small teams can launch quickly without complex billing logic, while Recurly’s pricing and setup are better suited for teams with dedicated finance or billing roles.

Does Stripe integrate with Recurly?

Yes, Recurly integrates with Stripe as a supported payment gateway. Many businesses use Recurly for subscription management while processing payments through Stripe for reliability and global reach.

Which tool has more features, Stripe or Recurly?

Stripe has more total features, including payments, invoicing, fraud prevention, and banking services. Recurly has fewer features overall but offers deeper functionality in subscription billing, such as advanced proration, billing alignment, and revenue recovery workflows.

Feature Deep Dive

Stripe’s Billing product includes subscription management with trial periods, metered billing, and coupon support, but requires more configuration for complex plan changes. Recurly excels with features like Dynamic Billing, Smart Proration, and Automated Dunning, which reduce churn and improve revenue recovery. Stripe offers Radar for fraud prevention and Sigma for analytics, while Recurly provides Revenue Optimization Score and Churn Insights. For global businesses, Stripe supports 135+ currencies and 40+ payment methods natively, whereas Recurly relies on gateways like Stripe for this reach.

Pricing Breakdown

Stripe charges 2.9% + $0.30 per successful card transaction with no monthly fee. Add-ons like Stripe Tax ($2/month) and Radar for Fraud Teams (free on most plans) are affordable. Recurly’s Starter plan is $99/month (up to $20K MRR), Pro is $299/month or 0.25% of revenue (whichever is higher), and Enterprise is custom. Note: Recurly does not process payments, so you’ll also pay gateway fees (e.g., Stripe’s 2.9% + $0.30), making total costs higher than Stripe alone.

Who Should Use Stripe

Stripe is ideal for startups, SMBs, and mid-market companies building digital products with hybrid billing models (one-time, subscriptions, usage-based). It suits developer-led teams needing fast integration and global scalability. Businesses with limited billing operations staff will appreciate Stripe’s low overhead and all-in-one platform for payments, invoicing, and compliance.

Who Should Use Recurly

Recurly is best for established SaaS or subscription businesses with complex billing needs, such as plan upgrades/downgrades, multi-phase subscriptions, or global tax compliance. It’s suited for finance and revenue operations teams focused on reducing churn and maximizing LTV. Companies already using Stripe or another gateway can layer on Recurly for advanced subscription logic.

Migration & Setup

Migrating from Recurly to Stripe is feasible using Stripe’s Import API and CSV tools, though custom logic must be re-implemented. Onboarding takes 1–4 weeks depending on complexity. Recurly also supports data export, but switching from Stripe to Recurly requires mapping billing logic into Recurly’s engine. Both platforms offer sandbox environments and testing tools to validate transitions.
