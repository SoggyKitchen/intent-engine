Frequently Asked Questions

Is GetResponse better than HubSpot?

It depends on your use case. GetResponse scores 8.6/10 vs HubSpot's 9.1/10. GetResponse wins on price and email-focused features — webinars, affordable automation, and a generous free plan. HubSpot wins on CRM depth, sales pipeline management, and an all-in-one suite. For email marketers on a budget: GetResponse. For CRM-first teams with budget: HubSpot.

How much does GetResponse cost vs HubSpot?

GetResponse starts at $15.58/mo for 1,000 contacts (Email Marketing plan). Full automation is available from $48.38/mo (Marketing plan). HubSpot's Starter is $15/user/mo, but the Professional tier — required for most advanced automation — costs $800/mo. For comparable email automation features, GetResponse is around 16× cheaper.

Does GetResponse have a free plan?

Yes — GetResponse has a free plan for up to 500 contacts with no credit card required and no time limit. It includes email marketing, a website builder, landing pages, and one automation workflow. HubSpot also has a free CRM tier, but email sends require a paid plan.

Can GetResponse replace HubSpot?

For pure email marketing and automation, yes. GetResponse handles email campaigns, automation workflows, landing pages, webinars, and basic contact management. It cannot replace HubSpot's advanced CRM, deal pipeline, service hub, or the breadth of a unified sales + marketing + service platform that larger teams rely on.
