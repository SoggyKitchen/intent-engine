Is Ramp Plus Worth Upgrading For?

If your team processes more than a handful of Bill Pay transactions per month, the math may favour upgrading. At $15/user/month for Ramp Plus, a team of 3 finance users pays $45/month. If the Bill Pay ACH fee is $1-2 per transaction, teams running 25+ monthly payments break even — those running 50+ bill payments save money.

Teams using Ramp primarily for corporate cards and expense reporting (not Bill Pay) are unaffected by the April 2026 change and can remain on the free plan.
