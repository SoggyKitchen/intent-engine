Common questions

Elementor FAQ

Is Elementor worth it in 2026?
For WordPress sites: yes. Elementor Essential at $59/yr gives you the theme builder, popup builder, and 100+ Pro widgets. For 1-3 sites, the $59-$99/yr cost is easily justified if it saves even one developer hour.
Is Elementor free?
Yes, Elementor has a free plan. You get 40+ core widgets and the basic drag-and-drop editor. But the free version lacks the Theme Builder, Form Builder, Popup Builder, and WooCommerce Builder — all of which require Pro.
What is the difference between Elementor Free and Pro?
Elementor Pro adds: 100+ additional widgets (vs 40 free), Theme Builder to control every part of your site, Popup Builder, Form Builder with integrations, WooCommerce product builder, and access to 300+ Pro templates. Pro starts from $59/yr for 1 site.
Does Elementor slow down WordPress?
It can — Elementor renders CSS and JS inline, which adds load. With proper caching (WP Rocket, LiteSpeed Cache) and a fast host, most sites score 90+ on PageSpeed Insights. The issue is usually misconfigured hosting, not Elementor itself.
Elementor vs Divi: which is better in 2026?
Elementor wins on UX and template quality. Divi wins on pricing (lifetime licence at $249 vs Elementor's annual subscriptions). For most new users, Elementor's interface is easier. For agencies avoiding recurring fees, Divi's lifetime is compelling.
