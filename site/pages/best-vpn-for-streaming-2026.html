What Makes a Good Streaming VPN?

  • Platform unblocking: Must reliably unblock Netflix, Disney+, and BBC iPlayer — the three most VPN-resistant platforms
  • Speed: Minimum 25 Mbps for HD, 50+ Mbps for 4K HDR. Good VPNs add under 10% overhead
  • Server count: More servers = more IP addresses = harder for Netflix to block all of them
  • Kill switch: Protects your real IP if the VPN connection drops during a stream
  • No-logs policy: Independently audited — you don't want your viewing habits recorded
