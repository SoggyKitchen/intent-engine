Elementor vs Squarespace: The Core Difference

Elementor and Squarespace solve the same problem — building a professional website without writing code from scratch — but they take fundamentally different approaches.

Elementor is a WordPress plugin. You install it inside WordPress, then use its drag-and-drop editor to design pages. This means you control your hosting, your data, and your full plugin ecosystem. The free version of Elementor is genuinely useful; paid plans unlock advanced widgets, theme builder, WooCommerce integration, and popup builder.

Squarespace is a fully hosted, all-in-one platform. Your website, domain, hosting, and email are bundled into one monthly subscription. There's no WordPress to configure, no plugins to update, and no server to manage. Templates are polished and ready out of the box.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Elementor better than Squarespace?
Elementor scores 8.7/10 vs Squarespace's 8.2/10 in our evaluation. Elementor wins for WordPress users, developers, WooCommerce stores, and anyone needing advanced SEO control. Squarespace wins for beginners who want a fully managed, all-in-one platform with zero hosting setup.
Can I use Elementor without WordPress?
No. Elementor is a WordPress page builder plugin — it requires a WordPress installation and separate web hosting. Squarespace is a standalone all-in-one platform with hosting included. If you don't want WordPress, Squarespace is the right alternative.
Is Elementor cheaper than Squarespace?
Elementor Free (no-cost plugin) plus budget WordPress hosting from around $3–5/month can be cheaper than Squarespace's $13/month Personal plan. Elementor paid plans start at $59/year ($4.92/month) plus hosting, making it comparable or cheaper than Squarespace for mid-tier plans. For larger stores, WooCommerce with Elementor costs far less than Squarespace Commerce at scale due to zero transaction fees.
Does Elementor or Squarespace have a free plan?
Elementor has a genuinely free WordPress plugin (Elementor Free) with no time limit, though you need separate WordPress hosting. Squarespace only offers a 14-day free trial — there is no ongoing free plan.
Which is better for ecommerce, Elementor or Squarespace?
Elementor with WooCommerce is more scalable and flexible for larger stores, with no transaction fees and access to thousands of WooCommerce extensions. Squarespace Commerce is easier to set up for small stores but charges transaction fees on its lowest-tier plan and limits third-party integrations significantly.
Can I switch from Squarespace to Elementor?
Yes, but it requires a full site rebuild. Export your content from Squarespace, set up WordPress hosting, install Elementor, and recreate your design. There is no automated migration tool. Most users allow 5–15 hours for a typical small business site, or hire a WordPress developer for a few hundred dollars.
