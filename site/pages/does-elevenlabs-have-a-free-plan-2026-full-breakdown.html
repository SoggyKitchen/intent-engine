The 6 Features Locked on the Free Plan

Before you commit to the free tier, understand exactly what you're giving up:

1. Commercial License

This is the biggest catch. Audio you generate on the free plan cannot be used commercially — no YouTube monetisation, client work, podcasts sold to brands, or business use. The moment money changes hands, you need a paid plan. Starter ($5/mo) unlocks commercial use.

2. API Access

Developers building apps, automations, or workflows need the API. It's completely locked on free. You can only use ElevenLabs via the web interface on the free tier. Starter unlocks the API.

3. Voice Clones Beyond 3

You can clone up to 3 voices on free (including your own). If you need separate voices for multiple clients, characters, or brand identities, you'll hit the limit fast. Starter gives you 10, Creator gives 30.

4. High-Quality Audio (192 kbps)

Free and Starter plans cap output at 128 kbps MP3. For professional audio production or podcast publishing, 192 kbps (available on Creator, $22/mo) delivers noticeably better quality. The difference is audible on good headphones.

5. Priority Generation Queue

At peak times, free-tier users wait longer for audio to generate. Paid plans get priority queue access. For production workflows with deadlines, the wait is frustrating.

6. Projects (Long-Form Audio)

ElevenLabs Projects is the feature for converting entire books, scripts, or long documents to audio with consistent voice and chapter structure. It's locked until Creator ($22/mo). Free and Starter are text-to-speech only — one clip at a time.

How Far Does 10,000 Characters Actually Go?

10,000 characters is the equivalent of approximately:

  • ~1,600–1,800 words of spoken text
  • ~8–10 minutes of generated audio at normal speech pace
  • ~2–3 short YouTube video voiceovers
  • ~1 full blog post read aloud

For personal testing and experimentation, 10,000 characters is generous. For any regular content production schedule — even just one video per week — you'll exhaust the free allowance in a few days. The Starter plan's 30,000 characters/month covers light professional use comfortably.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ElevenLabs free plan expire?

No — ElevenLabs' free plan does not expire. The 10,000 character allowance resets monthly. You can stay on the free plan indefinitely, though the commercial restriction and API lock remain until you upgrade.

Can I use ElevenLabs free plan for YouTube?

It depends on monetisation. If your YouTube channel is not monetised and you're creating personal content, the free plan technically permits this. If your channel earns ad revenue or the video is sponsored, you need a commercial license — that requires the Starter plan ($5/mo) or higher.

Does ElevenLabs free plan include voice cloning?

Yes — ElevenLabs free plan includes up to 3 custom voice clones (called "Instant Voice Cloning"). You can clone your own voice or create custom voices. However, commercial use of cloned voices requires a paid plan.

What happens when I run out of free characters?

When you exhaust your 10,000 monthly characters on the free plan, ElevenLabs will not generate more audio until your allowance resets at the start of the next month. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time to continue immediately.

Is there an ElevenLabs free trial for paid plans?

ElevenLabs does not offer a separate free trial for paid plans — the free plan itself is the trial mechanism. You can test the platform fully at no cost, then upgrade when you need commercial features or higher character limits. See our ElevenLabs free trial guide for the full walkthrough.
