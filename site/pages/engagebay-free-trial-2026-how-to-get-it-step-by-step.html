Common Questions

EngageBay Free Trial FAQ

Does EngageBay have a free trial?
Yes. EngageBay's free plan is permanent, not a 14-day trial. You get CRM, email marketing, deal management, and help desk tools for up to 250 contacts and 500 emails/month with no expiry date. A time-limited free trial with higher limits may also be available, check the signup page for current terms.
Does EngageBay require a credit card to sign up?
No. EngageBay's free plan signup requires only an email address and password. No credit card is collected until you choose a paid plan.
What features are included in the EngageBay free plan?
The free plan includes: CRM with contact management, deal pipeline, appointment scheduling, email campaigns (500/mo), and help desk ticketing. Marketing automation, landing pages, live chat, and custom reporting require Growth ($42.49/user/mo) or higher.
How does EngageBay compare to HubSpot on price?
EngageBay's paid entry ($11.04/user/mo annual) is roughly half HubSpot Starter's $20/user/mo. For full CRM + marketing automation + help desk combined, EngageBay Growth at $42.49/user/mo competes with HubSpot bundles that cost 3–5x more. The trade-off is fewer third-party integrations and a smaller app ecosystem.
Can I extend my EngageBay trial?
If you're on a time-limited trial, contact EngageBay support before expiry, they often extend for teams actively evaluating. The free plan is already permanent, so there's nothing to extend if you stay on free tier.
What happens to my data if I don't upgrade?
Your data is never deleted. If you hit free-plan limits, you retain read access to existing contacts and records. You can export your data at any time from the account settings.
