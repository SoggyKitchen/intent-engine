Quick answer — June 2026

PartnerStack prices on a custom basis — no public plan list, request-a-quote model. The platform fee is typically $500–800/mo for B2B SaaS companies running small-to-mid partner programs (<200 active partners). Larger ecosystems with resellers and deal registration run $1,500–3,000+/mo. Revenue share may apply on top. The free PartnerStack Marketplace lets publishers (like SaaSpare) join programs at no cost — the fee is paid by the SaaS company running the program.

What drives PartnerStack cost

Because PartnerStack quotes every customer individually, cost is driven by the following factors — all negotiable depending on your ARR and partner volume:

👥
Number of active partners
The primary cost lever. Programs with 1–50 active partners pay significantly less than programs with 500+. Inactive or stale partners still count in most base-tier definitions — keep your roster clean.
🏗️
Partner program types
Affiliate-only programs are cheapest. Adding referral partners and resellers (deal registration, co-sell) unlocks higher tiers. Enterprise programs with channel partners and MDF (market development funds) are top-tier.
🔗
Integrations (CRM, billing, SSO)
Native integrations with Salesforce CRM, HubSpot, Stripe, and Chargebee typically require higher-tier plans. Zapier/API access is available at mid-tier. SSO and advanced security features are enterprise-only.
📊
Commission complexity
Simple flat-rate affiliates (e.g., $50/referral) are straightforward. Tiered commissions, recurring revenue share, multi-touch attribution, and deal registration routing add complexity — and cost.
🛍️
Marketplace visibility
PartnerStack's Marketplace lets 80,000+ publishers discover your program. Featured placement and "Promoted" status cost extra. Standard listing is included in most base plans.

Hidden costs to budget for

💸
Revenue share on partner-driven sales
Some PartnerStack contracts include a small revenue share (0.5–2%) on top of the platform fee. This scales with your partner program success — model it at $1M+ ARR partner revenue before committing.
🚀
Onboarding and implementation fees
Enterprise setups with custom commission tiers, CRM sync, and SSO typically incur a one-time implementation fee ($2,000–5,000). Starter programs may have this waived — negotiate it out before signing.
⏱️
Annual commitment (no monthly option)
PartnerStack typically requires an annual contract. There is no month-to-month option. Build in 4–6 weeks for program setup before going live — that's 1–2 months of cost before your first partner conversion.
🔧
Developer time for integration
Connecting PartnerStack to your billing system (Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly) for accurate conversion tracking requires developer hours. Budget 10–20 hours for a clean integration — not covered in the platform fee.

PartnerStack pricing FAQ

How much does PartnerStack cost?
PartnerStack does not publish pricing publicly. It is custom-quoted based on partner program size, partner types, and features required. Based on market reports and community data, plans typically start at $500–800/mo for smaller programs (under 100 active partners) and scale to $2,000–3,000+/mo for larger partner ecosystems with resellers, deal registration, and CRM integrations.
Does PartnerStack have a free plan?
No. PartnerStack does not offer a free plan for companies running partner programs. A free demo is available. If budget is a constraint, lighter alternatives like Rewardful ($49/mo) or FirstPromoter ($49/mo) offer transparent pricing with lower entry costs. Note: joining PartnerStack as a publisher/affiliate is free — the fee is paid by the SaaS company, not the partner.
Can I negotiate PartnerStack pricing?
Yes. For annual contracts — especially at $1,000+/mo spend — PartnerStack sales teams regularly offer discounts. Typical negotiation outcomes include 10–20% off for multi-year commitments, waived implementation fees for early-stage companies, and custom onboarding support. Mention competitor quotes (Impact.com, Rewardful) in your conversation.
Is PartnerStack worth the cost?
For B2B SaaS companies with $1M+ ARR, PartnerStack's marketplace of 80,000+ publishers is the main value driver. If even 5–10 active partners generate $10K+/mo in new ARR, the $500–1,500/mo platform cost is easily justified. For pre-revenue startups or companies with less than $500K ARR, start with a lighter tool (Rewardful, FirstPromoter) and revisit PartnerStack when the ROI is clearer.
What is the PartnerStack Marketplace?
The PartnerStack Marketplace is a directory of 500+ B2B SaaS affiliate and partner programs. Publishers (bloggers, comparison sites, agencies) browse and apply to programs directly — free of charge. It's the primary way SaaS companies get discovered by affiliates. For publishers, it's one of the best sources of high-commission B2B SaaS affiliate programs in one place.
