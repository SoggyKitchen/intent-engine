Frequently Asked Questions

Does FreshBooks have a free trial?

Yes — 30-day free trial. 30-day free trial.

Is a credit card required for the FreshBooks free trial?

It depends on the plan. For most FreshBooks free tiers, no credit card is required. Paid trials may require a card but you won't be charged until the trial ends.

What happens after the FreshBooks free trial ends?

After the trial, your account moves to the free tier (if available) or pauses until you add payment. You won't lose your data. FreshBooks will email you before the trial expires.

Can I extend the FreshBooks free trial?

Yes — contact FreshBooks support before your trial expires and ask for an extension. Most companies grant 7-14 extra days, especially if you explain you need more time to evaluate with your team.

How do I cancel FreshBooks during the trial?

Cancel via your account settings before the trial ends to avoid charges. Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. FreshBooks typically confirms cancellation by email.

Is the FreshBooks free tier good enough?

30-day free trial. For solo users or very small teams, the free tier may be sufficient. For professional use, paid plans unlock features like advanced reporting, team collaboration, and API access.
