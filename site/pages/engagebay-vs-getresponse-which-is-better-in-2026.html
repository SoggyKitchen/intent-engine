Overview: EngageBay vs GetResponse

EngageBay
All-in-one CRM platform

Founded 2017 and built for small businesses priced out of HubSpot, EngageBay bundles CRM, email marketing, marketing automation, sales pipeline, helpdesk ticketing, and live chat into a single platform. Its free plan supports up to 250 contacts — a genuine no-credit-card free tier, not a trial.

GetResponse
Sales pipeline CRM

Founded 2010 and used by 100,000+ companies, GetResponse is built purely for sales teams. Its visual Kanban pipeline, activity reminders, and AI-powered sales assistant are best-in-class. It does not include marketing automation, email newsletters, helpdesk, or live chat — these require third-party integrations.

Who Should Choose EngageBay?

EngageBay is the better choice if:

  • You're a small business (under 50 people) that needs CRM + marketing + support in one platform
  • You're switching from HubSpot or similar tools and want to reduce your SaaS spend
  • You need built-in email campaigns, automation sequences, and customer support tickets without paying for separate tools
  • You want to start free and scale — EngageBay's free plan is genuinely useful for early-stage teams
  • You're an agency or reseller that needs to manage multiple client contacts affordably

Who Should Choose GetResponse?

GetResponse is the better choice if:

  • You have a dedicated sales team with a structured pipeline process and need the best deal-tracking UI available
  • You already use Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, or similar tools for marketing and just need a CRM that integrates with them
  • You rely heavily on AI-powered sales insights, activity tracking, and revenue forecasting
  • You need a large ecosystem of sales-specific integrations (Slack, Zoom, Calendly, Zapier, etc.)
  • Your team works in a B2B deal-driven model where deal stages and cycle time matter most

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay better than GetResponse?
EngageBay scores 8.6/10 vs GetResponse's 8.4/10 in our scoring. EngageBay is better for all-in-one SMB CRM — it includes marketing, live chat, and helpdesk that GetResponse charges extra for. GetResponse is better for sales-only teams that want the best pipeline UI and AI forecasting.
How much does EngageBay cost vs GetResponse?
EngageBay starts at $0/mo free forever (250 contacts), with paid plans from $14.99/user/month. GetResponse starts at $19/month (500 contacts) with no free plan. For a 5-person team needing CRM + marketing + support, EngageBay Pro ($80/seat) beats a comparable GetResponse stack ($59/mo Marketing Automation + a separate helpdesk tool).
Does EngageBay have a free plan?
Yes. EngageBay's free plan covers up to 250 contacts, 1,000 branded emails per month, and includes CRM, live chat, and basic helpdesk access — with no credit card required. GetResponse offers no free plan, only a 30-day trial.
What does GetResponse have that EngageBay doesn't?
GetResponse has a more polished visual sales pipeline, advanced AI-powered sales assistant, better revenue forecasting dashboards, built-in calling on all plans, and a larger marketplace with 700+ integrations. If pipeline clarity and sales workflow automation are your top priorities, GetResponse is the stronger dedicated tool.
Can I migrate from GetResponse to EngageBay?
Yes. EngageBay supports CSV import and has a migration team that helps move contacts, deals, and notes from GetResponse. Most teams complete the migration in under a week.
