Frequently Asked Questions

Is GetResponse better than ConvertKit?

It depends on your needs. GetResponse offers more enterprise-grade features like CRM, webinars, and advanced automation, making it better for scaling businesses. ConvertKit excels in simplicity and creator-focused tools, so it's better for solopreneurs and bloggers.

Which is cheaper, GetResponse or ConvertKit?

ConvertKit is generally cheaper at lower tiers, with a free plan and lower entry pricing. GetResponse starts slightly higher but offers more features per tier. At scale, GetResponse becomes more cost-effective for teams needing CRM and automation.

Can I switch from ConvertKit to GetResponse?

Yes, you can export your subscribers, tags, and sequences from ConvertKit and import them into GetResponse. GetResponse provides migration tools and CSV import support, though complex automations may require rebuilding.

Does GetResponse or ConvertKit have a free plan?

ConvertKit offers a free plan for up to 1,000 subscribers with core features. GetResponse does not have a permanent free plan but offers a 30-day free trial of all premium features with no credit card required.

Which has better customer support, GetResponse or ConvertKit?

GetResponse offers 24/7 support via live chat, phone, and email, with faster response times (under 2 hours). ConvertKit provides email and help center support, with priority responses only on higher-tier plans, averaging 12–24 hour replies.

Is GetResponse or ConvertKit better for small teams?

ConvertKit is better for very small teams or solo creators due to its simplicity and low cost. GetResponse suits small-to-mid-sized teams that anticipate growth and need CRM, automation, and collaboration tools.

Does GetResponse integrate with ConvertKit?

There is no direct integration between GetResponse and ConvertKit. However, you can migrate data via CSV export/import or use third-party tools like Zapier to sync subscriber data during transition periods.

Which tool has more features, GetResponse or ConvertKit?

GetResponse has significantly more features, including CRM, AI content generation, webinar hosting, and conversion funnels. ConvertKit focuses on core email marketing and visual automation, with fewer but highly polished tools for creators.

Feature Deep Dive

GetResponse offers a full automation suite with AI Workflow Builder, Dynamic Content, and Behavioral Tracking, enabling complex segmentation and drip campaigns. It includes built-in CRM with lead scoring and sales pipelines. ConvertKit counters with its Visual Automation Canvas, Tag-Based Sequences, and Broadcast Emails, ideal for content-led nurturing. While ConvertKit supports basic branching logic, GetResponse provides deeper logic conditions, A/B testing, and e-commerce triggers. For landing pages, GetResponse’s AI Page Builder outpaces ConvertKit’s simpler templates.

Pricing Breakdown

GetResponse starts at $19/month (Essential plan) for 1,000 contacts with email marketing and basic automation. The Plus plan ($59/month) adds CRM, automation, and landing pages. Max ($299/month) includes webinars, advanced reporting, and multi-user access. ConvertKit offers a free plan for 1,000 subscribers. Paid plans start at $15/month (Starter) for basic tools, $49/month (Creator Pro) for automation, and $99/month (Commerce) for digital product sales. GetResponse delivers more value at scale, while ConvertKit wins on entry-level affordability.

Who Should Use GetResponse

GetResponse is ideal for mid-sized businesses, e-commerce brands, and B2B companies needing a unified marketing and sales platform. Teams with 5+ members managing complex campaigns, lead nurturing, and customer lifecycle automation will benefit most. It suits organizations with budgets above $50/month seeking CRM integration, webinar hosting, and AI-powered content tools without adding third-party apps.

Who Should Use ConvertKit

ConvertKit is best for solopreneurs, bloggers, podcasters, and online course creators who prioritize ease of use and content monetization. It’s perfect for individuals or tiny teams managing email lists and selling digital products without needing CRM or advanced sales tools. With a low barrier to entry and intuitive design, it’s ideal for non-technical users focused on audience growth and engagement.

Migration & Setup

Migrating from ConvertKit to GetResponse is straightforward using CSV exports for subscribers, tags, and segments. Automation workflows will need to be recreated in GetResponse’s more advanced builder, which may take 2–5 hours depending on complexity. Both platforms offer onboarding guides, but GetResponse includes dedicated setup webinars. Typical onboarding time is 1–2 days for full migration and testing.
