Tool Overviews

NordVPN
From $3.39/mo · Panama · 6,400+ servers

NordVPN is the market-leading consumer VPN, used by over 14 million people. Its 6,400+ server fleet spans 111 countries, with specialised servers for P2P, double VPN, and Onion over VPN. The PwC-audited no-logs policy and Panama incorporation make it one of the most privacy-credible options on the market. Threat Protection (a built-in ad and malware blocker) works even when the VPN tunnel is off. Streams Netflix US/UK/JP, Disney+, BBC iPlayer, and Max reliably.

Windscribe
Free · Pro $5.75/mo · Canada · ~110 locations

Windscribe is a Canadian VPN notable for its genuinely useful free tier (10GB/month, 14 locations in 11 countries) and its Build Your Own Plan, which lets privacy-minded users handpick specific server locations at $1/location/month. Open-source desktop and mobile clients. The ROBERT filter blocks ads, malware, and tracker domains at the DNS level. Windscribe is a Five Eyes jurisdiction (Canada), which privacy purists may want to note. Pro plan unlocks unlimited data and all ~110 locations.

Pricing in Detail

NordVPN has three plan lengths, 2-year ($3.39/mo), 1-year ($4.99/mo), and monthly ($12.99/mo). All plans include the same feature set. The Basic tier (listed above) is VPN-only; Plus ($4.49/mo 2-yr) adds an ad blocker and password manager; Ultimate ($6.49/mo 2-yr) adds cloud storage.

Windscribe offers three options: Free (10GB/mo, no credit card required), Pro ($9/mo monthly or $5.75/mo annual, unlimited data, all locations), and Build Your Own ($1/location/month, pay only for the server countries you actually use). Build Your Own is genuinely unique and ideal for users who only need a handful of locations.

For most users comparing on price alone: NordVPN Basic 2-year ($3.39/mo) is cheaper than Windscribe Pro annual ($5.75/mo) and includes far more servers.

Privacy & Jurisdiction

This is where the gap is most significant. NordVPN is incorporated in Panama, which has no mandatory data retention laws and is outside the Five Eyes, Nine Eyes, and Fourteen Eyes intelligence-sharing alliances. Its no-logs policy has been independently audited three times by PwC, with full report published. RAM-only servers mean no data survives a physical seizure.

Windscribe is a Canadian company, Canada is a Five Eyes member and subject to intelligence requests. Windscribe has published a transparency report and their clients are open-source (auditable), but their server-side no-logs status has not been independently verified by a major auditor. For high-risk use cases, NordVPN's jurisdiction is meaningfully stronger.

For everyday privacy (ISP tracking, public Wi-Fi, geolocation), both are more than adequate.

Streaming Performance

NordVPN is one of the top three streaming VPNs on the market. It reliably unblocks Netflix US, UK, Japan, and Canada, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, Max, Hulu, and Paramount+. SmartPlay technology automatically routes streaming through optimised servers without manual server-switching.

Windscribe unblocks Netflix and some Disney+ libraries, but performance varies by server load. Some users report having to switch servers manually to find a working Netflix connection. For casual geo-unblocking it works; for reliable, set-and-forget streaming, NordVPN is the safer pick.

Final Verdict

NordVPN (9.2/10) wins for the vast majority of users, it's faster, has more servers, better streaming, stronger privacy credentials, and is actually cheaper on the 2-year plan than Windscribe Pro. The 30-day money-back guarantee makes it risk-free to test.

Windscribe (8.0/10) is the right pick in two scenarios: you want a genuine free VPN with meaningful data (10GB/month), or you want to handpick specific country servers via Build Your Own Plan. For privacy purists who care about open-source clients, Windscribe's transparent codebase is a real plus.
