EngageBay vs Freshsales — What's the Real Difference?

Both EngageBay and Freshsales are CRM platforms aimed at growing businesses, but they solve different problems. EngageBay is built for teams that want to consolidate their entire customer stack — marketing, sales, and support — under one subscription. Freshsales (part of Freshworks) is a dedicated sales CRM with strong AI features, built for teams whose primary job is closing deals.

EngageBay All-in-one SMB platform

EngageBay was built to replace HubSpot for budget-conscious teams. It includes marketing automation, email campaigns, a drag-and-drop landing page builder, CRM pipeline, live chat, and a helpdesk ticketing system — all in one subscription. The free plan covers up to 250 contacts.

Freshsales AI-powered sales CRM

Freshsales is a sales-focused CRM with Freddy AI at its core — providing deal scoring, next-step suggestions, and email sentiment analysis. It integrates natively with Freshworks' broader suite (Freshdesk for support, Freshmarketer for marketing). Free plan allows up to 1,000 contacts with basic features.

The Real Cost Difference

Freshsales Growth starts at $9/user/mo, which looks cheaper than EngageBay's ~$12/user/mo. But if your team also needs email marketing and a helpdesk (which most SMBs do), Freshsales requires buying Freshmarketer + Freshdesk separately. EngageBay bundles all three in one price — for many teams, the all-in cost is lower with EngageBay.

Feature-by-Feature Breakdown

CRM & Pipeline

Both tools offer contact management, deal pipelines, and activity tracking. Freshsales has an edge in pipeline UX — the drag-and-drop board is polished and Freddy AI surfaces which deals to prioritize. EngageBay's pipeline is solid for SMBs but less sophisticated in deal-scoring.

Email Marketing & Automation

This is where EngageBay clearly wins. Email campaigns, sequences, and marketing automation are built into every EngageBay plan. With Freshsales, you need to either use a separate tool or pay for Freshmarketer. For companies already paying for Mailchimp or ActiveCampaign, switching to EngageBay can eliminate that cost entirely.

AI Features

Freshsales's Freddy AI is a real differentiator: it scores deals based on CRM activity, suggests the next best action, and analyses email sentiment to flag at-risk conversations. EngageBay's AI features are more basic at this point. If your team is large enough to benefit from predictive scoring, Freshsales wins here.

Helpdesk & Customer Support

EngageBay includes a built-in helpdesk with ticketing and live chat from its Growth plan. Freshsales has no built-in helpdesk — you would need Freshdesk (a separate product, separately priced). For any team that handles customer support, this is a significant cost advantage for EngageBay.

Integrations

Freshsales integrates natively with the Freshworks ecosystem (Freshdesk, Freshchat, Freshcaller). It also has good third-party integration coverage. EngageBay connects with Zapier, Pabbly, and popular tools like Stripe, Shopify, and WordPress — sufficient for most SMBs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay better than Freshsales?
For most SMBs, yes — EngageBay (8.5/10) edges Freshsales (8.2/10) because it includes marketing automation and helpdesk in one subscription. Freshsales is better for sales-only teams that need Freddy AI pipeline intelligence.
How does EngageBay's free plan compare to Freshsales?
Freshsales offers a higher contact limit on its free plan (1,000 vs EngageBay's 250). However, EngageBay's free plan includes email marketing features that Freshsales does not — so the value depends on what you need most.
Can EngageBay replace HubSpot?
For SMBs on a budget, yes. EngageBay was explicitly designed as an affordable HubSpot alternative, offering similar all-in-one functionality at a fraction of the cost. See our EngageBay vs HubSpot comparison for details.
Does Freshsales have a free plan?
Yes. Freshsales offers a free forever plan for up to 1,000 contacts with basic CRM features — no credit card required. A 21-day full-feature trial is also available for paid plans.
What is Freddy AI in Freshsales?
Freddy AI is Freshworks' built-in AI layer that adds deal scoring (predicting which deals are most likely to close), next-step recommendations, and email sentiment analysis to the Freshsales CRM. It's available on Pro and Enterprise plans.
