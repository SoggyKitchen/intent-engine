#3 Alternative — Fastest Speeds
ExpressVPN

Consistently the fastest VPN in long-distance speed tests — ideal for 4K streaming and low-latency work. Lightway protocol (ExpressVPN's own) competes closely with NordLynx. TrustedServer tech means every server runs on RAM only — no data persists across reboots. More expensive at $6.67/month and limited to 8 devices. British Virgin Islands jurisdiction (no EU data retention laws).

From: $6.67/month (annual) · Best for: Streamers, travelers needing top speeds

#4 Alternative — Most Private
Mullvad

The privacy obsessive's choice. Mullvad requires no email address or account name — you get a random account number. Accepts cash and cryptocurrency. €5/month flat with no long-term plan lock-in (ProtonVPN requires annual commitment for the cheapest rate). Smaller server network (700+ in 40 countries) and does not unblock most streaming services. Sweden-based (EU jurisdiction, slight disadvantage vs Swiss ProtonVPN).

From: €5/month flat · Best for: Journalists, activists, maximum anonymity

#5 Alternative — Open-Source Budget Pick
Private Internet Access (PIA)

PIA is open-source (like ProtonVPN) and has a verified no-logs history — it has been legally subpoenaed twice and produced nothing. The largest server network in this list (35,000+ servers, 91 countries). Unlimited simultaneous connections. US-based (Five Eyes jurisdiction — significant disadvantage vs ProtonVPN's Switzerland). Budget pricing rivals Surfshark and CyberGhost.

From: $2.03/month (3-year plan) · Best for: Budget users who want open-source code

#6 Alternative — Beginner-Friendly
CyberGhost

The easiest VPN to set up if you're switching from ProtonVPN and want a simpler interface. 11,000+ servers with dedicated streaming and torrenting profiles make it beginner-friendly. 45-day money-back guarantee (the longest in this list). Romania-based (outside EU data retention directives, though still within Europol's reach). Privacy policy is less transparent than ProtonVPN's — Kape Technologies ownership is a concern for privacy-first users.

From: $2.03/month (2-year plan) · Best for: VPN newcomers, streaming

#7 Alternative — Best Free Plan
Windscribe

The best free ProtonVPN alternative. Windscribe's free plan offers 10GB per month across 14 countries with no time limit and unlimited devices. ProtonVPN's free plan offers unlimited bandwidth but only 1 device and 3 countries. If you want more country choice on a free plan, Windscribe wins. Paid plans from $4.08/month are also competitive. Canada-based (Five Eyes — same caveat as PIA).

From: Free (10GB/mo) or $4.08/month · Best for: Casual users, testing VPNs before paying

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best ProtonVPN alternative?

NordVPN is the best overall ProtonVPN alternative in 2026. It has 6,400+ servers across 111 countries, NordLynx (WireGuard) protocol for fast speeds, and a PwC-audited no-logs policy. Plans start from $3.39/month on the 2-year plan. Surfshark is the best budget alternative at $2.19/month with unlimited simultaneous connections.

Is NordVPN better than ProtonVPN?

For most users, yes. NordVPN has more servers (6,400+ vs 3,200+), broader country coverage (111 vs 91 countries), and is generally faster in independent speed tests. ProtonVPN's main advantage is its Swiss jurisdiction and fully open-source clients — strong for privacy purists. NordVPN is the better all-rounder for everyday use.

Is there a free ProtonVPN alternative?

Windscribe is the best free ProtonVPN alternative — it offers 10GB per month free across 14 countries with no time limit. ProtonVPN's own free plan (unlimited bandwidth, 1 device, 3 countries) remains one of the most generous free VPN options available. Avoid most other "free VPNs" — many log and sell your data.

Why would I switch from ProtonVPN?

Common reasons to switch: you want faster speeds (NordVPN benchmarks higher in most 2026 tests), more streaming unblocking (ProtonVPN's free plan does not unblock Netflix), more server locations, or a cheaper per-device price (Surfshark allows unlimited devices vs ProtonVPN Plus at 10). If price and speed matter more than Swiss jurisdiction, NordVPN or Surfshark win.

Does Surfshark match ProtonVPN on privacy?

Surfshark has been independently audited and has a verified no-logs policy. It is based in the Netherlands (EU jurisdiction — a disadvantage vs Switzerland for privacy purists). For most users Surfshark's privacy is strong enough; for activists or journalists, ProtonVPN's Swiss base and fully open-source code remain the gold standard.
