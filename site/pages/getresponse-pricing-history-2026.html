GetResponse pricing history FAQ

Has GetResponse raised prices recently?

GetResponse pricing has been stable in 2026. Free (500 contacts), Email Marketing from $19/mo, Marketing Automation from $59/mo, Ecommerce Marketing from $119/mo — all verified as of July 2026. The last structural change was the 2023 plan rename. No increase announced.

What is the true cost of GetResponse?

Advertised: $19/mo (1,000 contacts). Estimated true cost for a typical SMB: $59–150/mo. The contact scaling trap means costs 3–4× at 10,000 contacts. Factor in list growth before committing to annual billing.

Did GetResponse change pricing in 2023?

In 2023 GetResponse restructured and renamed its plans: Basic became Email Marketing, Plus became Marketing Automation, and Professional became Ecommerce Marketing. Feature sets were updated. The Free plan (500 contacts) was confirmed as a permanent free tier, not a trial.

Is GetResponse free plan real or a trial?

GetResponse Free plan is a permanent free tier — no credit card, no time limit. It covers up to 500 contacts with unlimited email sends, one landing page, and a website builder. Automation workflows, webinars, and advanced segmentation require a paid plan.

How do I lock in GetResponse's current price?

Annual billing saves approximately 18% versus monthly and locks in your contact-tier rate for 12 months. Buy annual at the start of a new contact tier — not when your list is about to cross into the next tier, where you'd be locking in the higher price.

Will GetResponse raise prices again?

SaaS platforms typically raise prices every 12–24 months, often citing AI or infrastructure investments. GetResponse has been adding AI features (email writer, website builder). We track GetResponse pricing weekly and update this page immediately on any change.
