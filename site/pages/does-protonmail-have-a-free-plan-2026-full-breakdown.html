The 3 Limits That Catch Most Free Users

1. 150 outgoing messages per day. Most personal users never hit this. Business users, newsletter senders, or anyone running campaigns will hit it immediately. If you send more than 150 emails per day, upgrade to Mail Plus.
2. 1GB storage fills faster than expected. Text-only email is fine. Any attachments — PDFs, images, zip files — eat into 1GB quickly. Once full, new emails bounce. Free users regularly hit this wall within a year of normal use.
3. No custom domain on free. You get a @proton.me or @protonmail.com address only. If you need yourname@yourcompany.com, you need Mail Plus (1 custom domain) or Proton Unlimited (3 custom domains).

Is ProtonMail Free Enough For You?

Free is enough if: You want a private, encrypted personal email address for sensitive communications. You send fewer than 150 emails per day. You are not a heavy attachment sender. You do not need a custom domain.

Upgrade to Mail Plus ($3.99/mo) if: Your 1GB is getting full. You send more than 150 emails per day. You want a custom domain. You want 10+ email aliases (great for filtering spam). Annual billing is $47.88/year — less than $4/month.

Consider Proton Unlimited ($7.99/mo) if: You want the full privacy suite — ProtonVPN Plus, 500GB shared storage, Proton Drive, and Proton Calendar Premium — all in one plan. At $95.88/year it replaces a VPN subscription too.

How to Get ProtonMail Free (Step by Step)

  1. Go to proton.me and click "Create a free account"
  2. Choose your username — this becomes your @proton.me address (cannot be changed later)
  3. Set a strong password. Proton uses this to generate your encryption keys — if you lose it, you cannot recover your email
  4. Complete a CAPTCHA (email or phone for verification if prompted)
  5. Download the Proton Mail app (iOS, Android, or use the web app at mail.proton.me)

Important: Unlike Gmail or Outlook, Proton cannot reset your password by accessing your email — zero-access encryption means only you can read your messages. Store your password somewhere safe (ideally a password manager).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ProtonMail free forever?

Yes — ProtonMail's free plan has no time limit or expiry. You can use it indefinitely with 1GB storage, 1 email address, and 150 messages per day at no cost.

What is the catch with ProtonMail free?

Three real limits: 1GB storage (fills up faster than you'd expect), 150 outgoing messages per day, and only 1 email address. No custom domains and no priority customer support on the free plan. There are no ads and no data selling — Proton funds the free tier from paid subscribers.

What is the difference between Proton Free and Mail Plus?

Proton Free gives you 1GB storage, 1 address, 150 msg/day. Mail Plus ($3.99/mo annual) gives 15GB storage, 10 email addresses, 1 custom domain, unlimited messages per day, and priority support.

Does ProtonMail free include ProtonVPN?

Yes — the Proton Free account includes ProtonVPN Free as well: 1 device, 3 server locations, unlimited bandwidth. They share the same Proton account login with separate apps.

Is ProtonMail free safe?

Yes — ProtonMail free uses the same end-to-end encryption, open-source code, and Swiss privacy laws as the paid plans. Zero-access encryption means Proton cannot read your emails. Only storage, address count, and daily message limits differ from paid tiers.

Can I switch from Gmail to ProtonMail free?

Yes. Proton provides a free Easy Switch tool that imports your Gmail emails, contacts, and calendar into your Proton account. Available even on the free plan. Note: imported emails stay encrypted in Proton's system after import.
