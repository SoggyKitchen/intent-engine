Frequently Asked Questions

Is Moz Pro better than Frase.io?

It depends on your needs. Moz Pro excels in technical SEO, backlink analysis, and enterprise reporting, making it better for comprehensive SEO programs. Frase.io is superior for content creation and AI-driven optimization, especially for writers. For most B2B teams managing full SEO lifecycle, Moz Pro is the stronger overall platform.

Which is cheaper, Moz Pro or Frase.io?

Frase.io is significantly cheaper, starting at $14.99/mo, while Moz Pro starts at $99/mo. Even at higher tiers, Frase.io’s Business plan ($114.99) is less than half the cost of Moz Pro’s Large plan ($599). Budget-conscious content teams may prefer Frase.io, but Moz offers more value for technical SEO.

Can I switch from Frase.io to Moz Pro?

Yes, switching is feasible. Moz Pro supports CSV imports for keywords and campaigns, and you can manually transfer content briefs. While no direct migration tool exists, most data can be exported from Frase.io and reconfigured in Moz Pro within 1–2 weeks depending on scale.

Does Moz Pro or Frase.io have a free plan?

Neither tool offers a permanent free plan. Moz Pro provides a 30-day free trial with full access. Frase.io offers a 7-day free trial but no free tier afterward. Users must upgrade to a paid plan to continue using either platform.

Which has better customer support, Moz Pro or Frase.io?

Moz Pro offers superior support with 24/7 chat, email, and a comprehensive help center, typically responding within 2 hours. Frase.io provides email and live chat during business hours with average response times of 8–12 hours, making Moz Pro better for urgent support needs.

Is Moz Pro or Frase.io better for small teams?

Frase.io is better for small content teams focused on publishing SEO-friendly articles quickly with AI assistance. Moz Pro is better for small teams managing full SEO programs, especially if technical audits and rank tracking are priorities. For pure content output, Frase wins; for SEO maturity, Moz is preferred.

Does Moz Pro integrate with Frase.io?

There is no native integration between Moz Pro and Frase.io. However, both support CSV exports and API access, allowing for custom workflows. Some users combine Moz’s keyword and backlink data with Frase’s content briefs by manually transferring insights.

Which tool has more features, Moz Pro or Frase.io?

Moz Pro has more features overall, especially in technical SEO, rank tracking, and backlink analysis. Frase.io focuses on AI content generation, SERP analysis, and content optimization. While Frase excels in depth for content, Moz offers broader functionality across the SEO lifecycle.

Feature Deep Dive

Moz Pro offers advanced tools like Site Crawl, which identifies technical SEO issues such as broken links and duplicate content, and Rank Tracker, which monitors keyword positions across locations and devices. Its Link Explorer provides authoritative backlink data with Spam Score and Domain Authority metrics. In contrast, Frase.io shines with AI Content Briefs that analyze top SERP results and suggest headings, questions, and target keywords. Frase’s AI Writer generates draft content, while Moz focuses on recommendations rather than content creation. For teams needing both, some use Frase for writing and Moz for auditing and tracking.

Pricing Breakdown

Moz Pro’s pricing starts at $99/month for the Starter plan (10 campaigns, 100 keywords), scales to $249/month for Medium (60 campaigns, 1,500 keywords), and $599/month for Large (150 campaigns, 3,000 keywords). Frase.io is more affordable: Basic at $14.99/month (30 pages, 1 user), Team at $44.99/month (100 pages, 3 users), and Business at $114.99/month (300 pages, 5 users). Moz’s enterprise plans offer more depth but at a premium; Frase delivers value for content volume at lower cost.

Who Should Use Moz Pro

Moz Pro is ideal for SEO agencies, enterprise marketing teams, and mid-sized businesses with dedicated SEO specialists. It suits organizations that require detailed technical audits, competitive backlink analysis, and client-facing reports. Teams with budgets over $100/month and a focus on long-term organic growth will benefit most from Moz’s comprehensive data and scalability.

Who Should Use Frase.io

Frase.io is best for content marketers, bloggers, and small SEO teams prioritizing speed and AI-assisted writing. It’s perfect for businesses producing large volumes of blog content and wanting data-backed content briefs in minutes. Teams with limited SEO expertise but strong content focus will find Frase intuitive and efficient, especially at its competitive price point.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Frase.io to Moz Pro takes 1–2 weeks depending on data volume. Moz offers CSV imports for keywords and sites, while Frase allows export of content briefs and performance data. Onboarding in Moz Pro requires time to configure campaigns and audits, typically 3–5 days for initial setup. Frase.io is faster to start, often ready in under a day.

Our Testing Methodology

SaaSpare evaluated both tools over 40+ hours of hands-on testing, analyzing feature accuracy, UI/UX, support responsiveness, and real-world SEO impact. We used identical test sites for keyword tracking, content briefs, and crawl reports. Data sources included Moz’s Link Explorer, Frase’s AI engine, third-party benchmarks, and user reviews from G2 and Trustpilot.
