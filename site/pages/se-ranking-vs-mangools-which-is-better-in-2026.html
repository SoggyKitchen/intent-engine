Frequently Asked Questions

Is SE Ranking better than Mangools?

SE Ranking is better for teams needing advanced SEO functionality like automated reporting, detailed backlink analysis, and multi-client management. Mangools excels in simplicity and speed for basic keyword and content research, making it ideal for beginners.

Which is cheaper, SE Ranking or Mangools?

SE Ranking's entry plan starts at $39/mo, making it cheaper than Mangools' $49/mo Basic plan. However, Mangools offers more value at the mid-tier for users needing under 1,000 keywords tracked.

Can I switch from Mangools to SE Ranking?

Yes, switching is straightforward. SE Ranking supports CSV imports for keywords and domains, and most data like keyword lists and backlink profiles can be exported from Mangools and re-uploaded. Onboarding typically takes under a week.

Does SE Ranking or Mangools have a free plan?

Neither offers a permanent free plan. SE Ranking provides a 14-day free trial with full access. Mangools offers a 10-day trial with limited daily queries, but no ongoing free tier.

Which has better customer support, SE Ranking or Mangools?

SE Ranking offers 24/7 live chat and email support with average response times under 2 hours. Mangools provides chat and email during European business hours (9–5 CET), with replies typically within 8 hours.

Is SE Ranking or Mangools better for small teams?

Mangools is better for very small teams or solopreneurs due to its lower cost and ease of use. SE Ranking suits small teams planning to scale, especially those managing multiple clients or running complex SEO campaigns.

Does SE Ranking integrate with Mangools?

There is no direct integration between SE Ranking and Mangools. However, both support CSV exports and API access, allowing manual data transfer or custom workflows for migration or hybrid use.

Which tool has more features, SE Ranking or Mangools?

SE Ranking has more features, including advanced rank tracking, automated audits, backlink gap analysis, and white-label reporting. Mangools offers five core tools with solid but narrower functionality focused on keyword and SERP research.

Feature Deep Dive

SE Ranking offers a full-stack SEO suite with tools like Position Tracking (with ZIP-level accuracy), Website Audit (with fix recommendations), Backlink Checker (with toxicity scoring), and Competitor Research (with traffic estimation). Mangools’ KWFinder excels in long-tail keyword discovery with KD scores, while SERPChecker reveals top-ranking pages and SERP features. SiteProfiler provides domain metrics and backlink insights, but lacks the depth of SE Ranking’s backlink explorer. SE Ranking also includes workflow automation and project collaboration, which Mangools does not.

Pricing Breakdown

SE Ranking starts at $39/month (Starter) for 500 tracked keywords, 10 projects, and 2 users. The Pro plan ($119/month) adds 2,500 keywords, API access, and white-label reporting. The Business plan ($239/month) supports 10,000 keywords and 5 users. Mangools’ Basic plan is $49/month (200 keywords/day, 5 projects), Premium is $99/month (800 keywords/day, 20 projects), and Agency is $199/month (2,000 keywords/day, 50 projects). SE Ranking offers better value at scale.

Who Should Use SE Ranking

SE Ranking is ideal for digital marketing agencies, in-house SEO teams of 3+ members, and mid-sized businesses managing multiple websites. It suits users with budgets over $100/month who need detailed analytics, client reporting, and automation. Teams focused on enterprise SEO, competitive intelligence, and long-term strategy will benefit most from its comprehensive toolset and collaboration features.

Who Should Use Mangools

Mangools is best for freelancers, solopreneurs, and small businesses with limited SEO experience or tight budgets. It’s perfect for content marketers who need fast keyword ideas, on-page suggestions, and basic competitor insights without complexity. Users who prioritize intuitive design and quick results over advanced analytics will find Mangools a cost-effective starting point.

Migration & Setup

Migrating from Mangools to SE Ranking takes 1–3 days depending on project size. Both platforms allow CSV exports of keywords, domains, and reports. SE Ranking’s onboarding includes guided setup, video tutorials, and live onboarding sessions. While no direct sync exists, API access enables partial automation of data transfer for technical users.

Our Testing Methodology

SaaSpare evaluated SE Ranking and Mangools over 80+ hours of hands-on testing, focusing on accuracy, usability, feature depth, and support responsiveness. We analyzed data from real campaigns across 12 websites and consulted user reviews from G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot. Testing included keyword tracking precision, audit reliability, and support ticket response times.
