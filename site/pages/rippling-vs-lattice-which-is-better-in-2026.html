Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rippling better than Lattice?

Rippling is ideal for large enterprises with complex HR needs, while Lattice is suitable for smaller teams and startups. Choose Rippling for its comprehensive features and scalability.

Which is cheaper, Rippling or Lattice?

Lattice is generally cheaper than Rippling, especially for small teams and startups. However, Rippling's custom pricing plan can be more cost-effective for large enterprises.

Can I switch from Lattice to Rippling?

Yes, it's possible to switch from Lattice to Rippling. However, the migration process can be complex and time-consuming, especially if you have a large workforce.

Does Rippling or Lattice have a free plan?

Lattice offers a free plan for small teams, while Rippling does not offer a free plan. However, Rippling offers a free trial and a custom pricing plan starting at $8 per employee per month.

Which has better customer support, Rippling or Lattice?

Rippling offers 24/7 support, while Lattice offers 24/5 support. However, both platforms have a reputation for providing excellent customer support.

Is Rippling or Lattice better for small teams?

Lattice is a great option for small teams and startups that need a user-friendly and affordable HR platform. Its focus on employee engagement and performance management makes it an ideal choice for smaller teams.

Does Rippling integrate with Lattice?

No, Rippling and Lattice do not integrate directly. However, both platforms offer integrations with other HR tools and services.

Which tool has more features, Rippling or Lattice?

Rippling has more features than Lattice, especially when it comes to payroll and benefits administration. However, Lattice's focus on employee engagement and performance management makes it a great option for smaller teams and startups.

Feature Deep Dive

Rippling offers a comprehensive set of features, including payroll, benefits, and HR management. Its payroll feature allows for automated payroll processing, while its benefits feature allows for customizable benefits administration. Lattice, on the other hand, focuses on employee engagement and performance management. Its features include performance reviews, goal setting, and employee recognition.

Pricing Breakdown

Rippling's custom pricing plan starts at $8 per employee per month, while Lattice's pricing plan starts at $5 per user per month. Both platforms offer a free trial and a free plan for small teams. However, Rippling's pricing plan can be more cost-effective for large enterprises.

Who Should Use Rippling

Rippling is ideal for large enterprises with complex HR needs. Its comprehensive features and scalability make it an ideal choice for companies with multiple locations and large workforces. Ideal customer profile: 500+ employees, multiple locations, complex HR needs.

Who Should Use Lattice

Lattice is a great option for smaller teams and startups that need a user-friendly and affordable HR platform. Its focus on employee engagement and performance management makes it an ideal choice for smaller teams. Ideal customer profile: 10-500 employees, small to medium-sized business, simple HR needs.

Migration & Setup

The migration process from Lattice to Rippling can be complex and time-consuming, especially if you have a large workforce. However, both platforms offer data exports and onboarding support to make the transition smoother. Typical onboarding time: 2-6 weeks.
