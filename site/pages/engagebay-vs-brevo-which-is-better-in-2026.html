Overview: EngageBay vs Brevo

EngageBay
All-in-one CRM platform

Founded 2017 and built for SMBs priced out of HubSpot, EngageBay bundles CRM, email marketing, marketing automation, sales pipeline, helpdesk ticketing, and live chat into one platform. Its free plan covers up to 250 contacts — a genuine no-credit-card free tier. Earns 30% recurring commissions for affiliates through the Awin network.

Brevo
Email & SMS marketing platform

Formerly Sendinblue, Brevo is a multi-channel marketing platform used by 500,000+ businesses. Its strengths are email deliverability, SMS and WhatsApp broadcasting, transactional email API, and automation workflows. It does not include a sales CRM pipeline or a helpdesk — these require third-party tools.

Who Should Use EngageBay?

EngageBay is the right choice when your team needs multiple tools unified in one platform. If you're currently paying separately for a CRM (e.g. Pipedrive), an email tool (e.g. Mailchimp), and a helpdesk (e.g. Freshdesk), EngageBay replaces all three at a lower combined cost. It's particularly strong for:

  • B2B SaaS teams with 2–50 people managing contacts, deals, and support from one place
  • E-commerce stores that need CRM + email automation + live chat without Shopify's premium app costs
  • Teams migrating away from HubSpot who need feature parity at a lower price
  • Startups that want to grow into a full marketing + CRM platform without switching tools

Who Should Use Brevo?

Brevo is the right choice when email deliverability and multi-channel messaging are your primary concern. It excels for:

  • High-volume senders (100K+ emails/month) who need dedicated IP warming and deliverability monitoring
  • Developers building transactional email flows (order confirmations, password resets) via API
  • Teams running SMS or WhatsApp marketing campaigns alongside email
  • Businesses that already have a CRM and only need a best-in-class email marketing layer

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EngageBay better than Brevo?
EngageBay scores 8.5/10 vs Brevo's 7.9/10. EngageBay is better for SMBs that need an all-in-one platform with CRM, helpdesk, and email marketing. Brevo is better for teams that only need email + SMS at scale and already have a CRM.
How do EngageBay and Brevo compare on price?
Brevo's Starter plan ($25/month) is cheaper for pure email sending. EngageBay's Basic plan ($14.99/user/month) is competitive once you account for the CRM, helpdesk, and live chat it includes — features Brevo doesn't offer at any price.
Does EngageBay have a free plan?
Yes. EngageBay free includes 250 contacts, 1,000 branded emails/month, full CRM, live chat, and helpdesk — no credit card required. Brevo's free plan covers 300 emails/day with unlimited contacts, but no CRM pipeline.
What does Brevo offer that EngageBay does not?
Brevo offers native SMS and WhatsApp campaign tools, a dedicated transactional email API with real-time delivery logs, better email deliverability (dedicated IPs on paid plans), and more advanced A/B testing on landing pages. If any of these are your primary use case, Brevo is the better choice.
Can I migrate from Brevo to EngageBay?
Yes. EngageBay supports contact CSV imports and has a Zapier integration to sync from most email platforms. Your contact list, tags, and basic segmentation migrate cleanly. Email templates require rebuilding in EngageBay's drag-drop editor.
