StreamYard

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Riverside.fm better than StreamYard?

It depends on your use case. Riverside.fm excels in recording quality and post-production, making it better for podcasts and professional content. StreamYard is stronger for live shows and real-time streaming to multiple platforms.

Which is cheaper, Riverside.fm or StreamYard?

Riverside.fm’s Pro plan ($24/mo billed annually) is slightly cheaper than StreamYard’s Basic plan ($25/mo billed monthly). However, StreamYard’s higher-tier plans are more expensive, especially when billed monthly.

Can I switch from StreamYard to Riverside.fm?

Yes, switching is straightforward. Riverside.fm offers import tools for show templates and supports similar live streaming destinations. Most users migrate within a week with minimal downtime.

Does Riverside.fm or StreamYard have a free plan?

Both offer free plans. Riverside.fm’s free tier includes 8 hours of recording per month with 4K video and local audio capture. StreamYard’s free plan allows unlimited streaming with a watermark and platform limitations.

Which has better customer support, Riverside.fm or StreamYard?

Riverside.fm offers 24/7 chat and email support on all paid plans with typical response times under 2 hours. StreamYard provides email support with 24–48 hour response times, making Riverside.fm faster for urgent issues.

Is Riverside.fm or StreamYard better for small teams?

Small teams focused on content quality and repurposing should choose Riverside.fm. Teams prioritizing speed, live interaction, and ease of use may find StreamYard more practical for day-to-day streaming.

Does Riverside.fm integrate with StreamYard?

No, Riverside.fm does not integrate directly with StreamYard. However, both can stream to the same platforms (e.g., YouTube, LinkedIn), and video files can be exported and shared across workflows.

Which tool has more features, Riverside.fm or StreamYard?

Riverside.fm offers deeper feature depth, especially in recording fidelity, AI editing, and post-production. StreamYard has strong live production tools but fewer advanced editing and automation features.

Feature Deep Dive

Riverside.fm records each participant’s audio and video locally using its desktop app, ensuring 4K video and 48kHz WAV audio even if internet drops. It includes AI Studio for automatic clipping, voice enhancement, and transcription. StreamYard operates entirely in-browser, relying on real-time streaming, which limits fidelity but enables instant scene switching, lower thirds, and live call-ins. Riverside’s Clip Editor and Multitrack Export are unmatched for post-production, while StreamYard’s Scenes and Multi-Streaming give it an edge in live production flexibility.

Pricing Breakdown

Riverside.fm offers a free plan (8 hours/month, 4K, local recording), Pro plan at $24/month (billed annually, 40 hours), and Business at $79/month (120 hours, team seats, SSO). StreamYard’s free plan includes unlimited streaming with watermark; Basic is $25/month (1 stream, 1 destination); Premium is $49/month (2 streams, 3 destinations); Professional is $99/month (unlimited streams and destinations). Riverside is more cost-effective for recording-heavy teams, while StreamYard charges more for multi-streaming capabilities.

Who Should Use Riverside.fm

Riverside.fm is ideal for content teams, podcast studios, and marketing departments that require broadcast-quality recordings and plan to repurpose content across channels. Teams with budgets for professional tooling and a focus on post-production efficiency will benefit most. It’s especially suited for remote interviews, video podcasts, and branded content creation where audio fidelity is critical.

Who Should Use StreamYard

StreamYard is best for agencies, educators, and live marketers who run frequent webinars, live Q&As, or social broadcasts. Its browser-based access and simple guest links make it easy for non-technical teams to go live quickly. Small businesses and solopreneurs who prioritize reach over recording quality will find StreamYard’s live streaming features highly accessible.

Migration & Setup

Migrating from StreamYard to Riverside.fm takes 1–2 weeks, including team training and show template recreation. Riverside supports exporting raw tracks, transcripts, and clips, enabling reuse of past content. Onboarding is smooth with guided tours and templates, though the desktop app requires installation. Data export tools are robust, and API access is available on higher plans for automation.

Evaluation Criteria

When choosing between Riverside.fm and StreamYard, prioritize recording quality, post-production needs, and integration with your CMS or editing suite. Consider onboarding effort, support responsiveness, and long-term scalability. Team size and technical comfort also influence fit—Riverside rewards investment in quality, while StreamYard favors speed and simplicity.
