Frequently Asked Questions

Is Riverside.fm better than StreamYard?

It depends on your use case. Riverside.fm excels in recording high-fidelity audio and video for post-production, making it ideal for podcasts and professional content. StreamYard is better for live streaming to platforms like YouTube and LinkedIn with real-time production tools.

Which is cheaper, Riverside.fm or StreamYard?

Riverside.fm's Pro plan starts at $24/month, while StreamYard's equivalent Professional plan is $49/month. Riverside.fm offers better value for teams needing high-quality recordings, though both have free tiers with limitations.

Can I switch from StreamYard to Riverside.fm?

Yes, switching is straightforward. Riverside.fm supports guest invites via link, and you can export StreamYard recordings to import into Riverside.fm for editing. Onboarding takes under an hour with available migration guides.

Does Riverside.fm or StreamYard have a free plan?

Both offer free plans. Riverside.fm’s free tier includes 8 hours of recording per month in 4K with local capture. StreamYard’s free plan allows unlimited streaming with a watermark and 480p cloud recording.

Which has better customer support, Riverside.fm or StreamYard?

Riverside.fm offers 24/7 chat and email support on all paid plans, with faster response times (under 2 hours). StreamYard provides email and help center support, with priority responses only on Professional and Teams plans (typically 24-hour turnaround).

Is Riverside.fm or StreamYard better for small teams?

Small teams focused on content creation (e.g., marketing, podcasts) benefit more from Riverside.fm’s quality and editing tools. Teams doing frequent live webinars or social broadcasts may prefer StreamYard’s ease of use and live features.

Does Riverside.fm integrate with StreamYard?

No, Riverside.fm does not directly integrate with StreamYard. However, you can export Riverside.fm recordings and upload them to platforms where StreamYard is used for live distribution, enabling a hybrid workflow.

Which tool has more features, Riverside.fm or StreamYard?

Riverside.fm offers deeper feature depth in recording, AI editing, and post-production (e.g., Smart Clip, Voice Enhance). StreamYard leads in live production features like overlays, multi-platform streaming, and teleprompter. Riverside.fm has a broader overall feature set for content lifecycle management.

Feature Deep Dive

Riverside.fm records each participant’s audio and video locally in up to 4K and 48kHz, ensuring studio quality regardless of internet drops—ideal for post-production. It includes AI tools like Smart Clip for auto-highlight extraction and Voice Enhance for noise reduction. StreamYard, in contrast, streams everything in-browser with real-time overlays, scene switching, and live call-ins, but records at lower quality (max 720p on Pro). Riverside.fm supports multi-track exports for editing in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro, while StreamYard focuses on instant publishing to Facebook Live, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

Pricing Breakdown

Riverside.fm offers a free plan (8 hours/month, 4K recording), Pro at $24/month (unlimited 4K, AI tools), Teams at $59/month (collaboration features), and custom Enterprise pricing. StreamYard’s free plan includes unlimited streaming with a watermark and 480p recording. Its Basic plan is $25/month (720p), Professional at $49/month (1080p, multi-streaming), and Teams at $149/month. Riverside.fm is more cost-effective for high-quality recording needs, while StreamYard’s live streaming capabilities justify its higher tiers for broadcasters.

Who Should Use Riverside.fm

Riverside.fm is ideal for content creators, marketing teams, and podcast studios that prioritize broadcast-quality recordings and efficient post-production. Teams of 2–10 members with budgets for professional tools will benefit most. It suits organizations producing evergreen content like interviews, podcasts, or training videos where audio fidelity and editing flexibility are critical.

Who Should Use StreamYard

StreamYard is best for marketers, educators, and community managers who run frequent live shows, webinars, or social broadcasts. It’s perfect for teams wanting to stream to multiple platforms simultaneously without technical overhead. Small to mid-sized businesses with limited AV resources will appreciate its browser-based simplicity and real-time production features.

Migration & Setup

Switching from StreamYard to Riverside.fm is simple: invite guests via link, record locally, and export high-res files. Riverside.fm supports MP4 and WAV exports, while StreamYard recordings can be downloaded and imported for editing. Onboarding takes under an hour, with templates and collaboration tools easing team adoption. No data sync exists between platforms, but workflows can be bridged manually.

Evaluation Criteria

When choosing between Riverside.fm and StreamYard, prioritize use case: archival quality vs. live reach. Key criteria include pricing structure, onboarding effort, integration depth (e.g., with CRM or editing tools), support responsiveness, and team workflow fit. Evaluate based on whether your content is consumed live or edited for long-term use.
