Frequently Asked Questions

Is Moz Pro better than SpyFu?

It depends on your goals. Moz Pro is superior for technical SEO, site health, and full-cycle optimization, while SpyFu leads in competitor keyword tracking and PPC intelligence. For most teams building sustainable SEO programs, Moz Pro offers broader utility.

Which is cheaper, Moz Pro or SpyFu?

SpyFu is generally cheaper at entry levels, starting at $39/month compared to Moz Pro’s $99/month. However, Moz Pro offers more value per dollar for teams needing site audits, rank tracking, and backlink analysis at scale.

Can I switch from SpyFu to Moz Pro?

Yes, switching is straightforward. Moz Pro supports CSV imports for keywords and competitors, and its onboarding tools help map existing campaigns. Data like historical ad copy from SpyFu will need manual export, but SEO strategy can transition smoothly.

Does Moz Pro or SpyFu have a free plan?

Neither offers a permanent free plan. Moz Pro provides a 30-day free trial with full access, while SpyFu offers a 7-day trial. Both allow limited functionality during trials but require payment to continue.

Which has better customer support, Moz Pro or SpyFu?

Moz Pro offers faster response times (under 4 hours via chat/email) and a more robust support ecosystem including Moz Community and Academy. SpyFu support is reliable but slower, averaging 8–12 hours for replies, with fewer educational resources.

Is Moz Pro or SpyFu better for small teams?

Small teams focused on growth and learning should prefer Moz Pro for its intuitive interface and training content. SpyFu suits small teams with experienced SEOs who prioritize competitor insights over technical SEO.

Does Moz Pro integrate with SpyFu?

There is no native integration between Moz Pro and SpyFu. However, both support CSV exports and API access, allowing manual data synchronization for keyword lists, rankings, and backlink reports.

Which tool has more features, Moz Pro or SpyFu?

Moz Pro has more features overall, including site crawling, page optimization suggestions, rank tracking, and backlink analysis. SpyFu focuses deeply on competitor intelligence but lacks technical SEO tools, giving Moz Pro the edge in breadth and depth.

Feature Deep Dive

Moz Pro offers robust tools like Site Crawl, which identifies broken links, duplicate content, and crawl errors, and Keyword Explorer, which provides keyword difficulty and opportunity scores. In contrast, SpyFu’s Kombat and Fili tools allow deep dives into competitors’ top-ranking pages and paid search history, showing exact ad copy and spend estimates. While Moz Pro excels in on-page and technical SEO with features like On-Page Grader and Link Explorer, SpyFu dominates in revealing 'hidden' competitor strategies, especially in paid search. However, SpyFu lacks automated site audits, making it insufficient as a standalone SEO platform for most teams.

Pricing Breakdown

Moz Pro starts at $99/month (Starter) for one site and 100 tracked keywords, scaling to $299/month (Large) for five sites and 3,000 keywords. The Medium plan at $179/month is popular among agencies. SpyFu starts at $39/month (Basic) with 200 keyword searches per day, rising to $249/month (Team) for 2,000 daily searches. While SpyFu appears cheaper, its usage-based model can limit access under heavy use. Moz Pro’s pricing is more predictable and includes broader functionality, making it more cost-effective for teams doing comprehensive SEO.

Who Should Use Moz Pro

Moz Pro is ideal for SEO agencies, enterprise marketing teams, and mid-sized SaaS companies building scalable SEO programs. It suits teams with dedicated SEOs or growth marketers who need technical insights, content optimization, and performance reporting. With strong collaboration features and API access, it integrates well into existing tech stacks. Budgets of $100+/month are typical, but the ROI in improved rankings and site health justifies the cost.

Who Should Use SpyFu

SpyFu is best for digital marketers, PPC specialists, and competitive intelligence analysts who need to reverse-engineer competitors’ search strategies. It’s especially valuable for agencies running competitor audits or businesses entering crowded markets. Solo consultants and small teams on a budget benefit from its lower entry cost and powerful keyword gap tools. However, it should be paired with other SEO tools for technical and on-page optimization.

Migration & Setup

Switching from SpyFu to Moz Pro takes 1–2 weeks for full onboarding. Moz Pro offers guided setup, site verification, and crawl configuration within days. Both tools allow CSV exports of keywords and rankings, though historical ad data from SpyFu won’t transfer. Moz Pro’s intuitive dashboard reduces training time, and most teams achieve full adoption within 10 business days with minimal IT support.
