Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Rippling cost per month?

Rippling starts at $8/employee/month for the Starter plan (after first 5 employees free), $18/employee/month for the Pro plan. Enterprise pricing is custom and typically starts at $25+/employee/month depending on modules and scale.

Does Rippling have a free plan?

Yes. Rippling offers a free tier for companies with up to 5 employees. It includes core HR, IT device management, and basic payroll. No credit card is required to start.

Is Rippling worth the price?

For companies scaling past 20 employees, Rippling often delivers strong ROI by reducing administrative overhead, minimizing IT-HR coordination gaps, and cutting down on tool sprawl. Buyers report saving 10–15 hours per week on ops tasks. However, for smaller teams or those with simple needs, the Pro plan may be overkill.
