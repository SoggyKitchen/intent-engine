Frequently Asked Questions

Is ActiveCampaign worth it in 2026?

Yes, especially for B2B marketers who rely on automation to nurture leads and sync with sales teams. Its AI features, CRM, and workflow depth have improved significantly, making it a strong contender in the mid-market space.

What are the main complaints about ActiveCampaign?

Users frequently cite the steep learning curve, especially for complex automations, and report that the reporting dashboards feel clunky. Some also note unexpected cost increases as contact lists grow.

Who should NOT use ActiveCampaign?

Small businesses or startups with simple email marketing needs, limited technical resources, or tight budgets should consider simpler tools. ActiveCampaign’s power comes with complexity that can overwhelm less experienced teams.
