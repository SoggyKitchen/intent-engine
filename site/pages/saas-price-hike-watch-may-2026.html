Why So Many SaaS Tools Raised Prices in 2026

Three forces drove the 2026 SaaS pricing wave:

  • AI feature monetisation. Vendors added AI features and used the launch as cover for repricing the underlying plan (Monday, Notion, Asana, Salesforce all did versions of this).
  • Macro inflation pass-through. Vendors had absorbed 2-3 years of input cost inflation. 2026 was the catch-up year, average B2B SaaS price increase was 8.7% per the SaaS Inflation Index.
  • Investor pressure on net revenue. Public SaaS companies (HubSpot, Salesforce, Atlassian) faced shareholder pressure to expand existing-customer revenue without acquisition cost.

What to Do If Your Tool Just Raised Prices

  1. Don't auto-renew. Set a calendar reminder 60 days before renewal. Vendors retain 90% of customers who don't actively shop competitors.
  2. Email the rep. "We're evaluating [Competitor]. What's the best price you can offer for an annual commitment?", this triggers the retention discount, typically 15-30% off list.
  3. Compare against the alternative we list above. Most of the hikes have a viable cheaper alternative.
  4. Negotiate a multi-year lock-in. 2-3 year contracts often hold pricing flat, avoiding the next annual hike.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much did SaaS prices increase in 2026?

The average B2B SaaS price increase in 2026 was ~9%, but ranged from 0% (stable vendors like Pipedrive, Linear) to 23% (Asana Starter tier).

Which SaaS tool raised prices the most in 2026?

Asana raised the Starter tier by 23% (from $10.99 to $13.49/user/month). Notion's Business plan increased 20%. HubSpot Professional rose ~11%.

Can I lock in current SaaS pricing before the next hike?

Yes, annual or multi-year contracts typically hold pricing flat for the contract term. Vendors usually honour grandfathered pricing for existing customers on legacy plans. See our Grandfathered SaaS Pricing 2026 tracker.
