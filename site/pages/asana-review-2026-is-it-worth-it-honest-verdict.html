Frequently Asked Questions

Is Asana worth it in 2026?

Yes — for Marketing teams, operations, cross-functional project work. Intuitive and clean UI; Excellent timeline/Gantt view. If budget is a concern, ClickUp offers 80% of the value at a lower price.

What are the biggest downsides of Asana?

The main complaints: No built-in time tracking; Free plan limited; Expensive per-user at scale. These are real limitations worth factoring into your decision.

How does Asana compare to ClickUp?

Asana vs ClickUp: Asana wins on intuitive and clean ui. ClickUp wins on most features of any pm tool. It depends on your team's primary need.

Is Asana good for small teams?

Asana starts at $0 (Free). For teams under 5 people, it's worth the cost if you use it daily. Larger teams should negotiate annual pricing.

Does Asana have a free trial?

30-day free trial of premium. We recommend using the full trial period to test your actual workflow, not a demo setup.

What do customers say about Asana?

On G2 and Capterra, Asana scores consistently high for intuitive and clean ui and excellent timeline/gantt view. The most common complaints are about no built-in time tracking.

How long does Asana setup take?

Most teams are fully set up in 1-3 days. Enterprise setups with integrations can take 1-2 weeks. Asana has onboarding resources and a support team to help.

Is Asana safe and secure?

Asana is SOC 2 Type II certified and offers enterprise security features including SSO, audit logs, and role-based access control on higher plans.

Who Should Use It

Asana is ideal for Marketing teams, operations, cross-functional project work. It's less suitable for teams that need no built-in time tracking; free plan limited — in those cases, look at alternatives.

Pricing Breakdown

Asana pricing starts at $0 (Free) for the entry plan, $10.99/mo for the mid tier, and $24.99/mo for the top tier. Annual billing saves 15-20%. Startup programs available.

Key Features Worth Knowing

Standout features: Intuitive and clean UI; Excellent timeline/Gantt view; Strong automation rules; Great integrations (200+). These are the capabilities that make Asana worth the price for the right team.

Our Verdict

After thorough testing, Asana earns 4.5/5. The positives (Intuitive and clean UI, Excellent timeline/Gantt view) outweigh the negatives (No built-in time tracking) for Marketing teams, operations, cross-functional project work. Start with the free trial.
