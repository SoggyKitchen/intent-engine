Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best video conferencing software for remote teams?

Zoom is the best overall for remote teams due to its reliability, high-quality video, and extensive integrations. Microsoft Teams is a close second, especially for companies already using Microsoft 365.

How much does video conferencing software cost for remote teams?

Most tools range from $4 to $15 per user/month. Free tiers are available on Zoom, Google Meet, and Loom, but with time or feature limitations.

Is there a free video conferencing tool for remote teams?

Yes—Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Loom all offer free plans suitable for small teams or light usage. Whereby offers free 1-on-1 meetings with no time limits.
