Frequently Asked Questions

Is Shopify better than Gumroad?

Shopify is a more comprehensive ecommerce platform with more features and integrations, but Gumroad is a better option for solo creators and small teams with simple digital product sales.

Which is cheaper, Shopify or Gumroad?

Gumroad is generally cheaper than Shopify, with lower fees and no monthly subscription costs.

Can I switch from Gumroad to Shopify?

Yes, it's possible to switch from Gumroad to Shopify, but you may need to export your product data and reconfigure your storefront.

Does Shopify or Gumroad have a free plan?

Shopify offers a 14-day free trial, but no free plan. Gumroad does not offer a free plan.

Which has better customer support, Shopify or Gumroad?

Shopify has excellent customer support, with 24/7 phone and email support. Gumroad has good customer support, but it's not as comprehensive.

Is Shopify or Gumroad better for small teams?

Gumroad is a better option for small teams with simple digital product sales, due to its ease of use and low fees.

Does Shopify integrate with Gumroad?

Yes, Shopify has a Gumroad integration that allows you to sell digital products through your Shopify storefront.

Which tool has more features, Shopify or Gumroad?

Shopify has more features and integrations than Gumroad, including product management, payment processing, shipping integration, and analytics.

Feature Deep Dive

Shopify offers a wide range of features, including product management, payment processing, shipping integration, and analytics. It also has a large app store with thousands of integrations, including integrations with popular services like Amazon, eBay, and Facebook. Gumroad, on the other hand, offers a simple and easy-to-use interface, with features such as customizable storefronts, payment processing, and analytics. It's a great option for solo creators and small teams with simple digital product sales.

Pricing Breakdown

Shopify's pricing plans range from $29/month to $299/month, depending on the plan you choose. Gumroad's pricing is based on a percentage of sales, with a 3.5% + 30¢ per sale fee for standard sales and a 2.9% + 30¢ per sale fee for Gumroad Pro sales.

Who Should Use Shopify

Shopify is ideal for established businesses with complex product offerings, including physical and digital products. It's also a great option for businesses that need a high level of customization and scalability.

Who Should Use Gumroad

Gumroad is ideal for solo creators and small teams with simple digital product sales. It's a great option for those who want to sell their work directly to customers and don't need a high level of customization or scalability.

Migration & Setup

Switching from Gumroad to Shopify may require exporting your product data and reconfiguring your storefront. Shopify offers a 14-day free trial, which can help you get started with the platform. Gumroad does not offer a free trial, but it's generally easy to set up and use.
