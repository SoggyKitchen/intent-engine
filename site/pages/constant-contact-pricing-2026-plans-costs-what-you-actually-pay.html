The fine print

Hidden costs to watch

📈
List-size price jumps
The biggest hidden cost. Standard doubles in price as your list grows from 500 to 10,000 contacts — with no warning in the advertised price. Always model 12-month list growth before signing up.
📱
SMS marketing is an add-on
SMS is not included in any plan. It's available as a paid add-on starting around $10/month for 500 SMS sends. If you want SMS + email, factor this in from day one.
🔒
Annual lock-in discount
The ~15% annual discount sounds good, but you're locked in for 12 months. If you decide to switch after 3 months, you've already paid for the rest of the year.
🚫
No free plan — trial only
Unlike Mailchimp (which has a permanent free tier up to 500 contacts), Constant Contact only offers a 60-day trial. After 60 days, you must pay or lose access to your account.

Common questions

Constant Contact pricing FAQ

How much does Constant Contact cost in 2026?

Constant Contact pricing starts at $12/month for the Lite plan (up to 500 contacts, billed monthly). Standard starts at $35/month and Premium at $80/month. All prices increase with contact list size — a 10,000-contact list on Standard runs about $120/month.

Does Constant Contact have a free plan?

No permanent free plan in 2026. Constant Contact offers a 60-day free trial with access to core features — no credit card required to start. After 60 days you must subscribe to a paid plan.

What is the Constant Contact list-size pricing trap?

Constant Contact's advertised price is based on 500 contacts. If your list grows to 2,500 contacts, Standard jumps from $35 to around $55/month. At 10,000 contacts, Standard costs roughly $120/month — over three times the entry price.

Is Constant Contact worth it in 2026?

Constant Contact is a solid choice for small businesses, nonprofits, and event-based organisations that value ease of use, strong deliverability, and phone support. For advanced automation or tight budgets, GetResponse or ActiveCampaign often provide better value per dollar.

What is the difference between Lite, Standard, and Premium?

Lite ($12/mo) gives basic email sends, 100+ templates, and list management. Standard ($35/mo) adds automated email series, contact segmentation, social media scheduling, and Google Ads. Premium ($80/mo) adds A/B testing, dynamic content, advanced automation, and priority phone support.

Does Constant Contact offer a nonprofit discount?

Yes. Constant Contact offers a 20–30% nonprofit discount on all plans, which is better than most competitors. To claim it, you need to provide proof of nonprofit status during signup.

How does Constant Contact compare to Mailchimp?

Constant Contact has no free tier (only a trial) while Mailchimp offers a free plan up to 500 contacts. Constant Contact is generally better for phone support, event tools, and nonprofit pricing. Mailchimp offers more integrations and a free tier for very small lists.
