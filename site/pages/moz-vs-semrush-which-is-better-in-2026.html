Verdict at a Glance

Both Moz and Semrush are legitimate, established SEO tools. The choice usually comes down to depth vs focus.

Semrush wins on breadth: keyword research, site auditing, competitor traffic analysis, PPC research, backlink analytics, content gap analysis, and social media scheduling — all in one subscription. Its keyword database is simply larger, returning more results, especially for long-tail terms.

Moz wins on simplicity and Domain Authority: if your workflow revolves around DA scores for link building, or you want fewer tools but better UX, Moz is a defensible choice. It's also slightly cheaper at entry level.

For head-to-head accuracy, also consider Ahrefs vs Semrush — Ahrefs is Semrush's closest peer and wins on backlink data freshness.

Our Final Verdict

Semrush is the better all-round choice for most SEO professionals and agencies. Here is the breakdown:

  • Choose Semrush if you need a single platform for SEO, PPC, content, and competitor research. The larger keyword database alone justifies the price difference for teams doing serious keyword research. Starting at $139.95/mo with a 14-day trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Moz or Semrush better for SEO?
Semrush is better for most SEO professionals — scoring 9.4/10 vs Moz's 8.8/10. Semrush has a larger keyword database (25B+ keywords), stronger competitor analysis, and covers PPC and content marketing. Moz is a solid choice if you primarily need Domain Authority scores or want a simpler interface.
How much does Moz cost vs Semrush?
Moz Pro starts at $99/mo (Starter). Semrush starts at $139.95/mo (Pro). Semrush costs more but includes significantly more tools. Both offer free trials — Moz offers 30 days and Semrush offers 14 days.
Which has a bigger keyword database — Moz or Semrush?
Semrush has a larger keyword database with 25B+ keywords and 808M+ domains. Moz Pro's keyword explorer has approximately 1.25B+ keywords. For most keyword research, Semrush returns more results, especially for long-tail and international queries.
Is Moz's Domain Authority reliable?
Moz's Domain Authority (DA) is widely used as a benchmark across the industry. It's still the most referenced third-party metric for backlink strength. However, it's a proprietary score and Google does not use DA to rank pages. Semrush uses its own Authority Score metric, which is similar but may vary.
Can I use Moz and Semrush together?
Yes, some SEO teams use both — Moz for DA-based link prospecting and Semrush for keyword research and competitor tracking. However, for most teams, Semrush alone covers all these use cases and is a more cost-efficient single subscription.
