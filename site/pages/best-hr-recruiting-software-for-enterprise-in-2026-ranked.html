What is the best hr recruiting software for enterprise?

Workday is the best overall HR recruiting software for enterprise due to its full-suite HCM capabilities, scalability, and advanced analytics. Greenhouse is a close second for teams focused purely on optimizing recruiting operations.

How much does hr recruiting software cost for enterprise?

Enterprise HR recruiting software typically costs between $70 and $200+ per user per month, with pricing often customized based on modules, company size, and integration needs.

There are no fully free HR recruiting tools suitable for enterprise use, but Greenhouse, Lever, Culture Amp, and Lattice offer free trials. Open-source or freemium models are rare due to security and scalability requirements at enterprise level.
