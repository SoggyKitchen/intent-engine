Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best crm software for B2B SaaS?

HubSpot CRM is the best overall for most B2B SaaS companies due to its free tier, ease of use, and deep integration with marketing and sales tools. Salesforce is ideal for larger, enterprise-level teams needing advanced customization.

How much does crm software cost for B2B SaaS?

CRM software for B2B SaaS ranges from free (HubSpot CRM) to $57+/user/month for specialized tools like Close or Keap. Enterprise solutions like Salesforce start at $25/user/month but can exceed $300/user/month with add-ons.

Is there a free crm tool for B2B SaaS?

Yes, HubSpot CRM offers a powerful free plan with no time limit, including contact management, deal tracking, email syncing, and basic automation—ideal for early-stage B2B SaaS startups.
