1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — for example, when you subscribe to our newsletter or submit a contact form. This may include your email address and name.

We also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit the site, including:

  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
  • Browser type, device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referral source (how you arrived at SaaSpare)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send the weekly SaaS Deal Digest newsletter (only if you subscribed)
  • Respond to contact form submissions and support requests
  • Analyse site traffic and improve our content
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies & Tracking

SaaSpare uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to measure site traffic and improve your experience. These include:

  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics (anonymised IP) to understand traffic patterns
  • Affiliate tracking cookies — set when you click an affiliate link to attribute referrals
  • Functional cookies — to remember preferences like newsletter subscription state

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work correctly if you do.

4. Affiliate Links

SaaSpare contains affiliate links. When you click a link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the vendor at no extra cost to you.

Affiliate links are tracked using cookies set by third-party affiliate networks, including Impact and the Amazon Associates Programme. These networks may collect data about your clicks and purchases in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial rankings or comparison outcomes.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis
  • FormSubmit — contact form and newsletter form processing
  • Impact — affiliate link tracking
  • Google Fonts — typography (loaded from Google's CDN)

Each service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them if you have concerns.

6. Data Retention

Newsletter subscriber emails are retained until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months. Analytics data is retained in aggregated, anonymised form indefinitely.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us via our contact page or email privacy@saaspare.org.
