## DO and DON'T

DO:
- Personalise every email with a specific reference to their work
- Lead with value (the data), not with the ask
- Keep it under 150 words whenever possible
- Follow up exactly once after 7 days

DON'T:
- Use "I wanted to reach out" or "I hope this finds you well"
- Send from a branded address on first contact
- Attach anything in the first email
- Reference the site as "my site" — use "SaaSpare" or "the site"
- Send more than 2 emails total to the same person
