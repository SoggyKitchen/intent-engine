## Post Title
Show HN: I built a SaaS comparison site that shows you the actual pricing (no affiliate ranking)

## Post Body
I got fed up with SaaS comparison sites that rank tools by commission size rather than quality, so I spent the last year building an alternative.

SaaSpare (saaspare.org) currently covers 1,000+ B2B SaaS products across 40 categories.

What makes it different:
- Every page shows the actual per-seat monthly price, not "plans from $X"
- Scoring uses 8 published criteria applied consistently across every tool
- Affiliate links are disclosed at the bottom of any page where they exist (about 15% of pages currently)
- A Shortlist Builder lets you filter by category, team size, and budget and returns a ranked list
- A Deal Radar page tracks active discounts and trial extensions

The data journalism side: we published a pricing report showing median SaaS prices rose 34% across all categories between 2023 and 2026. CRM is up 72% in three years. Zendesk went up 135%. Free to use and cite: saaspare.org/research/saas-pricing-2026

Happy to answer questions about the methodology, the stack, or what we found during data collection.
