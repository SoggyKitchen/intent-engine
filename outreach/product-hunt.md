Hi PH 👋

SaaSpare is something I built after getting burned by "best CRM" articles that were secretly sorted by commission.

The site covers 1,000+ tools across 40 categories. What makes it different:

1. **Real pricing numbers** — not "plans from $X" but the actual per-seat monthly cost
2. **Published scoring criteria** — 8 factors, same weight applied to every product
3. **Full affiliate disclosure** — if we earn a commission, it says so on the page
4. **Shortlist Builder** — filter by category, team size, and budget
5. **Deal Radar** — active discounts and trial extensions

We also just published a pricing inflation report: median SaaS prices are up 34% across all categories since 2023. Some categories (CRM, customer support) are up 70–135%. Free to use and cite.
